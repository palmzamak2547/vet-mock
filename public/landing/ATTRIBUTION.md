# Landing images: sources and rights

## vetxray-canine-cardiomegaly-*.webp

Canine lateral thoracic radiograph, labelled "cardiomegaly" by the dataset's annotators.
From VetXRay: 9,882 manually annotated canine and feline thoracic radiographs, Zenodo,
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19051776 (record https://zenodo.org/records/19051776).
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: rendered from the DICOM held for the Practical Imaging Lab (case
`vetxray-canine-cardiomegaly`) with the file's own window, resized to 1600 and 960 px
wide and saved as WebP. No identifiers are present in the file or the pixels. The label is
the dataset's annotation; the landing page says so and does not add a diagnosis of its own.
