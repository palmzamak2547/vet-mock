### 10. A paper examines TOPICS — never mix กลางภาค with ปลายภาค
Palm, 2026-09-16: "กลางภาคก็ควรอยู่กลางภาค ปลายภาคก็ต้องอยู่ปลายภาค ... แยกให้ชัด สร้างมาตรฐาน และความมั่นคง". The separation had been a label, not a filter: `'1-mid'` and `'1-final'` both mapped to semester 1 and nothing else, so the two picks served an identical pool.

**The model, in one line: scope belongs to the TOPIC, and a question inherits it.** Resolution lives in `src/lib/exam-scope.js`, first hit wins:
1. `question.examScope: 'continuous'` — the course has no written paper. Nothing overrides it.
2. `topic.examScope` in `curriculum.js` — **the normal path, the one to reach for, and the one that DECIDES.** A topic's scope is this year's timetable; a question's is the paper it was transcribed from, often another cohort's. When they disagree the topic wins, which is Palm's own instruction: "อาจมีบางปีที่ไม่ตรงกับรุ่นปัจจุบันก็ให้เทียบหัวข้อเอา".
3. `subject.examScope` — a subject taught and examined as one block (POA is `'continuous'`). Then `question.examScope`, read only when nothing above answers.
4. The faculty timetable, via `src/data/exam-papers.generated.js` — a subject sitting exactly one paper is settled with no data entry at all (epidemiology sits only the final, so all 100 of its questions are final scope and can never pad a midterm set).
5. Unknown — and **unknown is never filtered out**. Missing metadata must not silently shrink a student's practice set; `lint:exam-scope` is what stops unknown from becoming the norm.

Rules that follow from it, and they are not negotiable:
- **Never read an absence as a fact.** "Not in the timetable" does NOT mean "no exam": the year-4 COM III/IV/V banks and the VCA compilation (2,000 questions) are absent from the current timetable and would have vanished from every phase-filtered pool. A course with no written paper says so out loud with `examScope: 'continuous'`.
- **Never require both scopes to agree.** An AND between the question's scope and its topic's is unsatisfiable for a question tagged `midterm` on a `final` topic, and it hid eleven of them from midterm and final alike on 2026-09-19. `tests/unit/exam-scope-reachable.test.mjs` fails if any question is invisible in every phase; a hidden question is worse than a loosely placed one.
- **A question on the wrong topic is a scope bug, not a topic bug.** 24 frog/amphibian/turtle/ornamental questions sat on `aqua-aquarium-vet` (a post-midterm lecture), so กลางภาค showed none of them. When a set looks thin, check what its questions are filed under before writing new ones — `node scripts/audit-scope-contradictions.mjs` lists the disagreements, and the cohort's own midterm sheet is the evidence for where a lecture actually sat.
- **Adding a topic means declaring its paper.** `npm run lint:exam-scope` fails when a topic in a both-paper subject does not say which paper it sits, against a budget that may only shrink. Lower the budget when you map a batch; never raise it.
- **`examScope` is not prediction metadata.** It is a fact about the syllabus and is validated on its own; `predictionMetadataIssues` deliberately excludes it. Folding the two together meant labelling a legacy question's paper flipped it to "partial metadata, invalid" and would have failed 5,169 questions against the standard ratchet.
- **The timetable is the source of truth for which papers exist.** After it changes, run `npm run regen:exam-papers`; `lint:exam-scope` fails if the generated map has drifted.
- **Copy may promise only what the engine keeps.** The phase subtitles say *"ไม่รวมเนื้อหาปลายภาค"* (what is excluded), never *"เฉพาะกลางภาค"* (that what remains is exhaustive), because unmapped topics are still shown. `audit-sense-fixes.test.mjs` pins both halves.

**Which paper is current, and everything that follows it** (Palm, 2026-10-28: "สิ่งที่เรามีปัญหา ตลอดเรื่อง Vetmock ของเราเคยไม่ตรงกับกลางภาค ปลายภาค ปัจจุบัน วันนี้ต้องแก้ไขให้หมดสิ้นไปครับ", then "อย่าให้logic เพี้ยนหรือขัดกัน เช่น พอไปปีหน้าและผมไม่ได้ททำเพิ่มแล้ว กลายเป็นว่าคนไม่เห็นข้อสอบเลย", and "ถ้าช่วงสอบที่ระบบเลือกไม่มีข้อมูลในตารางสอบ ให้ไม่กรองเลย คือเห็นทุกข้อ ทำได้แต่ต้องบอกดีๆ แยกชัดๆ"). Until then a saved กลางภาค never moved, and the wrap-ups, lecturer sets and panic cards were pinned to `{ year: 5, phase: '1-mid' }`, so under ปลายภาค they showed nothing.
- **Only the timetable picks a paper.** `timetableStatus(year, now)` in `src/lib/current-phase.js`: the paper whose last exam has not ended, from that year's own timetable on Bangkok's clock (`PAPER_WINDOWS` in `exam-papers.generated.js`, so the boot chunk never loads schedule.js). Neither SEMESTER's exam periods nor the month picks one. With no paper still to come the status says why: `no-timetable` (no table for the year), `term-over` (every paper sat, the next term's table not in), `stale-year` (150 days past the last paper: a past year's table).
- **One answer for every surface.** `phaseState({ saved, anchor }, year, now)` returns `{ phase, filtered, reason, current, advancedFrom }`. App keeps the pick with `hooks/useSelectedPhase.js` and hands every view `selectedPhase` = the phase when `filtered`, else null, so practice sets, counts, the badge and the three registries cannot disagree. Reasons: `timetable`, `student-choice`, `not-chosen` (no pick), and the three statuses above, which narrow nothing.
- **A saved pick follows it, and data never empties a set.** A pick whose paper is over moves to the current paper, and Home says so in one line (`movedNotice`: "...สอบไปแล้ว" only when the timetable shows that paper over, else "ตามตารางสอบ"). A pick for the current paper or one ahead stays, and a finished paper picked to revise stays while the paper current then still is. With no paper still to come in the timetable nothing is narrowed, whoever picked and when: last year's table and topic scopes never filter this year's cohort. The pick is kept, so it moves on by itself once a table is in. A pick saved before anchors existed that this term's table has no window for (a term-2 pick under term 1's table) is read as last school year's and follows the timetable. `vmx-phase-anchor` is `{ at, term }`: when the pick was made, against which SEMESTER. Choosing another year on the landing page starts that year with no pick.
- **Not split is never silent.** `unsplitNotice(state)` is the one line, for every state the timetable cannot back, with or without a pick: Home shows it under the subject header, and the header pill reads ไม่แยกช่วงสอบ on every page with a header. ปี 6 (rotations) has no papers and no line. Under a paper, a topic whose paper nobody recorded keeps its place and is tagged ยังไม่ระบุช่วงสอบ. The phase screen's badge reads the same state as every other page.
- **Content is kept per paper, per term, and last year's is never thrown away.** `WRAPUPS` (exam-wrapups.js), `LECTURER_SETS_BY_PAPER` (lecturer-sets.js) and `PANIC_CARD_PAPERS` (panic-cards.js) are keyed term (SEMESTER.id), then year, then phase. `paperContent` returns this term's entry for the paper, else the newest earlier term's for the same paper, with `{ term, own }`; a later term's entry replaces it the moment it is filed (Palm, 29 Oct, "อ้าว" at hiding it). Content that is not `own` (another school year's: this year is the later of SEMESTER's and the calendar's, which turns on 1 August) carries `oldContentLine`, "จากปีการศึกษา 2569 (รุ่นก่อน) อาจไม่ตรงกับปีนี้", and its paper-bound facts show their year: the lecturer-set intro leads with the year and dates the exam, the wrap-up's exam date prints its year. The paper whose content a page shows is `contentPhase(state)`: the paper narrowed to, else the kept pick, so a pick in a no-data year still opens its paper's content (and its practice buttons serve on that paper, as their counts are printed). The final's content is one more key and no code; a paper with no entry anywhere hides only that section. Read them through `wrapUpFor`, `loadWrapUp`, `lecturerSetFor` and `panicCardPaper` (or their `has*`), and name the paper in copy through `phaseLabel` or `EXAM_SCOPE_LABEL`, never a literal กลางภาค. Home's "ไม่มีสอบ..." on a card is claimed only for a paper the timetable holds.
- **The back office shows the state.** `/app/admin` → ช่วงสอบ (`lib/exam-period-report.js`): each year's status, its papers, the moment the next term's table is needed, what to fill in, which year's wrap-ups, lecturer sets and panic cards each paper uses, and per subject the topics with no paper (this term's subjects named, the other term's counted). It is a page, never a CI check that fails by the calendar.
- **Guards.** `tests/unit/exam-period-matrix.test.mjs` (saved pick × eight moments from Sep 2026 to Feb 2028 with nothing updated × years 5, 4, 2: the paper, the badge, the line, a non-empty set for every subject that has questions, and the same matrix under TZ=UTC, Bangkok and Los Angeles); `exam-period-foundation.test.mjs` (a single-paper scope in a registry or reader, a label that hard-codes the paper, timetable rows outside SEMESTER's periods); `lint:exam-scope` (every topic of a subject with a paper still ahead, and `audit-scope-contradictions.mjs --check`, midterm against final, budget 10, may only shrink); `tests/e2e/phase-advance.spec.js`.
- **A new term:** the `vetmock-new-term` skill. In short: SEMESTER and EXAM_SCHEDULE together, `npm run regen:exam-papers`, then the topics' `examScope`, then check /app/admin → ช่วงสอบ.

## 🔀 Gitflow & parallel harnesses
Full rules: [docs/GITFLOW.md](docs/GITFLOW.md). The short form:
- **Releases are pull requests, and CI is the gate** (OWNER DECISION 2026-10-22, top of this file). Branch from origin/main in your own worktree, push the branch, `gh pr create`, then `gh pr merge --squash --auto`; no local gate first. CI runs the full matrix on the PR and GitHub squash-merges it when its own checks pass (no up-to-date rule, no Queue workflow; rebase only on a reported conflict). No messages about push order or version numbers.
- **A merge to `main` is the deploy.** Vercel builds main only and promotes once Build and smoke pass on that SHA; watch that run and fix forward or revert when it goes red. Never push to main directly, never `vercel --prod`. A merge is not production proof.
- **Disjoint file ownership across harnesses.** Check the one claim record (`work/loop-20260930/COORDINATION.md`) and the working tree before touching a file; shared surfaces need explicit hunk ownership. Never stage or commit another lane's files or untracked work — stage exact paths only.
- **Competing lanes use separate worktrees**, each with its own `npm ci` after lockfile changes (a node_modules junction silently bundles the primary checkout's packages).
- **Integrate with the checklist**, not from memory: recheck main, build, applicable gates, exact-path staging, one push, verify the live journey, update the claim record.
- **Bad commit on main → revert forward.** Never force-push or rewrite history.

Regenerate it whenever another pull request adds past-paper items: two pull requests green alone turned main red on 29 Oct (fixed in #119). `src/lib/exam-origin-parse.js` is the one parser of `{ cohort, paper, year }`. It reads `examOrigin`, and `source` only for an explicit post-exam record. A file label ("Mid 86", "TJ86") or a compilation's author never names a sitting cohort, and `verified` is never read. A same item is stem plus key under guards, joined by complete linkage. Content lanes name the sitting cohort and the paper in `examOrigin`, never the compilation's. A near duplicate from another cohort is recurrence evidence: allow-list it in lint:dupes, never merge it. Any label reads as history ("เคยออก N รุ่น"), never as what will be asked (archive: Exam recurrence data).
- Every question bank is its own chunk importing none (`npm run lint:dist` runs `scripts/lint-bank-chunks.mjs`): never share a module between two banks, and give a new bank file an exact manualChunks rule (archive: Night release 5.144.0, the 5.144.4 note).
- Never run `apply-note-corrections.mjs` to add one correction: it rewrites corrections.js from its input alone and drops every existing entry. Write from a deck's printed slide content, never a student's handwriting on an annotated copy; correct spelling-only slide typos and keep meaning-level doubts as printed. The summary lint fails on a middle dot (archive: Night release 5.144.0, the 5.146.1 and 2b-ledger notes).
- Video summaries: `npm run fetch:videos`, `node scripts/flatten-transcript.mjs <videoId>`, a draft in house order (Pathophys, Signalment, Clinical Signs, Dx, Tx, Monitoring, ending with "📝 Exam Hot Spots" and a "💡 Closing" blockquote), the matching `src/data/video-summaries-<subject>.js` with its metadata, one commit per 3-8 clips. The figure-extractor deck choice is held for round 3 (`work/polish-1011/round2/K.md`) (archive: Content Pipeline (video summaries); Polish sweep release 5.143.0).
