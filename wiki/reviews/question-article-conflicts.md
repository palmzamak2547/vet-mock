# Questions whose linked article does not back the marked answer

Every question here was just linked to a governed article. A check with the
marked answer in hand found that the article does not support it.

These are not caused by the link. The question and the notes already disagreed;
the link is what made it visible, because the app is now about to show a reader
"read the article about this" straight after they get the question wrong. If the
article then says the opposite, the reader is stuck between two things the same
app told them.

**contradicts** — the section states something that makes the marked answer wrong.
**undercuts** — the marked answer is not false, but the section makes another option
defensible too, so the item no longer forces one answer.

Nearly every proposed repair is a stem clarification or a distractor swap, not a
change of answer. That is deliberate: the exam is written by the lecturer who taught
the marked answer, so the answer stays and the ambiguity goes.

Generated from a check of all 316 newly linked questions.
287 were consistent.

## <a id="q-76"></a>Question 76 — contradicts

- **Bank:** `questions-part1.js`
- **Question:** Fertilization period ใน progesterone level
- **Marked answer:** 4-6 ng/mL จุดเหมาะสม
- **Linked section:** `repro-lect--semen-ai--ovulation-timing-aj-kc`

**What the section says**

The P4 table places the fertilization window at a different concentration than the key: '10-20 | Fertilization period (LH+4 to +6) - best AI window!', while it labels the 4-10 ng/mL band as 'Ovulation (LH+2) - eggs immature'. So the article says a P4 of 4-6 ng/mL is the ovulation point with immature eggs, not the fertilization window.

**What it supports instead:** None of the four options. The article's fertilization figure (10-20 ng/mL) is not offered.

**Smallest repair:** Renumber the marked option to the article's band while keeping it the answer, for example 'ช่วง 10-20 ng/mL (LH+4 ถึง +6)'. If the lecture's 4-6 figure must stand, add a stem clause pinning it to the lecture, for example 'ตามค่าที่ใช้ในเลคเชอร์', so the reader sees the two number sets are deliberately different.

## <a id="q-1533"></a>Question 1533 — undercuts

- **Question:** Underlying mechanism ที่พบเฉพาะใน **stress starvation** แต่ไม่พบใน simple starvation
- **Marked answer:** Inflammatory cytokines

The Hormones row splits as 'Simple starvation: Insulin down, glucagon up' against 'Stress starvation: Cortisol up, catecholamines up, cytokines'. Cortisol is therefore listed as belonging to the stress column only, exactly like cytokines, so adrenal glucocorticoid reads as an equally correct answer to a stem asking what is found only in stress starvation. **What it supports instead:** Glucocorticoid จาก adrenal is made defensible alongside the marked Inflammatory cytokines.

**Smallest repair:** Swap that one distractor for something the article assigns to simple starvation, for example 'ใช้ ketone จากไขมันเป็นแหล่งพลังงานหลัก' or 'protein sparing effect'. Then only cytokines survives the stem.
