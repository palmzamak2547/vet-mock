# Artwork on vca.vetmock.com: sources and rights

## footer-della-bella.webp

Stefano Della Bella (Italian, 1610–1664), *Various Figures and Landscapes: A Lost Horseman
Enquiring the Way*, 1649. Etching. The Cleveland Museum of Art, Gift of Carl Fredric Clarke,
1945.401.4.
Source: https://clevelandart.org/art/1945.401.4
Licence: CC0 1.0 (Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access).
Changes: cropped inside the plate mark, the inscription line removed, and the ink kept as an
alpha mask so the site prints it in two colours.

## Domain plates

The bookplates on the domain cards are VetMock's own course artwork, served from the main
app's `public/subject-art` and `public/panic-art`.
