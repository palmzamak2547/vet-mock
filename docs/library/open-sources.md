# Study library: sources from outside Chula

Palm (2026-10-21): the library should not hold only Chula's own material. Trusted outside sources
come in one subject at a time; zoonoses and One Health went first (2026-10-22). The registry is
`src/data/library-open-sources.js`; this file records why each entry may be on VetMock and how.

## Rules

- **Read from the source itself.** Title, organisation, year, address and licence are read on the
  publisher's own page or file on the `checked` date. Never from LibGen, Sci-Hub, Anna's Archive,
  ResearchGate uploads, Scribd or any mirror.
- **Hosted** means the licence allows an unchanged copy for free, non-commercial study. VetMock
  charges nothing and shows no ads (About page), so CC BY-NC terms are met. A hosted file is a
  `library_docs` row like any other (R2, `scripts/ingest-local-docs.mjs`), with the licence in
  `license`, the quoted line in `permission_evidence` and the source page in `source_url`. It is
  kept exactly as published: the copyright page inside each file carries its licence and citation,
  and figures credited to a third party keep their own rights (VetMock never extracts them).
- **Link** means free to read but not ours to copy: the card opens the publisher's page.
- When a file and its web page disagree, the stricter statement wins (see below).
- Every card says where the source comes from, what kind it is and its licence
  (`openSourceLine`, src/lib/open-sources.js). Logos of WHO, FAO, UNEP and WOAH are never used.

## Decisions

- **Linked although a page shows an open licence.** *Taking a multisectoral, One Health approach*
  (Tripartite, 2019) and *Brucellosis in humans and animals* (2006): the WHO (and FAO) pages list
  CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO, but the PDF's own imprint says all rights reserved. The stricter statement wins.
- **Hosted under a publisher permission, not a CC licence.** FAO/WHO *Multicriteria-based ranking for
  risk management of food-borne parasites* (2014) prints that material "may be copied, downloaded and
  printed for private study, research and teaching purposes, or for use in non-commercial products or
  services" with acknowledgement; VetMock is that use.
- **Linked although the licence allows a copy.** Four PubMed Central reviews (Nipah, rabies laboratory
  diagnosis, ACVIM leptospirosis consensus, animal prion diseases) are CC BY, CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA or
  CC BY-NC-ND, but PMC and the publishers answer scripted downloads with a bot check or 403. VetMock does
  not work around bot checks, so the card opens the PMC article.
- **WOAH Terrestrial Manual.** Each chapter links to its PDF on woah.org, the versions listed in WOAH's
  Manual index on 2026-10-22. The online edition may carry newer versions; the chapter's own page wins.
- **Thai government sources** (DDC, DLD, NIAH) print no open licence, so they are links. The DLD
  sampling manual links to DLD's own page for it; the file behind that page sits on Google Drive,
  so the link stays on DLD's page.
