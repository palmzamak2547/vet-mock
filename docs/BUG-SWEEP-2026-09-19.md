# Bug sweep, 2026-09-19

Everything Palm asked for in this round, in the order it will be worked. Nothing
here is closed until it has been verified on the built app, not on the source.

His standing rules for this sweep, which outrank speed:

- **No guessing. Find the fact first.** Do not be confident before the evidence.
- **Do not disturb anyone using the app** — never during an exam, a submission,

## P0 — a student is being misled right now

### 1. Final-exam topics appeared under กลางภาค ✅ shipped in a5baff83

`TopicSelectView` never read `topic.examScope`, and the study catalog has no
scope logic at all, so choosing กลางภาค listed all ten equine reproduction
topics. The five marked `final` came up empty and printed "รอเนื้อหาเพิ่ม",
which tells a student the content is coming when the truth is that the topic is
not on their paper.

**A wrong turn worth keeping.** The first fix also kept a topic when it *held*
questions scoped to this paper. That reads as generous and is wrong: a question's
`examScope` is the paper it was **recorded from**, and many of those papers
belong to another cohort — the single midterm question under equine pregnancy is
sourced "บันทึกหลังสอบของรุ่นพี่ Vet 85", and the ten under equine dentistry are
"รวบรวมโดยรุ่นพี่ Vet 85". A topic's `examScope` is **this** year's timetable;
the entries carry "(Course Schedule 2026)" beside them. Only the topic's own
scope can answer "is this on my paper". Pinned by `tests/e2e/topic-scope.spec.js`.

### 2. The same leak everywhere else ✅ done

Palm: "ดูทุกวิชาครับ จุดอื่นๆด้วย". Every surface that lists topics or counts them was
read. Two more were making a claim about the chosen paper and getting it wrong.

**The reading list** (`ReadingChecklistView`) listed the whole year. The phase
screen describes เทอม 1 กลางภาค as "วิชาเทอม 1 ไม่รวมเนื้อหาปลายภาค", so the app already
promised the exclusion; a year-5 student on กลางภาค was handed **86 topics off the
final paper** and a progress bar measured against them. Same rule as the topic
list, nothing erased: a tick on a final topic stays in storage and reappears
under ปลายภาค. Pinned by `tests/e2e/reading-checklist-scope.spec.js`.

**The subject card's อ่านแล้ว X%** divided by the whole subject. With the list
scoped, that would print two different percentages for the same reading on two
screens. Ticking the 13 midterm topics of สุขศาสตร์น้ำนม + เนื้อ now reads 100% on
กลางภาค and 54% across the subject, which is the same arithmetic the list does.
Pinned by `tests/e2e/reading-progress-agrees.spec.js`.

**Deliberately left alone, with the reason:**

- `NotesView` — notes are the textbook, not the paper. Filtering them would take
  away reading a student asked for, and the list makes no claim of covering a
  paper. The reading checklist does make that claim, which is why it filters.
- `ContributeView`'s topic dropdown — a contributor files a question against any
  topic, whenever they write it.
- `AdminView`, `LandingView` and `YearSelectView` — whole-bank or pre-phase
  surfaces. The landing page has no year or paper selected at all.
- `study-catalog.js` returns every topic unscoped. Its one consumer,
  `TopicSelectView`, filters. A phase parameter plumbed through a module with a
  single caller would be a second place for the rule to drift.
- A dead `checklistTopics` in `HomeView` was deleted rather than scoped: it was
  computed on every render and read by nothing.

### 3. A matching question showed no answer ✅ shipped in a5baff83

`MatchDragDrop` rendered the student's own choice and a ✗, and the banner said
"ถูกบางคู่ — ดูเฉลยด้านล่าง". `pair.right` was never rendered anywhere, so a
student who got three of four pairs wrong could not learn the right pairing from
the page that told them to look for it. The answer now sits on the row it
belongs to and the banner says where it is.

### 4. True/False reads as both right and wrong at once ✅ shipped in a5baff83

Palm: "ภาษามันแปลกๆไหม … คนจะสับสนไหม ว่าสรุปถูกหรือผิด". On a correct answer
the chosen button is **red**, the banner says **✓ ถูกต้อง!**, and the
explanation directly under it opens with **ไม่ถูกต้อง** — which is about the
statement, not the student. Three signals, two of them saying the opposite of
what happened.
