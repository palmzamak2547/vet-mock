# 5.283.0: a student can put their own picture on the profile

The profile's second slice: besides the 16 ready-made pictures, a student can now use a picture of their own. It is
built private from the start: classmates who are signed in with a Chula account see it, nobody else does.

## What a student notices

1. **"ใส่รูปของคุณ" on the identity card.** On the profile hub, under the name, one button picks a picture from the
   device or, on a phone, takes one with the camera. The browser shrinks it (longest side 512 px) before it is
   sent, so an upload is small on a mobile connection. While it travels the line under the button reads
   "กำลังอัปโหลดรูป"; when it is kept, "ใช้รูปนี้แล้ว เพื่อนในจุฬาที่เข้าสู่ระบบเห็นรูปนี้", and the picture is on the
   card at once.
2. **Replace, remove, go back.** With a picture in place the button reads "เปลี่ยนรูป", and "ลบรูป กลับไปใช้รูปสำเร็จรูป"
   asks once, then brings back the ready-made picture the student chose earlier. A student who uploads nothing
   keeps the ready-made set exactly as before.
3. **Every outcome is said where the student acted**, on the line under the buttons, 14 px: not a picture ("ไฟล์นี้
   ไม่ใช่รูป ใช้ได้เฉพาะ JPEG, PNG หรือ WebP"), too large (over 2 MB, or too many pixels), an animated picture, offline
   ("ออฟไลน์อยู่ ต่ออินเทอร์เน็ตแล้วลองอีกครั้ง"), too many tries in a short time, refused, removed.
4. **The picture is redrawn, not stored as sent.** The server keeps a new 256 px square WebP made from the upload,
   turned upright, with every piece of metadata removed: where it was taken, the camera, any comment. A file is
   judged by its bytes, never by its name or the type the browser claims; SVG, animated pictures and pictures past
   the pixel limit are refused.
5. **Who sees it.** A picture is kept in the app's private storage and reaches a viewer only through a signed link
   that expires within 20 minutes, minted for a signed-in Chula account. Guests, accounts outside the university,
   share cards and link previews see the ready-made picture only.
6. **"รายงานรูปนี้".** On a group's member list, under a classmate's own picture. After 3 different members report the
   same picture it is hidden from everyone but its owner, whose card then says "รูปของคุณถูกซ่อนจากเพื่อน เพราะมี
   เพื่อนหลายคนรายงานรูปนี้ ..." with the way forward (a new picture or the ready-made one).
7. **For the admin**, /app/admin has "รูปโปรไฟล์ที่ถูกรายงาน": each reported or hidden picture, its report count, hide
   and show again (showing again clears that picture's reports). A hide also closes the account to new uploads until
   the admin opens it again, even after the student removes the picture; accounts reported in the last 30 days stay
   on the list after a new picture.
8. **Deleting the account** removes the picture from storage first, and the picture's key and every report made or
   received go with the account.

## How it is built

- `api/profile-picture.js` (one new function): GET signed links for up to 60 accounts, POST an upload or a report,
  DELETE the student's picture. Signed-in Chula members only (`chula-gate.js`), counted per account
  (`limitCaller`: 12 uploads an hour, 30 reports an hour; removing is never held back). The body arrives as base64, as the read-aloud
  routes already send theirs.
- `api/_lib/profile-picture-image.js`: decoding and re-encoding with **sharp** (libvips), already in the repository
  for build scripts and now a runtime dependency. The format is taken from the file signature and must match what
  the decoder reads; the header's pixel count (40 megapixels at most) is checked before any pixel is decoded.
- Storage: the library's private bucket, under `profile-pictures/<random id>.webp`, written with the same signed
  storage route the library uses (`writeObject` in `api/_lib/r2.js`) and read through `/api/library-blob` with a
  restricted, no-store link. Each link carries its expiry and the browser keeps it no longer; a picture that does
  not load falls back to the ready-made one with a line saying so. The replaced or removed object is deleted.
- Database: `supabase/migrations/20261116120000_profile_pictures.sql`, **written and not applied**; it is applied by
  hand after a second reader. Until then every student keeps the ready-made picture and an upload answers "ยังใส่รูป
  เองไม่ได้ในตอนนี้". The picture's key and state live in `profiles`, outside the sync engine; a student's own token
  cannot write them (a trigger), only the signed server request can. Reports have their own table with row
  security. The admin's block has its own table too (`private.profile_picture_blocks`, keyed on the account, no
  client access), so a student who deletes and recreates their own profile row does not lift it. A stored picture
  that cannot be deleted is written to the server log with its key and the reason. The undo is written at the top
  of the file.

## How it was tested

- `tests/unit/profile-picture-api.test.mjs`, 24 tests with fixture files in `tests/fixtures/profile-picture/` (made by
  `make.mjs` there): a valid JPEG, PNG and WebP each kept as a 256 px WebP; a file over 2 MB; a text file named
  `.jpg`; an SVG sent as `image/jpeg`; a JPEG with a GPS location and camera name, proven absent afterwards; a PNG
  claiming 30000 x 30000 pixels; an animated WebP; guests and non-members on every route; the per-account limit; a
  picture the admin hid, also after removing it; removal never rate limited; old objects deleted; each link's real
  expiry, counted on the device's own clock; links signed, restricted and short-lived; a failed storage delete
  logged with its key.
- `scripts/test-profile-pictures-db.mjs` (15 checks, PGlite locally and Postgres 17.6 in CI): the trigger, the
  signature, who sees a shown or hidden picture, the threshold of 3 distinct reporters, a new picture starting clean,
  report rows readable only by their reporter, admin-only listing, hiding and restoring, a hide surviving removal and re-upload, a hide surviving the student deleting and recreating
  their own profile row, no client grant on the block table, a request signed for another account, the cascade on account
  deletion, and the undo followed by a second apply.
- `tests/e2e/profile-picture.spec.js`: the profile flow against the built app with a stubbed store: the picture
  shrunk to 512 px before sending, shown on the card, each refusal said in place, offline, removal with its
  confirmation, and the hidden-after-reports notice. Pictures at 390 x 664 and 1280, light and dark, in
  `work/profile-picture/`.
