# 5.297.0: แตะช่องพิมพ์ทันทีหลังเปลี่ยนหน้า แล้วพิมพ์ต่อได้เลย

## สิ่งที่นิสิตเห็น

- **แตะช่องพิมพ์หลังเปลี่ยนหน้า:** ถ้าเปลี่ยนหน้าแล้วแตะช่องพิมพ์ทันที เช่น ช่องค้นหาในคลังเอกสาร
  ช่องนั้นจะยังพร้อมพิมพ์อยู่ เดิมบนเครื่องที่ช้า ระบบอาจดึงเคอร์เซอร์ออกจากช่องนั้นเอง
  คีย์บอร์ดจึงหุบและตัวอักษรที่พิมพ์ไม่ขึ้น

## What changed

- `src/App.jsx`: one frame after a page change the shell moves focus to the page (`#main`), so a
  keyboard or a screen reader starts at the new page. That frame can come after the student has
  already focused a field of the new page, and the move then took the focus away from the field. It
  now leaves focus alone when focus is already on something inside the page. Everything inside the
  page is replaced on a page change, so focus found there was put there on the new page.

## Checks

- `tests/e2e/library.spec.js`, new case: the shell's frame is held, the library is opened from the PDF
  reader, the search field is focused, then the frame is let go. Against a build without this change
  it failed on Chromium desktop and phone, Firefox and WebKit, 4 of 4, at the check after the frame
  (the field was no longer focused). With the change it passed 12 of 12, three runs on each.
- What led here: the case "the personal reader and shelf connect without losing the shelf search"
  passed only on its retry on WebKit in CI on 17 Nov 2026. The screenshot of its first attempt shows
  the search field empty after the text had been typed, and the field not focused.
- The twelve spec files that check where focus is after a page change (`library`, `answer-evidence`,
  `connect-apps`, `instant-feedback`, `mochi-combo`, `mochi-presence`, `optional-feature-failure`,
  `palette-frame`, `results-peak-end`, `study-reminder`, `update-and-intent`, `perf-budget`), whole,
  on the same four: 211 passed and 9 skipped.
- The six uses of `autoFocus` in the app were read: each is in a dialog or in a form that opens after
  a tap, so no page puts focus on a control of its own as it opens.
- Unit suite 5,168 pass, 0 fail, run on a tree that also holds the CI changes of #291; `lint:all`
  clean; size budgets met (first-load JS 232,942 B of 240,800 B).
