# 5.191.1: the video library's audit fixes

The main session's audit of 2 Nov found six problems in the video library. They sit on top of 5.191.0, the second batch (#147), and this PR merges after it.

## What changes

- **Transcripts for every carded clip.**
  - Every carded clip's transcript has been in R2 since 5.189.0. `/api/video-file` served one only for a clip with our own copy, and the study panel did not ask without a copy, so 5 of 71 clips showed a transcript.
  - A transcript is now served for every card (still signed-in, through the Chula gate). Our copy and its slides still need the clip to be held.
- **One row per clip under a topic.** The topic button's "คลิป N" counts clips, but its list showed one row per stretch, so it said 1 and opened 3. `clipRows` (src/lib/video-library.js) now gives each clip one row: its surest stretch, and the minutes and number of its stretches.
- **The index loses heading words.** Capitalised slide headings had left ordinary words in the index ("IN", "OF", "TO", "HEALTH", "DOG").
  - **Acronyms:** a capital word stays as an acronym when the decks write it in capitals at least as often as in ordinary case, plurals and numbers after it included ("TLRs", "COVID-19"). A short one (CO, HR, ET) must be ten times commoner in ordinary case to count as a word.
  - **Capitalised words:** otherwise it is a word a heading set in capitals. It drops when a quarter of the lectures use it ("HEALTH", "DOG"), and otherwise goes back to its usual spelling ("SALMONELLA" is "Salmonella", "BIOPSY" is "biopsy").
  - **Joining words** go in any case.
  - **Cut endings come back:** "endometriti", "Coronaviru", "antibodie" and "MER".
  - **Shorter duplicates:** a single word goes when the index says it longer within a minute ("standing" beside "Standing anesthesia", "culture" beside "cultures"). Elsewhere it is a jump point of its own.
  - **By hand:** words a reader judged to be heading or table words, or slide labels, are dropped in that clip's `polished.json`, nine clips in all (for example HK, CU, DC on BnfVPW0S_lw, and Present and Absent from S6uo2I2gfXQ's reflex table).
  - **Number rows** without a number go.
  - **Not covered by the rule:** ordinary words with no capitals cannot be told from terms by rule, so only those a reader picked out are dropped.
- **Chapter titles:** a stray letter ("o" on Ztccmz_HjfY) or a running header ("Equine", six times on MGU2qNX2paA) leaves the chapter untitled, and its line says what it teaches.
- **2025 summaries:** three summaries of last year's recordings said their class was "NOT IN EXAM" (Epidemiology XTDvx2Bym1w, One Health YWlclk9v6cE, and Avian Medicine pQ3y5Ki_DmQ). The audit found the first two on this year's final, and the third makes the same kind of claim. That year's words are taken out; the lecture content stays.
- **Credit line:** the shelf card and the player say "โดย" for the uploader, and a video without one shows none ("by Unknown" before).
- **5.189.0's changelog promised more than shipped.** Skipping dead air works only where we hold the copy and have measured the sound, and the headline and the entry now say so. The transcript part is true as of this release.

## Review

An independent review of the code found four defects; all are fixed here:
- **The first index rule dropped real terms written in capital headings,** such as BIOPSY on Nto0dg3GP1w and FAT on WX7vrDP9MjA (SALMONELLA and MASTITIS would have gone next). Such a word now returns to its usual spelling, and drops only when a quarter of the lectures use it.
- **The shorter-duplicate rule ignored time.** "vestibular" at 19:17, where vestibular gait is taught, went because of a recap mention an hour later. The rule now looks within a minute.
- **The sign-in prompt promised slides** on clips without our copy, which never have them. It now promises the transcript only.
- **The changelog promised "only real terms",** which no rule can promise for ordinary words. It now says what the rule does, and a reader dropped the junk the review listed.

The review also found that the API test failed once every clip had our copy. It now skips then, and it pins a guest's 401 and the 404 for frames on a clip without our copy.
