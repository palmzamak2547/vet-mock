# 5.191.1: exam day opens that course's Panic set; dates and copy match the data

From the 2 Nov cross-lane audit of dates, scope, counts and copy.

## What shipped

- **Home's top action on a paper day or the day before** names the course in Thai ("วันนี้สอบเวชปฏิบัติม้า + ศัลย์") and
  opens that course's own Panic set. It used to start the cross-subject cram, which on 24 Nov served Swine, sat the day
  before, ahead of Equine Med.
- **The next-exam tile** prints the course's Thai name and paper.
- **POA under ปลายภาค** says it has no paper in this term's faculty timetable and offers no เปลี่ยนช่วงสอบ button.
- **The phase picker** counts the 10 courses that sit the final.
- **The evidence set** is "ชุดหลักฐานตรงกัน" with the badge "หลายหลักฐาน", no longer "ชุดน่าจะออก".
- **The weekly timetable** no longer prints LAB twice.
- **Home's subject-card dates** use the paper's own day in any phone time zone.
- **Text fixes** in curriculum banners and notes.
