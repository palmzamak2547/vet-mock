# 5.191.1: VetMock AI opens a PDF at its page, on a phone too

The follow-up 5.185.0 left open ("Not in this release"), agreed with the Chula sign-in lane, with the
conditions set on 7 Nov: sign-in keeps the destination, the reader never ends without a way to the file,
and the release number is taken at merge time.

## Bugs, causes, fixes

1. **VetMock AI's "เปิด" and "เปิดหน้า N" showed page 1 on an iPhone, and Android often downloaded the file.**
   The links were `/api/library-file?...&open=1#page=N`, and only desktop Chrome and Firefox honour `#page`.
   Fix (`fileLinks` in `api/_lib/ai/library.js`): a PDF opens in VetMock's reader,
   `/app/tools/pdf?doc=<slug>&page=N`, through `readerUrl` itself. Files that are not PDFs, and copies kept on
   Google Drive or another site, open through the gate as before, the way the shelf opens them
   (`docOpenMode`); the AI now reads `storage_provider` for that (anon and authenticated may select it,
   checked in `information_schema.column_privileges`). Download is unchanged. On 7 Nov the catalogue held
   1,821 PDFs (1,820 on R2, one in Supabase storage) and 327 other files, all on R2.
2. **Signing in from a document link landed on an empty reader.** ai.vetmock.com keeps its own session
   (`ai/src/lib/auth.js`), so a student signed in only there meets vetmock.com's Chula wall first. The wall's
   redirect sign-in came back to the path alone (`origin + pathname`), so the deck and its page, which live in
   the query, were gone; the wall's other Google button came back to the bare origin. Fix (`returnAddress` in
   `src/lib/supabase.js`): Google brings the student back to the whole address, without what an earlier
   sign-in left in it (`code`, `state`, `error`, `error_code`, `error_description`, the hash). The project's
   redirect allow-list holds `https://vetmock.com/**`, and its Site URL is vetmock.com.
3. **A link to a deck the reader could not find ended on its reason, with no way to the file.** A deck named
   only by the address has no `resolve` until its row is found, so the panel offered only "ลองเปิดอีกครั้ง".
   Fix (`PdfAnnotateView.jsx`): the panel still offers "เปิดไฟล์ต้นฉบับ" and "ดาวน์โหลดไฟล์", through the
   server's own mint by slug, which knows rows this device's copy of the catalogue does not. The open ticket
   a VetMock AI link carries (`vt`, `ve`) goes along with that mint (`readerTarget`, `resolveDocUrl`), and the
   address drops it, with the page, once the reader has taken them.

## Review

One independent review of the diff. It found no open redirect, no new way past the Chula gate, and no Referer
leak: vercel.json sends `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, and the AI's links are `noreferrer`. Fixed from it:

- An account change cleared the reader's address before the wall opened. So an expired session the server refused
  to refresh, or "ใช้บัญชีอื่น" on the wall's link screen, still sent the student to an empty reader. Now the address
  is cleared only when another account arrives for a deck that was open for someone else (`deckOwnerRef` in
  App.jsx). When a sign-out closes a deck a link named, the link's page goes back into the address.
- The ticket went to Google and Supabase inside the return address; `returnAddress` now leaves `vt` and `ve` out,
  since the session makes them redundant.
- The reader's "เปิดในเบราว์เซอร์" (in-app browsers) read the address after it had dropped the page; it carries the
  page again.
- The found-deck ticket path had no test; it has one now.

## Limits, and what is not in this release

- In LINE, Facebook and Instagram, a student signed in only on ai.vetmock.com now meets the wall, where Google
  refuses to sign in, and reads the deck after "เปิดในเบราว์เซอร์". Before, the ticketed file opened in the app, at
  page 1. The AI hides its download button in those browsers. Raised with the main session.
- The one PDF in Supabase storage, if the reader cannot find it, gets fallback buttons the mint answers with
  `not_r2`. Google Drive and external rows would get the gate link, which also answers `not_r2`; none exist.
- The Android app's sign-in fallback (an Auth Tab registered for `/`) now returns to `/app/tools/pdf?...`. Not
  checked on a device; the app's links to vetmock.com are not live yet.

## Tests

Run against main's sources for the changed files (origin/main 439cc92f), the four unit files below fail 10 tests,
every new or changed one; with the fix all pass.

- `tests/unit/ai-chat-library.test.mjs` (4 of them): a PDF's link is the reader at its page; the ticket rides in it
  and on the download; a slide file and a Drive copy keep the gate link.
- `tests/unit/library-doc-open.test.mjs` (2): `readerTarget` takes a well-formed ticket only; a found deck, and one
  the reader could not find, mint with the link's ticket.
- `tests/unit/pdf-reader-races.test.mjs` (1): a deck the address names but the shelf lacks gets a mint by slug, with
  the ticket, for its open and download (the real `ingestRemote`).
- `tests/unit/sign-in-return.test.mjs` (3): the return address keeps the deck and its page, and drops a code, a
  refusal, a hash and the ticket; both of the wall's Google buttons use it.
- `tests/e2e/chula-wall.spec.js`, on the connected bundle (chromium desktop and mobile):
  - A visitor opens a deck link, meets the wall, signs in through Google's redirect, and the reader opens at page 3.
    On main's sources it fails waiting for page 3.
  - A member whose expired session the server refuses to refresh signs in again and lands on page 3. Under the old
    owner-change rule it ends on the empty "เปิด PDF แล้วเขียนทับได้เลย" page.
- `tests/e2e/library-doc-open.spec.js` (chromium desktop and WebKit mobile): a link to a deck the shelf cannot find
  shows why and drops the ticket from the address. Its "เปิดไฟล์ต้นฉบับ" opens the file through a mint that carried
  the ticket. On main's sources the panel has no such button.
