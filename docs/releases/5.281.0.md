# 5.281.0: reading notes and the wiki on a phone

Six fixes to reading the notes and VetWiki, judged at 390 x 664 first, then 820 x 1180 and 1280.

## What a student notices

1. **The "กลับไปต้นหัวข้อ" pill never covers the last line.** In the notes reader the pill stood aside
   only near a marker placed after the previous and next buttons, so on a phone it sat over the last
   lines of a topic. The marker now sits right after the last section: the pill leaves before the last
   line reaches it and stays away over the buttons below.
2. **A table wider than the screen says it scrolls.** In the notes and in a VetWiki article, a wide
   table (the One Health GMO table, for one) was cut at the right edge with nothing to show
   there was more. Its right edge now fades until the student scrolls to it, a line under it reads
   "เลื่อนตารางไปด้านข้างเพื่อดูคอลัมน์ที่เหลือ", and the table takes focus so the arrow keys scroll it.
3. **Notes tables and callouts are 14 px or more.** They were 0.93 em, which is 13 px on a desktop.
4. **A source title in a wiki article is a 44 px row to tap.** The link was a 19 px line of text.
5. **The wiki's "ที่มา" line reads as a document name.** It printed the stored pointer as written
   ("Role_of_Vet_in_One_Health_Saharuetai_2026 p.2-5"). It now goes through the same label as the
   notes reader. The stored pointer is not changed.
6. **Every subject with notes is in the VetWiki index.** Seven Year 2 subjects (97 topics) have notes
   whose sections have not yet been checked against a source, so they have no articles, and the index
   left them out. They are now listed under the shelves as "โน้ตที่ยังไม่ได้ตรวจทาน", each with its year
   and topic count, and open in the notes reader. Back from there reads "สารบัญ VetWiki".

## How it was checked

Unit: every subject with notes is a shelf or a notes-only row; the source line label.
Browser (`tests/e2e/notes-wiki-phone.spec.js`): the pill against the last line walked through the end
of a topic at 390 x 664, a wide table's cue and scroll, 44 px source titles, no raw file name in a
"ที่มา" line, the notes-only rows opening the notes, and 14 px tables and callouts at 1280.

## Not changed

Which sections count as checked, the wiki's sources, and the stored source pointers.
