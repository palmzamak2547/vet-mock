# 5.161.1: a smoother 3D Mochi

The 3D Mochi (the buddy stage on /app/mochi and the Motion Kit's 3D view, both built by
`buildMochi` in `src/lib/motion-kit/mochi/model-3d.js`) is sculpted smoother, with every value
derived rather than picked.

- Tessellation from the eye: a silhouette edge strays from the true curve by the sagitta
  r(1 - cos(pi/n)), and on the buddy stage the head is about 150 px in radius, so each part gets
  the side count that keeps its edge within 0.35 px: 48 sides for the head (it was 20, 1.8 px off,
  a visible polygon when zoomed), 36 for an ear, 32 for an arm. Glints and toe pads keep 20. One
  geometry per count, shared. Tubes (tail, stethoscope, scarf band) go from 8 to 12 sides.
- Rounded fabric and nose: the bandana, scarf knot, nose and book corners are drawn with quadratic
  curves; the stars keep their points.
- `Picture` in `src/components/Mochi.jsx` keeps its element and the image on screen when the pose
  changes (it was keyed by pose, which blanked the slot for a frame or two each time a review card
  turned or a verdict came in).
- Removed, never loaded: `public/motion/assets/3d/mochi-smooth-27-clips.glb` (2,392,340 bytes) and
  its clip list `animations.json`.
- Budget, from the pull request: triangles 30,144 to 58,536, unique geometry vertices 9,171 to
  35,764 (about 1.1 MB of GPU buffers), draw calls unchanged (84 meshes).
