# 5.167.0: the exam timetable downloads as an A4 PDF

Palm, 28 Oct 2569: "อยากให้คุณทำเอกสารสวยๆจาก Vetmock ให้ download ได้ด้วยอะครับ แบบตารางสอบฉบับอัพเดตล่าสุดแบบดีๆเลย ออกแบบดีๆ".

**ดาวน์โหลดตารางสอบ** on the timetable page opens the coming exam period as one A4 page at 300 dpi.
- Each paper shows its date and weekday, its time with a morning or afternoon tag, its room and its duration.
- Papers are grouped by week. The faculty's edition and the source are printed on the page.
- **ดาวน์โหลด PDF** saves a PDF, and **บันทึกเป็นรูป** saves the image. In an in-app browser such as LINE's, a hint says to hold the image to save it.

`src/lib/share-card/timetable-card.js` draws the page on a canvas from `examTimetable(year)` (`src/lib/exam-timetable.js`), with a layout check of its own.
