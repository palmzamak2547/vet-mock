# 5.186.0: the Epidemiology Panic card, and Avian and Equine Repro topics follow the 2569 syllabi

- **Epidemiology Panic card:** Epidemiology sits the final (4 Dec) but had no card, so its final pool was reachable
  only through its topics. The card is drawn in the set's manner: an engraving of a magnifying glass over a map of
  outbreak clusters, with an epidemic curve behind it. It was generated from a text prompt with Canva's image tool,
  exported at its full 1264 px, and keyed into one ink at 418 px the way the nine are (72% of its covered pixels are
  solid ink; the set runs 52 to 88%). The plum is a hue no other card uses; its text contrast is 10.5:1 in light and
  11.8:1 in dark (the set runs 5.5 to 10.8 and 10.4 to 12.7). The final's list now cards it, and POA stays on the
  no-card list, since it is examined through the term. The keying script is kept with the design kit, outside the repo.
- **209591 (Equine POA, lameness case):** the one item the PDF session left open. The key stays ก, flexion test: the
  printed feedback on the same page orders hoof testers, then flexion tests ("could be tried"), then "the next step
  would be to begin blocking nerves"; the class worksheet records the same order; and the MSD Veterinary Manual's
  lameness examination puts flexion tests before diagnostic analgesia. The re-check is noted in the row's `verified`.

The syllabus lane read the 2569-1 syllabi (docs/SYLLABUS-2569-1-Y5.md on its branch) and found two topics scoped
`both` that the 2569 timetables teach on one side of the midterm, plus the POA lecturers the 3107522 syllabus names.
Each row moves by its content, so no final content leaves the final.

- **Avian, `avian-intro`:** the intro is taught on 25 Aug, before the midterm, and the final covers 6 Oct to 17 Nov,
  yet 84 rows sat under it. A classifier read each row against the 2569 decks: 55 go to midterm disease topics (AI 8,

  read of the 62 rows leaving the final, for content a 2569 final lecture also teaches, moved one: 109006 (which group
  of poultry diseases are all zoonoses) names ND and salmonellosis but also Campylobacteriosis and listeriosis, both
  final topics, so it goes to Campylobacteriosis rather than ND. The five mare rows leaving the final are on the mare
