## What a student gets

- **Study panel under the player (`ClipStudy`):** it opens on the summary's one-liner and its first three key points, and ดูเพิ่ม shows the rest with จุดเน้น and ต้องจำ. Every line carries its time and seeks the player there. The panel holds its space while the card loads, so no empty-state text flashes.
  - **Chapters:** a bar, with what has been watched, and a list of the chapters with the slide on screen at each.
  - **Index:** terms and slide numbers.
  - **Transcript:** searchable, and it follows playback. It needs a sign-in.
- **ข้ามช่วงที่ไม่มีเนื้อหา:** on by default and remembered per device. It jumps a stretch that has no caption, is 70% or more silent by ffmpeg, and lasts 20 s or more. In mid-lecture the slide must also stay unchanged. Only playing on into a stretch jumps it; a seek or a cited moment inside one plays it. All times stay the original ones.
- **ดูต่อจาก m:ss:** reopening a clip offers the place the student stopped.
- **From a topic or a question to the stretch that teaches it:** the topic list's คลิป button, and ดูช่วงที่สอนหัวข้อนี้ under a question's source.
- **Our own copy:** a signed-in student plays the library's copy from private R2 storage behind the Chula gate. YouTube stays the fallback, and a guest goes straight to YouTube.

## How the content was made and checked

- **Scope:** the 192 uploads of @dai.1387 only. The 40 clips of eight other channels in its playlists are not downloaded, stored, summarised or shown until each owner approves.
- **Deterministic first:** captions, chapters from the lecture's deck, topic stretches with their evidence, slide frames cropped to the slide, and silence from ffmpeg. A model only drafts the summary text.
- **Summary gates, all of which must pass:**
  - lint: length caps; voice (no narration, no study advice, no talk of the exam); one sentence per item;
  - coverage of every must-cover item: concepts, stresses, slide marks and numbers;
  - no number or term without evidence at its moment;
  - a model verifier reading each item against that moment's transcript and slides;
  - a clarity reader. A held summary stays out of the batch.
- **The sample (Endometritis in mare, 74 min):** polished by hand against the transcript and slides. Its card is 250 words, 2.33% of the transcript, and covers all 108 must-cover items. It skips the 4:48 before the lecture.
- **Topic stretches:** a stretch is published only as a topic the lecturer set gives the session, or one the session's title names. Every stretch of the batch was read against its transcript. Five fell outside that rule, and all five were wrong (a PRRS lecture read as CSF and ASF on shared biosecurity words); none ships.

## Review

An independent review of the runtime code found these, all fixed in this release:
- **The transcript tab never loaded.** The effect's own `loading` state cancelled its request. The answer is now matched to its clip by a ref.
- **The arrow keys switched clips** on the study panel's tabs and on our own video. Both now keep their own arrows.
- **The resume offer was overwritten** by the player starting from zero, and the intro skip made it worse. The saved place now waits until the student resumes or has watched a minute elsewhere.
- **The panel kept the last clip's state** on the next clip of a playlist. It now remounts per clip.
- **The transcript list scrolled the whole dialog,** and its own scrolling paused the following. Only the list scrolls now, and its own scroll events are ignored.
- **Every transcript line re-rendered twice a second.** The seek handler is now stable.
- **A seek into the intro jumped it again.** The intro is skipped only when the clip starts from zero by itself.
- **Signed-in students lost YouTube's captions** on our copy. Our copy now has WebVTT captions built from the transcript.
- **A link that expired** under a lecture paused overnight fell back to YouTube. It is now renewed once, at the same second.
- **The transcript answer was cacheable** on a shared computer. It is now no-store.
- **A missing transcript showed a load error.** It now says there is none.
- **Odd text:** "8, 9 Anatomy" read ", 9 Anatomy"; index rows repeated; slide bullets showed. Fixed and pinned in `tests/unit/video-registry.test.mjs`.
- **The topic list's คลิป count** counted stretches. It now counts clips.
- **Transcripts for this batch** are now in R2, 17 of 17, checked by HEAD.
