## Pipeline fixes found while repairing

- **The library behind the Chula wall.** Since 5.184.0 the anon key gets 401 on the library. #166 moved the deck step to `scripts/lib/library-read.mjs`. The deck step was re-run for every clip without a deck.
  - Nothing a card shows was lost to the 401. Every clip's deck decision predates the wall.
  - The two recorded deck errors have other causes. F1jwpqQCGBM's lecturer set names two decks that are not in the library (`avian-influenza-jiroj-2026`, `embryo-breakout-analysis-jiroj-2026`). The GI I deck sits in Supabase storage.
  - A stale deck error is now cleared on the next attempt.
- **Long chapters split.** When the slides are taught early, the deck path can leave one chapter of 83 to 134 minutes. A chapter that no page can split past 12 minutes is now cut by time, like a lecture without a deck.
  - `speechCuts` is shared by both paths. On a lecture without a deck the output is identical, which was checked on two clips.
  - 35 live cards still have a chapter over 12 minutes. They change only when re-analysed, which needs their chapter lines redone.
- **"10,000" became "10, 000".** Cutting a chapter line at commas rejoined every line, so a thousands separator gained a space and the number check failed. Ten summaries were held by this. A line is now rewritten only when it loses a part, and a thousands separator is no list comma.
- **Thai digits on slides** (๕) reach the writer and the checker as 5.
- **A break is no gap.** The key-point gap rule now subtracts class breaks. An 80-minute exercise counted as a gap in the economics lecture.
- **Empty lines on break chapters.** A chapter that is mostly a class break keeps an empty line, which the card leaves out, and the two model readings skip it.
- **Voice lint:**
  - Class arrangements are flagged: "พักเที่ยง", "พัก 10 นาที", "ส่งคำถาม", "กลับมาเจอกัน".
  - "red" is framing only when it names a mark ("marked in red", "red คือ"), so "Red meat" and "red muscle fiber" pass.
  - "การสอบ" alone is no exam talk. "การสอบกลับสินค้า" (traceability) on a live card was flagged by the 7 Nov rule.
  - Letters from another alphabet are caught: a Cyrillic "диаметร" and an Arabic "حجم" were in two held summaries.
- **COM2 summary page:** a Korean syllable sat in an elbow dysplasia sign ("가uddling gait"). The corrupted word is gone.

## Review of the new code

An independent review found nine defects. All are fixed with tests, and the five re-analysed lectures were analysed again and re-keyed:
- A page taught at a chapter's end fell in no part, so one slide was in no chapter.
- A mark deep in a break cut in the silence, so two chapters started in silence. Speech now resumes the next chapter.
- The six-minute page split never looked at a shortened first half again, so pages that could split it were cut by time, under concept names. That half is now checked again, and a part with slides keeps a slide heading.
- An acronym covered a red mark as a substring ("ADI" inside "irradiation"). It now has to be a whole word.
- "ตอนสอบ" and "วันสอบ" hit traceability and calibration (สอบกลับ, สอบเทียบ).
- "in red blood cell" read as framing, and "ให้สัตว์พัก 10 นาทีก่อนวัดความดัน" read as class talk. A timed break counts only as a whole line or with class wording.
- A Thai mark written as a list ("เขตโรคระบาด, เขตเฝ้าระวังโรค") could never be covered. Any one of its terms now counts.
- A bare ล้าน read as 0 ("ประมาณล้านตัว").
- A deck added after an analysis did not make the analysis stale, so a hand-chosen deck needed `--force`.

The new class-talk rule then found a break notice on a passing card ("พักเบรกถึง 10:30"). A new lint rule for two chapters with the same line found two cards; both are fixed.

## The audit fixes (formerly #163)

- **Transcripts for every carded clip.** `/api/video-file` served a transcript only for a clip with our own copy, so 5 of 71 cards showed one. Every card's transcript is now served, signed-in, through the Chula gate.
- **One row per clip under a topic.** `clipRows` (src/lib/video-library.js) gives each clip one row: its surest stretch, plus the minutes and number of its stretches.
- **The index loses heading words.** Acronyms stay when the decks write them in capitals. A capital word drops when a quarter of the lectures use it, and otherwise goes back to its usual spelling. Cut endings come back. Shorter duplicates within a minute go. Words a reader judged to be heading or table words are dropped in `polished.json` (9 clips).
- **Chapter titles:** a stray letter or a running header ("Equine") leaves the chapter untitled, and its line says what it teaches.
- **2025 summaries:** three no longer say their class was "NOT IN EXAM".
- **Credit line:** the shelf card and player say "โดย".
- **Review:** the independent review found four defects, all fixed in 86a3647c: real capital terms dropped, a time-blind duplicate rule, a sign-in prompt promising slides, and an overpromising changelog.
