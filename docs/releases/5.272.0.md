# 5.272.0: Vet Juris midterm, the recorded paper

The three midterm topics of Veterinary Jurisprudence, Ethics and Animal Welfare (`vet-practice-act-2545`,
`vet-professional-ethics`, `animal-welfare-act-2557`) had no questions. This release converts the senior record of
that midterm one for one, in the format it was recorded in, with the answer the record gives.

## What changed

- **33 recorded items** appended to `src/data/questions-vet-juris.js`, ids 218700-218732.
  - 218700-218711, `vet-practice-act-2545`: the 12 multiple-choice items of the practice-act part. Vet 86 wrote on
    the same copy that their paper had these 12 items exactly.
  - 218712-218729, `animal-welfare-act-2557`: welfare items 2-14 and 16-20 (multiple choice), each ticked by Vet 86 as
    sat. 218723 carries the sow-stall photograph extracted from the PDF at its embedded resolution
    (`public/figures/questions/vet-juris-218723.webp`, 719 x 482).
  - 218730-218731, `animal-welfare-act-2557`: welfare items 21 and 23, recorded as a question with a one-line
    answer and converted as `short`. Vet 86 marked both as not on their paper.
  - 218732, `vet-professional-ethics`: the written ethics case (a dog that died during thoracoscopic surgery), as
    `short` with the recorded answer as the model answer.
- **Two origins**: `Vet Juris midterm Vet 85, Vet 86 บันทึกหลังสอบ` (paper, cohorts 85 and 86, midterm) for items
  Vet 86 confirmed; `Vet Juris midterm Vet 85 บันทึกหลังสอบ` (paper, cohort 85, midterm) for the rest.
  `sourceType` is `student-compilation`; scope comes from the topics, which are midterm topics.
- **Six source documents** in `src/data/source-docs.js`: the Vet 85 record with Vet 86's marks (11 pp.), the Vet 86
  re-typed copy with key (13 pp.), and the 2568 decks the explanations cite (practice act 58 pp., welfare week 1
  72 pp., welfare week 2 89 pp., ethics 63 pp.). Every new row carries `sourcePages`.

Stems and options are the record's words. Typing errors in the record are corrected and each correction is listed in
the row's `verified`; where a word in the record cannot be read (practice act 5 and 6), the Vet 86 re-typed wording is
used and named. Phrasings the house style checks flag (ทำการ before a verb in three options of practice
act 3, มีความเสี่ยง in the ethics case) and the word for students in practice act 5 option b (now ผู้ที่กำลังศึกษา)
are reworded without changing the meaning, and listed the same way. Practice act 5 was recorded with options a, b, d and e only, and is kept with four.
