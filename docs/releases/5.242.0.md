## What was held back, and why

- **Four picks were the wrong slide** (`milk-meat-hygiene:105745`, `:214195`, `swine-clinic:105579`,
  `zoonoses:210530`): each page is on another topic (raw-milk storage, milk colour, a PCV2 test,
  yellow fever). Redrawn and read again; none shows a slide.
- **Three keys in doubt.** Redrawn against today's bank. Two keys were corrected in the bank since
  the pass was read and now agree with their slide (`zoonoses:106150`, penned livestock more than
  free-range; `equine-medicine:202053`, HR 28 to 44 and RR 12 to 16); they show no slide until the
  next run matches the new wording. One still disagrees (below).
- **A slide that does not show the key by itself shows nothing.** 243 picks over both passes were
  judged so (the 30 of 5.211.0 among them); none is in the index.
- **Pest control.** Four questions keyed "Destruction of the pest" (`milk-meat-hygiene:212030`,
  `:212037`, `:212038`, `:212041`) rest on a slide headed "Direct demolition" or a handout page
  headed "การกำจัดโดยตรง". Read again: the same group of methods (baits, rodenticide, traps,
  fumigation), and each explanation already names it "direct demolition". No change.
- **Handout pages.** Pages of a printed handout read as pictures are shown whole with no
  highlight, as in 5.211.0: 9 in Milk Hygiene and Meat Inspection (7 handout pages, 2 slides), 56
  over all subjects. All nine Milk and Meat pages were redrawn: each prints the sentence that
  holds the key.
- **One more turned down on this reading:** `zoonoses:210505` pointed at a title page with a
  campaign poster; the statement is not on it.

## How it was checked

- **Every pick of the pass was looked at** beside its question and key before it reached the
  index: 260 undecided or re-matched picks gave 145 right, 108 weak, 4 wrong, 3 keys in doubt.
  Only right ones are published; every published row's verdict is recorded for its exact page.
- **A second reading before release.** 32 of the 136 new slides, drawn at random, were drawn again
  from the page as the student sees it, with the question, all options, the key and the
  explanation: 31 hold, 1 turned down (`zoonoses:210505`, above). All 32 pages come from a
  document of the question's own subject, and none shows a student's name.
- **Every page image answers** at the slide store: 791 pages over the ten subjects.
- `npm run lint:evidence`: 952 slides in 10 subjects. `tests/unit/answer-evidence.test.mjs`
  covers the two matching fixes (a Thai sara am split by the text layer, and a fact set over many
  short lines). `npm run lint:data:all`, the unit suite and a build with `npm run perf:budget`
  pass.
