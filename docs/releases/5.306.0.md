## What changed

- `src/data/evidence/zoonoses.generated.js`: 140 questions on 102 pages of 11 documents, from 61. The decks shelved
  after the first matching run (Prion Diseases, Epidemiological approach to zoonoses, and two on wildlife and
  emerging zoonoses) were read and matched. Each of the 91 proposed slides was drawn beside its question and key and
  looked at: 83 were found right and are published, 7 weak and 1 wrong are left out. A second, independent reading of
  20 published picks agreed on 18; the other two (two of a three-part key framed, and a deciding term only in the
  slide title) were withdrawn before publishing. No slide published before this release was lost.
- `scripts/evidence/build.mjs`: `find` keeps a pick that was looked at and found right while its question is
  unchanged (`keepSeen`), and does not ask about it again. This run had found no pick for two such questions; their
  published slides were put back from the verdict's own record, which names the page and the boxes it was given on.
- `tests/unit/answer-evidence.test.mjs`: `keepSeen` keeps the pick and its state, refreshes the question's own
  fields, and asks again for an edited question or a pick that was not found right.
- `docs/ANSWER-EVIDENCE.md`: the run and the rule.
- The library titles of the four decks above now match the names the Zoonoses final wrap-up cites, which is how a
  wrap-up line finds its picture (`slideIndex` matches by title).

## How it was tested

- Unit tests, `lint:evidence` and the data lints.
- Every page the new index cites was requested from the image host: 102 of 102 are served.
- Wrap-up pictures counted with the live rule: Zoonoses final from 0 to 21; One Health final stays 24 and every
  other wrap-up is unchanged.
