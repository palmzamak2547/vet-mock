# 5.232.0: the schedule page, rebuilt to the screen gate

Wave 7 of the 15 Nov redesign (`/app/schedule`, `src/views/ScheduleView.jsx`, `src/components/WeekTimetable.jsx`,
`src/components/AcademicCalendar.jsx`, and the page's own stylesheet `src/styles-schedule.css`).
`src/components/TimetableSheet.jsx` (the printable sheet) is not changed.

## What the page is for, and what it holds now

One job: tell a Year 5 student what is on today, and which paper comes next, with one way into practising it.

Top to bottom:

1. The classes of the day in question: today while a class is still to come or under way, else the next day that has
   one. Each class is called by its course name, the one Home and the exam rows use (the faculty's abbreviations
   were cut short, "VET EPIDEMI (LAB)"). A room every class of the day shares is said once, beside the day.
   "ทั้งสัปดาห์" opens the five days in place; there every class is a link into its notes or practice. The week is
   mounted only when it is opened.
2. The next paper as the one card: date leaf, course, day, time and room, days left, and the page's only filled
   button ("ฝึกข้อสอบวิชานี้", or "อ่านสรุปวิชานี้" when a course has notes and no questions).
3. The other papers as rows on a hairline: date, course, days left on the right, day, time and room. Nothing to press.
4. One group of rows for what is opened now and then, each body mounted when it is opened: ฝึกวิชาอื่น (one button
   per paper that has content), ดาวน์โหลดตารางสอบ, สอบที่ผ่านแล้ว, ประมวลรายวิชาและตารางสอน, and the registration
   and payment calendar (its row shows the nearest event or the window that closes first).
5. A one-line foot: the timetable may change, with "แจ้งข้อมูลผิด".

In an exam period the classes are off, so the papers come first and the line saying so goes to the end.

Decisions and reasons:

- The week was five cards of every class, 3,000 px on a phone before the first paper. A student opening this page
  asks one question first ("วันนี้เรียนอะไร ที่ไหน"), so the page answers that one day and keeps the week one tap away.
- A past paper used to fade to half opacity, under the contrast floor. It now keeps full ink and sits behind its own row.
- The hero line held four facts (term, year, both exam windows). The exam window is said by the papers themselves, so
  the hero keeps the year and the term.
- The registration calendar's title, legend and list moved into its row; the row's own line says the one date that
  matters now.
- Arrows inside button labels ("ฝึกข้อสอบวิชานี้ →") are gone; the button shape says it is a button.

## Motion

A folded body and the opened week rise in by 6 px with opacity, `--dur` and `--ease-out`, only after a tap and never
on arrival; the row's chevron turns with `--dur-fast`. Both are inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, so
under reduced motion what was opened is simply there. The sheet's loading line appears only after 400 ms.
