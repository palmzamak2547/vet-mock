# 5.192.3: read-aloud seeks, skips and stops cleanly; year pages ask for the year; chat and Research fixes

## What shipped

Lane fixes from a subsystem sweep. Each finding was confirmed by two independent reviews (one reproducing it
against the real code, one checking intent and reachability), fixed with a test seen failing first, and read
again before it was picked.

1. **Read-aloud** (`src/lib/read-aloud.js`, 3 lines in `src/lib/tts.js`): a device-voice failure note stays until
   the bar is closed; a seek while the piece downloads, sits in its gap or failed is kept; "next" past the last point
   ends the reading so a replay starts at the stem; a stopped reading's late failure no longer cuts the next one off;
   leftover clip downloads after a hand-over to the device voice no longer throw. No function that feeds `clipKey`
   changed, so no shared clip was renamed.
2. **Year pages ask for the year first** (`src/lib/view-route.js`, `src/lib/phase-return.js`, `src/App.jsx`):
   `yearGateFor` runs on every view change, so the bar, the rail, a Home card or the palette no longer open a
   year page as year 5 with a subject grid that never finishes loading. `/app/phase` and `/app/reading` are
   year-scoped. The saved front-door return no longer outlives its flow.
3. **VetMock AI chat** (`api/_lib/ai/*`, `api/ai-chat.js`): a short turn naming its own course no longer inherits the
   previous course; ✓ ✅ ❌ ✗ reach the writer as + and − instead of empty cells; limits key on the signed-in student
   (`gate.userId || ip:<addr>`), and a burst refusal gives its day count back; a library outage is not told as "no
   such file". Limiter keys changed, so every student started a fresh window at deploy.
4. **VetMock Research** (`research/src/lib/intake/pii.js`, `recipe.js`, `report/build.js`, `ComparePane.jsx`): the
   methods draft and the double-entry comparison mask hidden and personal-data values (and keys) on screen, in the
   copy, and in the HTML, Word and CSV downloads.
5. **Next school year's rollover** (`src/lib/current-phase.js`): last school year's `1-final` pick follows the
   timetable instead of being kept as the student's choice through the next midterm.
6. The 5.192.2 release record.

Checks before merge: 33 affected unit files on the combined branch, 383 pass, 0 fail, 6 todo (the evals' existing
weak cases); per lane, failing-first tests, the Research suite 1755/1755 (also UTC) and its build, and the
exam-period matrix under Asia/Bangkok, UTC and America/Los_Angeles.
