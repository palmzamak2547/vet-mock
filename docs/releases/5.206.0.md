# 5.206.0: calculation explanations read as steps

Sixteen explanations of calculation questions in the Year-5 Epidemiology final banks were one
paragraph each, 300 to 600 characters with the formula, the substitution, the result and the
reasons the other options are wrong all in one run. On a phone that is a wall of text at the
moment a student wants to see where their own working went wrong.

## What a student sees

- **Steps, one per line, each with a bold label**: `สูตร`, `แทนค่า`, `แปลผล` (or the label that
  fits the step, such as `ตัวหาร` or `แปลงหน่วย`).
- **The other options on their own lines.** Seven explanations account for every wrong option and
  carry the house section `❌ ทำไมข้ออื่นผิด`. Seven do not cover every option; they end with
  `ข้อควรระวัง` and the same kind of lines, and the wrong-answer coach stays on offer for them.
  Two end with a table and a closing sentence.
- **A table where values are compared**, in four of the sixteen (for example the sample size at
  each confidence level, or six test measures side by side), two columns so it fits a phone.
- **The voice reads what it read before.** Read-aloud says the original paragraph, not the labels
  and the table.

Questions: 214506, 214547, 214560, 214561, 214572, 214576, 214636, 214650
(`questions-y5-epid-final-marked.js`) and 213503, 213520, 213521, 213525, 213535, 213538, 213545,
213558 (`questions-y5-epid-final-recall.js`).

## How

A text laid out for the eye is poor text for the ear and for a model, so the paragraph is kept:

| | |
|---|---|
| `explainSay` (new field) | the paragraph the explanation was written as |
| `wholeExplain(q)` in `src/lib/rich-blocks.js` | `explainSay` when there is one, else `explain` |
| `src/components/Question.jsx` | the read-aloud job reads `wholeExplain`; no phrase is marked in an explanation read from `explainSay`, and the explanation box is still the block kept in view |
| `scripts/tts-clips.mjs`, `api/study-coach.js`, `api/_lib/ai/corpus.js`, `api/_lib/ai/quiz.js`, `scripts/evidence/build.mjs` | read `wholeExplain`, so recorded clips keep their keys and a model never sees table bars |
| `scripts/lint-question-tables.mjs` | fails when the paragraph says a figure the screen does not show, when the screen shows a figure neither the paragraph nor the question holds, and when `explainSay` equals the explanation or holds a table row |
| `docs/QUESTION-STANDARD.md` (C1), `AGENTS.md` | the rule for writers: when the section on the other options may be used, what to use when not every option is covered, and that a line says which option it is about |

## What was checked

- **Nothing was reworded for the voice.** The kept paragraph is byte-identical to the explanation
  on main in 16 of 16. The other 256 questions of the two files are deep-equal to main, and on the
  sixteen no field but the explanation changed.
- **No figure lost, none added.** Every figure of each paragraph is on screen, and the screen
  shows no figure that the paragraph, the stem, the passage, its title or the options do not hold
  (the lint above; 79 tables in the bank, all drawn as tables).
- **Every wrong option is accounted for** in the seven explanations that carry
  `❌ ทำไมข้ออื่นผิด`, read option by option, because that heading hides the wrong-answer coach.
- **Two reviews** of the screens and the data (phone and desktop screenshots of the feedback
  panel): no blocker. Applied from them: lines that say which option they are about, the six-row
  table cut to two columns, `ข้อควรระวัง` lines in place of a closing paragraph, the AI quiz card
  reading the paragraph.
- **Tests.** `npm run lint:data` passes (the glossary index was regenerated: question 213503 is
  now listed under "95% CI"). `npm run test:unit`: 3,997 tests, 3,991 pass, 0 fail, 6 todo (the
  six are retrieval-quality cases marked weak on 28 Oct). `tests/e2e/question-tables.spec.js` and
  `tests/e2e/read-aloud.spec.js` on Chromium desktop, Chromium mobile and WebKit mobile against the
  dev server: 33 of 33 before the final wording pass, which changed bank text only; the test of a
  laid-out explanation picks its question from the bank, and the pull request's CI runs the whole
  matrix on the final text.

## Known limits

- The explanations that end with `ข้อควรระวัง` do not say why every wrong option is wrong, which
  is why they do not carry the heading. Writing those lines is new bank content, not layout, and is
  left to the bank's owner.
- A step that wraps to three lines on a phone sits directly above the next step; the bold label is
  what separates them.
- The floating "ดูตาราง" button can sit over the right edge of an explanation's table until the
  student scrolls.
- Not walked in a signed-in session on vetmock.com.
