## What changed

- **Subjects and topics taught** (`subjects`, `topics`), only additions: 98 subject links on 65
  profiles and 246 topic links on 60, read from the repository's own course materials: a topic's
  `lecturer` in `src/data/curriculum.js`, a session's `lecturers` in `src/data/course-syllabi.js`
  (2569 schedules), and a note's `lecturer` (its title slide) in the files `note-corpus.js` lists.
  The faculty screen's subject chips and each card's subject tag read this field, so a student who
  filters by subject now finds these lecturers. Every link is in the table below.
- **Positions**, from the current department rosters (English and Thai pages agree; the older
  `researcher_info` pages still print the previous title):
  `sumpun-thammacharoen` รศ. to ศ. (physiology-unit), `praopilas-phakdeedindan` อ. to ผศ.
  (animal-husbandry), `suphachai-nuanualsuwan` รองศาสตราจารย์ to ศาสตราจารย์
  (veterinary-public-health), all under `https://vet.chula.ac.th/en/department/`.
- **Alias**: `chaidate-inchaisri` gains `ชัยเดช อินทร์ไชยศรี`, the spelling in
  `src/data/epi-module5.js:5` and `:38` (the roster spells อินทร์ชัยศรี), so that label resolves.
- **New profile**: `tussapon-boonyarattanasoonthorn`, named on the title slide of the CULAC
  handling and euthanasia deck (`src/data/notes-y2-physio-lab-2.js:24`). Thai name, title
  (น.สพ.ดร.), role (นักวิจัย) and work email from https://culac.chula.ac.th/about/staff. Two papers
  already in the directory whose author lists carry his full name (DOI 10.1016/j.jtcme.2024.05.004,
  10.2147/jep.s382895). No photo.
- `scripts/instructor-course-teaching.mjs` holds these inputs, and
  `scripts/generate-instructor-directory.mjs` applies them over the research cache, so a later
  regeneration keeps them. `src/data/instructors-directory.js` was patched with the same values
  (the research cache is not in the repository); `instructor-index.generated.js` regenerated
  (gzip 25,237 to 25,719 bytes; the `lecturerIndex` budget is 25,900).
