## What changed

- `src/data/evidence/zoonoses.generated.js`: 178 questions on 137 pages of 12 documents, up from 151 questions on 11
  documents.
- `src/data/evidence/aquatic-clinic.generated.js`: 181 questions on 139 pages of 12 documents, up from 144 questions
  on 11 documents.
- Every forecast item without a slide was given a page and the lines on it that state the key, chosen by hand on
  this year's deck first. Two Zoonoses items (210578, 210579) use the earlier wildlife deck, because this year's deck
  has no slide on *A. cantonensis* or on targeting the reservoir host.
- Every pick was then checked by eye on a picture of the page with its highlight drawn, and only the ones judged to
  state the key were published:
  - Zoonoses: 36 items lacked a slide. 30 were picked and 6 have no page that prints the answer. 26 passed the
    first look; two were picked again and one of them passed. Three stay without a slide: two written answers whose
    points are spread over pages that do not print them all, and the country where BSE was first reported, which no
    page names.
  - Aquatic: 83 items lacked a slide. 42 were picked and 36 have no page that prints the answer (most are dose
    calculations). 30 passed the first look; one ordering question was judged from its line text, because the
    picture showed only the first of its numbered steps, and passed. The five biosecurity items added in 5.310.0
    and 105122 (rewritten in 5.311.0) were picked after they landed and all six passed. Eleven stay without a slide:
    the highlighted lines carry only part of the key, or the slide prints a different detail.
- 33 Zoonoses and 27 Aquatic page images were added to the evidence image folder. The index names no image path.
