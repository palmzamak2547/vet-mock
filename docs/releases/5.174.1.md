# 5.174.1: Panic Mode cards for the Year-5 final

Since 5.173.0 the timetable picks the paper on its own, and for Year 5 that has been the final since the
midterm ended (25 Sep, 11:30). `PANIC_CARD_PAPERS` held only `'2569-1' → 5 → '1-mid'`, so every Year-5
subject page showed no Panic Mode card. This files the final as one more key, no code:

- `'1-final'` cards for the nine subjects that sit it (all nine sit both papers in
  `exam-papers.generated.js`).
- `noCard`: epidemiology sits only the final, but its card is not drawn yet; POA is examined through the
  term (`examScope: 'continuous'`).

Panic pool on the final, per subject, from `Q_PANIC_COUNTS_BY_SUBJECT_BY_SCOPE.final` on main before this
release (it grows as final content lands): Equine Med 233, Swine 127, Aquatic 89, Equine Repro 78, Avian 60,
Zoonoses 43, Milk and Meat 24, One Health 11, Food Industry 1. The card prints the pool it opens.
