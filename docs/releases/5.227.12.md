# 5.227.12: the first message to the assistant is not refused again

## What happened

Release 5.227.11 (live 20:16 on 15 Nov 2026) opened one connection to the limiter's store as an instance starts. On its first deployment the first chat request answered 503 after 5.5 s ("ระบบจำกัดการใช้งานขัดข้องชั่วคราว"); the next two answered 200 in 3.1 s and 3.4 s. This is the refusal 5.227.10 had removed two hours earlier.

## Cause

After the warming call answers, the instance counts as warm and every later store call is given 1.8 s. A chat asks two limits side by side, so a second connection is opened beside the warmed one, and a new connection from this region took about 2 s that evening (the log of 18:38: "first store call of this instance took 2019 ms"). The second call ran out of time and the limiter failed closed.

## The fix

`api/_lib/rate-limit.js`: a warm call is given 3.5 s instead of 1.8 s, since any call can be the one that opens a connection. The first call of an instance keeps its 6 s. A store that is really down still fails closed. A call slower than 1.2 s on a warm instance is logged once a minute, so the logs show how often a connection is opened late.

## Checks

The three limiter test files pass (`rate-limit-cold-start`, `rate-limit-warm`, `rate-limit-failure`, `rate-limit-window`).

## Not verified, and open

- On production: three first requests after this deploys must all answer 200.
- Why a new connection takes about 2 s. Production moved from Node 26 to Node 24 at 17:41 the same day and no refusal is in the logs before that; the two log lines of this file and of 5.227.10 are the evidence to read before deciding whether the pin stays.
