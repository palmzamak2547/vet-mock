# 5.256.0: Food Industry QC final, the seniors' marked points on this term's final topics

The Food Industry and Quality Control final is on 3 Dec 2026. Release 5.177.0 already turned the seniors' recorded
items and exam-word points for this final into questions. This release goes through every point the seniors marked on
their copies of the decks (highlight, star, red pen, the legend tree) for the three final topics, and writes a question
wherever the bank had none and this year's deck or class teaches the point.

## This term's midterm and final are kept apart

The final topics `fiqc-haccp` and `fiqc-poultry-export` (14 Oct) and `fiqc-aquatic` (28 Oct) are all taught after
the 22 Sep midterm, and `topicOnPaper` agrees for each; the four midterm topics (19 Aug to 9 Sep) agree too. Every item
was placed by content into one of the three final topics; none could belong to a midterm topic. The new rows carry no
`examScope`.
