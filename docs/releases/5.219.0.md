## Pipeline fixes found tonight
- **The media run waited eight hours on its own lock.** Wrapped in the shared heavy-job lock, `video-media.mjs` saw that lock as another lane's job. It now waits only on a live holder that is not its own wrapper (`HEAVY_LOCK_PID`, listed in the env contract).
- **Why 207 copies failed on 9 Nov was never logged:** a failure kept the first 300 characters of the command. It now keeps yt-dlp's own last words.
- **Hand-chosen decks blocked a first summary:** a `polished.json` holding only `decks` made the summary step skip the clip.
- **Decks:** the title match took a two-page class timetable for two avian lectures and found nothing for six. A timetable is never a deck now, and when no title word is distinctive the subject's decks carrying the recording's teaching date are taken (3 Nov had four).
- **Checks:** a Thai slide mark matches either spelling of sara am.
- **Chapter titles:** bullets, URLs, a split sara am, page numbers and course codes come off; titles that named a slide not taught in those minutes were replaced by hand.
