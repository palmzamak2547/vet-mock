# 5.252.0: read-aloud wherever a question is read (16 Nov 2026)

## What a student notices

- **Results review.** Each row has the listen button beside its question; the เหตุผล has its own where it is open
  (a correct row folded shut shows none until it is opened). Scores, filters and the list do not change.
- **Spaced review.** The card's front has a listen button; its เหตุผล gets one only once the card is turned over.
  Grading or leaving stops the reading.
- **The daily question.** The question is read with its options in the order shown, each with its letter; the
  เฉลย gets a button only after an answer. The bar sits above the dialog, and a tap on it never closes the dialog.
- **Wrap-up pages.** Each item has a listen button that reads the item as it shows in the mode on screen: its title,
  keywords and lines, without its buttons, slides, clip minutes and citations.
- One engine, one bar and one voice settings dialog as on the practice card (`src/components/ReadAloudListen.jsx`):
  one voice per item, the words read are the words shown, and leaving a screen stops its reading.
- A question or เหตุผล that already has a recorded clip plays that clip here too, because the sentence read is the
  one the practice card reads; anything else is read by the device voice. A timed paper is unchanged.

## How it was checked

- `tests/e2e/read-aloud-everywhere.spec.js`, one test per surface, with a stubbed speech engine: the button is there,
  the text handed to the voice comes in the order shown, and leaving the screen stops the reading.
- `tests/e2e/perf-budget.spec.js` (Home's first load) unchanged, `npm run perf:budget`, the unit suite, `npm run build`.
- Screenshots at 390 x 664 and 1280 x 800, light and dark, for each of the four screens.

Not checked on a real iPhone, iPad or Android phone.
