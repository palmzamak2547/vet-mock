# 5.213.0: the answer panel uses the passage and the checked references the bank holds

Asked for: two places where the answer panel ignored data the question bank already holds.

|---|---|
| Questions in the bank / deliverable | 9,043 / 8,832 |
| Questions with a `passage` | 98 (all deliverable) |

## What changed

- **The wrong-answer coach is shown the passage** (`api/study-coach.js`, `coachPassage`,
  `missMaterial`). A question without `say` gets its passage and `passage_title` ahead of the stem,
  labelled as shown above the question. A `say` question gets none: `wholeStem(q)` already holds the
  whole sentence with its numbers. The rule lives once, as `passageBesideStem(q)` in
  `src/lib/rich-blocks.js`, and the coach's footer reads it ("เรียบเรียงจากเรื่องที่ให้มา ตัวเลือก
  และคำอธิบายของข้อนี้" when a passage went in).
- **Cap: 2,000 characters** (`MAX_PASSAGE_CHARS`, label included). The longest passage a coach
  question holds is 1,491 characters with its title, so all 15 go whole; the cap only bounds a
  future one. A longer passage is cut after its last whole line that fits, so a table row is never
  split, and the cut is marked `[the passage continues]`. A single prose line longer than the cap is
  cut at a word.
- **Cache key**: unchanged in form. It already hashes the whole material the model is shown, and
  the passage is now part of that material, so editing a passage retires its entries. A question
  without a passage (or with `say`) builds byte-for-byte the material it built before, so its cached
  answers stay valid. The grounding check reads the same material, so a figure the model takes from
  the passage table is no longer refused as ungrounded.
- **Source panel lists the checked references** (`src/components/QSourceChip.jsx`,
  `src/lib/source-refs.js`). After the answer and "ดูเต็ม", an "อ้างอิง" row lists each reference:
  its title, a link only when `safeLinkUrl` passes the url, it is absolute https and it is not a
  Google Drive file (a member who cannot open one meets a dead link; vca.vetmock.com also leaves
  them as text), opening a new
  tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`, and its locator as text below. The wording follows
  vca.vetmock.com's provenance list (title linked, locator after it).
  On the bank: 409 references, 399 linked, 10 as text. The panel's own source-document link
  is unchanged.
- **The "ตรวจกับ" line leaves bare addresses to the references list** (`verifiedLine` in
  `src/lib/source-refs.js`, display only, the bank is untouched). On a question with
  `verificationSources`, a bare url in `verified` and the separator before it are left out of the
  line, since the list below names the same source. This changes the line on 29 questions (all
  deliverable, none left empty), for example "Immunology for VCA 2026, หน้า 15-16; https://drive…"
  now reads "Immunology for VCA 2026, หน้า 15-16". A question without references keeps its line
  exactly as the bank holds it.
- **Source document link names the question number**: "เปิดเอกสารต้นฉบับ (อ้างหน้า 9 ข้อ 2)"
  instead of "(อ้างหน้า 9)".

## Known limits

- Token cost is estimated, not measured (no provider call was made): DeepSeek does not publish a
  Thai ratio. Counting a Thai character as about one token and a Latin character as about 0.3, the
  longest real passage (1,528 characters with its label, 320 of them Thai) adds about 700 input
  tokens per call, and a full 2,000-character cap at most about 2,000.
- The chip was checked in Playwright's Chromium only, not by eye on a phone, in WebKit or Firefox
  (CI runs those).
- The review mode of the coach still gets each stem without its passage (clipped to 300 characters).
