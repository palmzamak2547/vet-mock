# 5.317.0: เลือกให้แอปเป็นภาษาอังกฤษได้ทุกหน้า

## สิ่งที่นิสิตเห็น

- **แถวภาษาอยู่บนสุดของเมนูธีม** เปิดได้ทั้งจากหัวแอปและหน้าโปรไฟล์ เลือก English แล้วหน้าที่เปิดอยู่เปลี่ยนเป็นภาษาอังกฤษทันที
  ไม่ต้องโหลดหน้าใหม่ เลือกไทยก็กลับมาเหมือนเดิมทุกตัวอักษร เครื่องนี้จำภาษาที่เลือกไว้ เปิดครั้งหน้าจะเป็นภาษานั้นตั้งแต่เฟรมแรก
- **เปลี่ยนแค่ตัวแอป ไม่เปลี่ยนเนื้อหา** ปุ่ม เมนู หัวข้อ คำอธิบาย วันที่ และตัวนับถอยหลังเป็นภาษาอังกฤษ ส่วนโจทย์ ตัวเลือก เฉลย
  โน้ตที่เขียนเอง ความเห็นใต้ข้อ ชื่ออาจารย์และชื่อนิสิต ชื่อภาษาไทยของอวัยวะใน Atlas และรายการในอัปเดตล่าสุด ยังเป็นภาษาที่เขียนไว้
- **การ์ดสรุปช่วงสอบเป็นภาษาที่เลือก** ทั้งหัวการ์ด หน่วย ชื่อวิชา และประโยคปิดท้าย ปีการศึกษาเป็น ค.ศ. ข้อความที่คัดลอกหรือแชร์ไปพร้อมการ์ด
  ก็เป็นภาษาเดียวกัน สลับภาษาแล้วการ์ดวาดใหม่ทันที
- **วันที่อ่านแบบอังกฤษ** "ศุกร์ 25 ก.ย." เป็น "Fri 25 Sep" ปี พ.ศ. เป็น ค.ศ. ทั้งแบบสี่หลัก สองหลัก "26/11/2569" และ
  "ภาคการศึกษาต้น 2569" ส่วนเวลา "10:00 น." เป็น "10:00"
- **โหลดภาษาอังกฤษไม่ได้ก็ไม่ค้าง** ถ้าเปิดครั้งแรกตอนออฟไลน์ หน้ายังเป็นภาษาไทยและเมนูบอกว่าเป็นไทยตามจริง กลับมาออนไลน์แล้วกด English อีกครั้งได้เลย
- **คนที่ใช้ภาษาไทยไม่ต้องโหลดอะไรเพิ่ม** ตัวแปลและคำศัพท์จะโหลดก็ต่อเมื่อเลือก English เท่านั้น

## What changed

- `src/lib/ui-lang.js` (new): the language key `vmx-lang`, `readUiLang()`, and `showInterfaceIn(lang)`.
  - The last one imports the translator only for English and asks for the dictionary at the same time.
  - It resolves to the language actually on screen, Thai when either fetch fails. Chromium keeps a failed import
    failed for the page's life, so the next try fetches the translator and the dictionary under a fresh query
    (`importFresh`, `src/lib/chunk-error.js`), as lazy screens already do.
  - `uiText(text)` reads a string a canvas draws through the dictionary on screen, and `useShownLang()` is the
    language on screen for a component that draws one. It changes when the translator switches, not when
    `index.html` sets `<html lang>` early: a boot slower than the start deadline draws before the dictionary is in.
  - The key is not `vmx-landing-lang`: that one set only the landing page's language, before the app had English.
- `index.html` sets `<html lang>` from `vmx-lang` before the first paint.
- `src/App.jsx`: when English cannot be fetched, this visit shows Thai and the menu says Thai. The saved choice stays,
  so the next press of English tries again.
- `src/atlas-main.jsx`: the Atlas waits at most 4 seconds for English before it draws.
- `src/lib/ui-translate.js` (new): a MutationObserver translator.
  - It writes only text-node values and four attributes (aria-label, title, placeholder, alt).
  - It keeps what React wrote and puts every original back on the switch to Thai. A write of the page's own always
    wins, even one that writes the English last shown there, and a part the page removes takes its English with it.
  - It skips any element with its own `lang`, any `translate="no"`, and editors.
  - Lookup goes in this order:
    1. exact match;
    2. numbers as `{0}` with a one/other plural;
    3. page titles split at " | ";
    4. `{a}` patterns, most specific first; two placeholders side by side take one capture;
    5. lists split at " — " and ", ", where the longest run of pieces that is an entry as it stands is one part
       (a set's tab title lists its kind, subject and topic with ", ", and a topic's label can hold ", " too);
    6. Thai phrases between English words, numbers and times.
  - A number is digits with thousands groups ("2,014"); the comma in "บทที่ 3, ส่วนที่ 2" belongs to the list.
  - A string longer than the longest pattern by 160 characters is a paragraph and is read only whole. Each answer is
    kept per string, so React's repeated writes are looked up once.
  - A line break the page shows stays where it was: each line is read on its own.
  - Dates use English weekdays and months, and the Common Era year, in `d/m/yyyy` and after a term or academic year
    too. A two-digit year counts only where a date ends, and a weekday only right before a date or a time, so
    "อาทิตย์ 3 จาก 16" stays "week 3 of 16" and "4 ธ.ค. 75/100" keeps its score.
  - A label that starts with an emoji is read without the emoji, unless an entry keeps it.
  - Text React wrote as several nodes is read whole. If that reading still holds Thai, the nodes are read one at a time instead.
  - `data-phrase` marks a label split into word spans; it is read as one. `data-ui` marks a control named inside a
    sentence (`HowToText`); the sentence is read whole and its words are written around the controls.
  - A switch made while the dictionary is on its way wins over the one before it.
- `src/data/ui-en.js` (new, 6,635 entries): the source catalogue translated in batches. It also holds the English
  that the data already carries: subject `name_en` (and the short names the VetWiki shelf shows), mascots, the atlas
  `th`/`en` pairs and the feature registry's `labelEn`. Hand corrections are on top, among them the alt text of
  every picture and the term names.
- `src/components/ThemePicker.jsx`, `HeaderBar.jsx`, `ProfileView.jsx`: a Language row comes first in the theme menu.
- `src/components/HomeQuickGrid.jsx`, `src/styles.css`: tile labels are one `data-phrase`. A Thai word stays unbroken
  only under `:lang(th)`, so English words wrap.
- `src/components/OfflineGame.jsx`: the prompts the game draws on its canvas follow the page's language.
- `src/lib/share-card/phase-card.js`, `src/views/PhaseWrappedView.jsx`: the recap card's words come from
  `phaseWords(stats, lang)`, read through the dictionary in English, with the units beside a numeral counted
  there ("1 question", "3 days", "min", "h") and the Common Era year behind the card. The picture's alt and the
  text shared with it are built from the same words (`phaseEnglish`). The card is drawn again when the language
  changes.
- These carry their own `lang="th"` (or `translate="no"` for a name) so they stay as written:
  - `src/lib/richtext.jsx` and `src/components/TermLinkedRichText.jsx`: every question stem, choice, key and
    explanation drawn through them.
  - `QuestionPaper`, `QComments`, `ReviewQueueView`, `QuestionManagerView`, `LibraryView` snippets, the `ClozeEditor`
    preview and `PrivateNotes`: questions, comments and notes shown outside the quiz.
  - `IdentityEditor`: the name being previewed.
  - `src/views/AtlasView.jsx`, `src/components/AtlasPartDirectory.jsx`: Thai organ names.
  - `AboutView`, `ReadingChecklistView`, `NotesView`, `BenchView`: names and lessons. In `BenchView` the mark moved
    from the page to the lesson's contents, so its Home button reads in English.
  - `src/views/FacultyView.jsx`, `src/components/InstructorPortrait.jsx`: a lecturer's names, position and areas,
    and the portrait's alt and initials.
  - `src/components/WikiShelves.jsx`: VetWiki's featured topic and the ranked topics beside it. The book's name is
    "อ่านหัวข้อ" and the title, joined with `aria-labelledby`, so the word is read in English and the title as written.
  - `src/views/HomeView.jsx`: release notes (the what's-new note and the newest one on Home's rail). The dictionary
    reads them only in part, and they are written per release.
- `tests/unit/ui-translate.test.mjs`: 14 tests of the lookup rules and of the dictionary. No Thai may appear outside
  quotation marks, every placeholder must be kept, and there is no middle dot. A paragraph of about 1,250 characters must
  be read in under 100 ms (an earlier draft of the translator took 9 seconds).
- `tests/unit/share-card-kit.test.mjs`: the recap card in English has English for every term, every closing line and
  all 86 subjects, counts one question and one day in the singular, and puts the Common Era year behind the card.
- `tests/unit/phase-wrapped-study-time.test.mjs`: the card's study-time hedge is tested on `phaseWords` itself
  (guessed, measured, and stats that never carried the flag) instead of a pin on its source text.
- `tests/e2e/ui-language.spec.js`:
  - Every route in `tests/e2e/lib/routes.js` is opened in English at 390 px on chromium-mobile. Each must say
    `lang="en"`, must not scroll sideways, and must show no Thai of its own, on a canvas included.
  - A question opened from a shared link reads exactly as the bank wrote it, its choices included.
  - The page's own writes win over the English, and English that cannot be fetched leaves the page and the menu Thai
    until a later press loads it.
  - The what's-new note opened in English shows no Thai of its own; the release notes in it keep their language.
  - On every project, Home is switched to English and back. It must reach no Thai, keep the choice, and return to
    the same Thai text.
  - `VMX_UI_LANG_REPORT=<file>` keeps one JSON line per route of the walk (listed in `scripts/lib/env-list.mjs`,
    `.env.example` and `docs/ENV.md`).
  - A route is read once the app has drawn it. Before React's first frame there is no `#main`, nothing loading
    and no Thai to find, and a probe read the review queue that early 2 times in 8.
  - VetWiki's index is read once its featured topic is drawn (it arrives with the topics).
- `tests/e2e/first-load-lazy.spec.js`: the first screen is read once no script or data request is open as well as
  after 800 ms with no change. On stillness alone, one load of a returning Year-5 student had Home's Daily Q row and
  the other did not (the row waits for the year's question banks): 1 of 6 runs alone on chromium. WebKit's entries
  for these two tests on the unsteady list record a first-screen mismatch with no cause found; whether this was it
  shows only in the next Smoke runs, so the entries stay.

## Not in this release

- The choice is kept per device, like the theme. Keeping it per account goes through the sync engine, after the
  Year-5 finals.
- The other share cards (score, daily question, timetable, VetWiki topic, student card, course document, the IG
  studio) are drawn only when a student asks for one, and stay Thai. `phaseWords` is the way to bring each across.
- Release notes are written in Thai, one per release, and stay so in English.
