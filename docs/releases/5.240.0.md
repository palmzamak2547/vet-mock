# 5.240.0: the case reader, slice 1: a full-screen sheet, the hand, a highlighter that snaps to words

Cases and continued questions, read the way a student reads them: the case opens to the full screen,
writing and highlighting are smooth and erasable, and moving the page and writing need no switch. The case
reader is built in slices; this release is slice 1 and nothing from the later ones.

## What a student sees

- **อ่านเต็มจอ** at the right end of the case pane's header opens the case as a sheet over the whole screen, in the
  page's own colours: the case typeset with 5.236.0's blocks (`proseBlocks`, `RichTable`), this question's findings
  under `ในข้อนี้` with a 3 px ocean rule, its figure, and the question docked at the bottom (two lines, a tap opens
  the rest). 18 px type at 36 em on a tablet or a desktop (34 em in iPad portrait), 17 px on a phone. In a timed
  paper the sheet shows the same clock. `กลับไปที่คำถาม` (the arrow alone on a phone) or Escape closes it.
- **The same place both ways.** The reading place is a character, not a pixel: opening puts the character at the top
  of the pane at the top of the sheet; closing scrolls the pane to the character at the top of the sheet and puts
  the page back where it was; a rotation keeps the character on top.
- **The pane** is one scroller: the 380 px box inside the sticky column is gone, and the title, อ่านเต็มจอ and the
  tools stay at the top of the pane while the case scrolls under them. A phone's pane carries no tools; marking
  happens in the sheet, where a bar replaces the bottom nav.
- **Tools** (`NavIcon` icons, 44 px, in DOM order): pen, highlighter, eraser, colour, undo, redo, clear. The armed tool
  is shown by fill and `aria-pressed`, never by size; colour is one button with its swatches in a flyout. No button
  moves when a tool or a colour is picked (e2e measures the clear button before and after). Colours avoid the
  verdicts' green, red and pink: ink, ocean, plum; lemon, sky, lilac (tokens `--clr-case-*`).
- **The hand** (`createHandPolicy`, `handwriting-pad.js`): a pen marks with the tool in hand and a finger moves the
  page with the browser's own scrolling, with no mode to switch. The first pen on the device is remembered
  (`vmx-ink-pen-seen`); from then a touch never marks. A device with no pen seen gets `เขียนด้วยนิ้ว` in the sheet: one
  finger marks, two fingers scroll, a stroke a second finger joins at any moment before it is committed is dropped and the page scrolls. A mouse marks with its left
  button. On iPad a non-passive touch listener stops the Pencil from scrolling and a resting palm from dragging the
  page while the pen is down.
- **The highlighter snaps to words.** A drag paints a live wash and, on lift, becomes the range from the word under
  its start to the word under its end (`caretPositionFromPoint` / `caretRangeFromPoint`, then
  `Intl.Segmenter('th', { granularity: 'word' })`). A drag that leaves the text column marks nothing; a tap is not a
  mark. A mouse selection with the highlighter in hand is a highlight; a finger's long press keeps the system's copy
  and look-up and offers `ไฮไลต์` beside them. A tap on a highlight opens a small bar (its colour, ลบ) instead of
  deleting it.
- **Eraser, undo, redo, clear.** The eraser removes the highlights its path crosses (sampled every 4 px, so a fast
  swipe does not skip) and the pen strokes it touches. One history per case for every kind of mark, 100 steps,
  kept for the page's life. Clear asks nothing; `ล้างแล้ว 12 รอย` with `ย้อนกลับ` stays 8 seconds, and undo brings them
  back too.

## Marks belong to the account that made them

Until now marks sat under `vmx-pass-<djb2 of the passage>-hl` / `-dr`, the same keys for every account on a device,
so the next student on a shared iPad saw the last one's marks after sign-out. Now:

- `vmx-case-<ownerId|guest>-<caseKey>` holds `{ v: 1, marks }`. `caseKey` is a hash of the subject, the title and the
  first 200 characters of the passage, so a case whose passage grows from stage to stage keeps one key.
- **Adoption, once.** The first time a case is shown on a device after this release, the account signed in then (or
  the visitor) is written as the owner of the old marks in `vmx-case-adopt`. That owner alone gets a copy of a
  passage's old marks the first time that passage opens; the copy is written at once, so a later clear is never undone
  by adopting again. Another account, or a visitor after sign-out, sees none of them.
- **Nothing is deleted.** The old `vmx-pass-*` keys are never written or removed: they are the backup slice 3 reads
  when it converts the pen strokes. An empty case is saved as an empty record, never removed. A record that cannot be
  read is never written over.
- Highlights convert exactly (same offsets), with a quote (the text and 16 characters each side) computed at
  conversion; yellow stays lemon, green becomes sky, pink becomes lilac. When a passage's text at a range no longer
  equals its quote, the range is found again by the quote and its context; when it cannot be found the mark is kept,
  unseen, never dropped.
- Local storage only. Nothing touches the sync engine (`sync_user_data_v2`), `useExamSession.js` or scoring.

## After two adversarial reviews

- **Another question under the sheet closes it**, whatever the reason: a timed paper that moves on when time runs
  out (its notice `หมดเวลาข้อ N` is then in view), a passage shared by several questions, another case. A sheet still
  loading for a question the card has left does not open.
- **Back closes the sheet first** (iPad's edge swipe, Android's back, the browser's), in practice and in a paper,
  without the leave-the-set dialog: the sheet stands on a history entry of its own, and its own buttons go back
  through it.
- **A mark is never moved onto other words.** A range past the end of the text in view belongs to a longer stage of
  the case and is hidden there, not placed; after an edit a range is placed again only where at least 8 characters
  of the words around it still match. Clear and the eraser act only on what the view shows.
- **Undo, redo and the clear's way back in any order**: any undo, redo or new mark ends the way back, and the way
  back puts back only marks that are missing (`restoreOp`).
- **The reading place is measured once the hand's code has loaded**, never before, so the sheet opens at the pane's
  character however slow the page is; the sheet reads the place again only after the reader has moved it, once the
  scroll stops.
- Smaller: a tap is under 250 ms and 8 px; a tap on a highlight that is already wholly marked adds nothing; the mark
  under a point is judged by the character's own box; the word snap segments only 64 characters around each end; a
  mark that cannot be saved stays for the visit and the pane says so; one damaged old key no longer blocks a case;
  the way back never writes over a record it cannot read; the sheet's clear removes highlights only;
  `touch-action: manipulation` (WebKit drops a declaration naming `pinch-zoom`).
- **Bundle**: to stay under the exam budget, what is needed only once a case is touched moved behind the case's lazy
  import (`ink/case-hand.js`): the adoption and conversion of old marks (adoption now happens a moment after the case draws, once
  that code has loaded), the way back to another case, the pane's scroller and top-bar measurement, and the mouse
  and finger selection handlers.

## Old pen strokes

They are pixels from the top left of the old 380 px window. In this slice they keep drawing where they do today in
the pane, on a canvas now inside the scroller (they scroll with the text), copied unchanged into the owner's record
(`kind: 'px'`, `legacy: 1`), and are not shown on the sheet. The pen in the sheet is present and disabled with its
reason; it draws from slice 3, which anchors ink to the words and converts these strokes from the backup.

## Measured

- **Frame time** (Chromium, built preview, 1280 x 800, a highlight dragged across the longest passage in
  the bank, 5,038 characters, 150 pointer moves): the work per frame (`performance.measure('vmx-case-paint')`: the
  word snap, the range and the wash) p50 0.5 ms, p95 0.9 to 1.4 ms over four runs, max 2.6 ms; frame intervals p50 and p95 16.7 ms
  (60 Hz headless, no frame dropped). React commits during the drag: 0.
- **Bundle**: the exam route is 163,330 B gzip against its 163,500 B budget (after merging main, which carries 5.237.0 and 5.239.0);
  no budget raised. What a hand needs to mark (`src/lib/ink/case-hand.js`) and the tools load with the case, after the
  pane draws (the row keeps its height meanwhile); the sheet (`CaseSheet.jsx`, `styles-case.css`) loads on its first tap.

## Specs changed, and why

- `question-tables.spec.js` "a highlight stays on its words around a table": the highlighter is `ปากกาไฮไลต์` (the PDF
  reader's word) and a phone marks in the sheet; the assertions are the same.
- `exam-clock.spec.js`: the old test pinned the confirm dialog of clear and the removal of the old keys, both of which
  the design reverses (no dialog; nothing deleted). It now pins the same risk the other way round: a clear, a time-out
  to the next passage, then the way back, which must restore the first passage's marks under the first case and leave
  the second's and every old key as they were.
- `smart-passage-scope.test.mjs` became `case-pane-scope.test.mjs`; `passage-opens-with-the-tap.test.mjs` drives
  CasePane; the card harnesses stub CasePane where they stubbed SmartPassage.
