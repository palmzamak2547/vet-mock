## What changed

- `src/data/evidence/one-health.generated.js`: the One Health answer-evidence index, rebuilt from the evidence
  registry. Each new row is the slide page the printed answer key of the forecast set marks for that item, moved onto
  the library copy of the same deck and snapped to that page's own text lines. The livestock waste deck's text layer
  breaks its Thai, so its lines were chosen on the library copy by hand.
- Every new row was checked by eye on a picture of the page with its highlight drawn. 86 rows were proposed and 52
  passed the first look; the other 34 were picked again where a better line existed, and 14 of them passed the
  second look. A highlight that stopped mid-sentence was extended over the wrapped lines under it and checked again
  (11 rows). 66 rows are published; 20 are left out (19 where the page does not state the answer by itself, 1 on the
  wrong page).
- `src/data/source-docs.js`: the library now shelves the 11 Nov 2569 livestock waste deck under the title printed on
  its first page instead of its file name, so its entry also matches that title, anchored whole so no free-text
  citation reaches it that way. The 2024 deck's pattern does not match the new title. Only the title column of that
  library row changed.
- Page images for the new pages are in the evidence image folder; the index names no image path.
