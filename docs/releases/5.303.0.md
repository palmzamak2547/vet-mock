## What changed

- `src/data/evidence/one-health.generated.js`: the One Health answer-evidence index, rebuilt from the evidence
  registry: 110 questions on 85 pages of 9 decks, up from 24 questions on 18 pages of 7. Each new row is the slide
  page the printed answer key of the forecast set marks for that item, moved onto the library copy of the same deck
  and snapped to that page's own text lines. The livestock waste deck's text layer breaks its Thai, so its lines were
  chosen on the library copy by hand.
- Every new row was checked by eye on a picture of the page with its highlight drawn. 86 rows were proposed and 52
  passed the first look as lines that state the answer; the other 34 were picked again where a better line existed,
  and 14 of them passed the second look. A highlight that stopped mid-sentence was extended over the wrapped lines
  under it and checked again (11 rows).
- The remaining 20 rest on what was said in class: the page is the one that taught the point, but its printed lines
  do not state the whole answer. Each was laid beside the answer key's picture, and 19 show the same page with the
  same lines marked. The 20th key page carries the formula only as a handwritten note, which the library copy lacks,
  so that item shows the risk analysis deck's printed definition of risk characterization (probability of occurrence
  and severity) instead. The slide now sits under every item of the forecast set.
- `src/data/source-docs.js`: the library now shelves the 11 Nov 2569 livestock waste deck under the title printed on
  its first page instead of its file name, so its entry also matches that title, anchored whole so no free-text
  citation reaches it that way. The 2024 deck's pattern does not match the new title. Only the title column of that
  library row changed.
- Page images for the new pages are in the evidence image folder; the index names no image path. All 85 cited pages
  answer with an image.
