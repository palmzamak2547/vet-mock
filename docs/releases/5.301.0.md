## What changed

- `src/data/predict-sets.js` (new): the forecast as data. Each part names its topic, lecturer, session, format,
  count, evidence and confidence, the ids it serves in order, how many are senior-paper items (`past`) and how many
  were written from this year's session (`fresh`), and its extras. `predictSetFor(subject, year, phase, now)`
  returns the set until `ends`.
- `src/components/PredictSet.jsx` and `src/styles-predict.css` (new, one lazy chunk): the poster (an SVG ring
  chart, role img with a Thai label), the whole-paper button, and one card per part. Every colour is a theme token,
  so the five palettes and both themes keep their harmony; the poster inverts ink and paper. The ring draw-in uses
  the `--vm-dur-page` and `--vm-ease-page` tokens and is off under reduced motion. Markerless lists carry
  `role="list"`; each part's buttons carry the part title as hidden text, so seven buttons do not share one name.
- `src/views/TopicSelectView.jsx`: the card under the wrap-up entry (the one-page read stays first in exam week),
  behind `OptionalFeature` with a placeholder in the card's own three blocks, measured at 320, 375, 540 and 720 to
  1,280 px and picked by width, and a retry card when the chunk fails or the device is offline.
- `src/App.jsx`: `startPredictSet` starts the whole paper in exam mode or one part in quick mode; a written part
  runs untimed, like a lecturer's written set. In `startExam`, a `keepOrder` list is served in its own order and
  only from its own subject's bank questions (a same-numbered id of another subject, or a personal question
  restored under the same number, stays out), waits for its subject's year rather than every year's banks, skips
  the shuffle and the examOrigin id re-sort, and states `panicPool: false` and `onlyPastPaper: false`, so a Panic
  or past-paper choice still armed cannot re-rank or narrow it.
- `src/components/PredictSet.jsx`: focus moving into a part card on the phone rail scrolls the rail to that card,
  so the focused button is never left off screen.
- `src/hooks/useExamSession.js`: `startNewSession` takes `budget`, the paper's length in seconds, for the one
  clock. Seventy objective items and two written ones add up to 89:56 at 71 seconds a question and 91:12 at 72, so
  the whole paper names its 5,400 seconds. Without `budget` every caller adds up its allowance as before.
- `src/data/questions-y5-one-health-2026-c.js`: 40 items (219600 to 219605, 219610 to 219619, 219620 to 219630,
  219640 to 219645, 219650 to 219653, 219660 to 219662), each checked against this year's recording and slides;
  five new `source-doc` origins in `src/data/exam-origins.js`. `src/data/questions-y5-one-health-2026-b.js`:
  105962's correct option and explanation.
- `docs/css-token-baseline.json`: `--tone`, `--tone-soft`, `--tone-text`, `--ring-x`, `--ring-y` are the card's
  own custom properties, set per part and per width, each used with a theme-token fallback.
- `AGENTS.md`: the stylesheet in the Styles row.
