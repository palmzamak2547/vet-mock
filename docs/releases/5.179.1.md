# 5.179.1: the source line keeps the key until the verdict, and the verdict loses its Mochi stamp

Palm, 1 Nov 2026: "ตรงที่มาตอนทำโจทย์ บางอันอ่านแล้วสามารถรู้เฉลยได้ แก้ที", then, with a screenshot of the
wrong-answer card: "อันนี้เอาmochiออกไปที ดูแปลกตา".

## The leak, measured on the bank of 1 Nov (7,814 questions, main at cf5964b9)

Every in-session surface (practice, exam mode, Panic Mode, the quick sets, shared sets) draws one question card,
`Question.jsx`, and its source chip `QSourceChip.jsx`. The chip's closed line printed `examOrigin`, else the source
pointer through `humanSource`, and "ดูเต็ม" opened the full pointer, the checking note (`verified`), the VetWiki
title, the flag note and the tags, all before an answer. The VCA app already shows provenance only after the reveal.

`scripts/lib/answer-leak.mjs` judges a text against the key, ignoring what the card already shows (stem, passage,
course name), stopwords, numbers and the words every source line carries:

- key-words: a word of the correct option that no other option holds (English, 4+ letters or an acronym; or two
  Thai words; or a three-word Thai phrase);
- names-answer: the disease, drug or organism the key names, in either language, through the glossary's names;
- states-fact: a fill-in blank or a short answer's keywords, or for true/false two words of the explanation's
  verdict the statement does not use.

## The fix

- Until the key is on screen the line names only the kind of source (`src/lib/source-line.js`), in Panic Mode's
  order through the existing predicates: ข้อสอบเก่า with the filed paper and cohort (3,217), อิงแนวข้อสอบ (1,324),
  เขียนจากเนื้อหาที่เรียน (1,544), จากชีทรุ่นพี่ (271), ข้อสอบจำลอง (49), and มีแหล่งอ้างอิง for legacy rows with no recorded
  kind (1,409). "ดูเต็ม" stays shut and says "ดูเต็มหลังเฉลย"; the button is `aria-disabled`, keeps its row, and the
  full line and panel come with the verdict (a choice question's answer, a full matching set, a written answer's
  เฉลย). A timed paper, or practice with instant answers off, shows them in Review.
- A filed cohort shows only where `exam-origin-parse.js`, the one reader of who sat a paper, finds the same one.
  When this was found, 30 legacy origins (448 questions) were filed with a file label's number ("COM I Final 86",
  "Swine Herd Mid TJ86"), which would have printed "Vet 86" on papers Vet 86 never sat; the ledger gave the list to
  the bank lane, and #143 filed 29 of them as unknown. One remains: "Avian Medicine final recall (Vet 85
  compilation)" is filed as Vet 85 with its evidence, but the parser reads "(Vet 85 compilation)" as an author, so its
  10 questions print the paper and no cohort.
- The "อิงแนวเดิม" chip follows the same kind on the card and in Review: 370 rows written from a recording or a
  summary ("Kim TJ86") had it above a line that now says เขียนจากเนื้อหาที่เรียน.
- The chip is keyed per question, so an open panel no longer carries over to the next question.
- The neutral kinds do not hint at any key: no kind phrase, cohort, paper or the wait hint appears in a key whose
  stem lacks it.

## The topic chip

The meta row above the stem printed the topic label before an answer, and on 135 questions (129 before main's #140
moved six avian rows) the label alone named the key: "Autoimmune Skin Diseases" above "Sebaceous Adenitis
เป็นโรคที่" (com4 1068), "Mitral Valve Disease" above a murmur case whose key is degenerative mitral valve disease
(com1 90001), "ยาถ่ายพยาธิ / ยาต้านโปรโตซัว" above a question whose key is the only option that names โปรโตซัว
(vet-pharm-2 53036). Those labels now wait for the key behind "หัวข้อแสดงหลังเฉลย". Label and stand-in share one grid
cell (the hidden one is `visibility: hidden` and `aria-hidden`), so the part is the same size before and after and the
row never reflows; every other topic prints as before.

## The data-conflict flag

"ข้อมูลขัดแย้ง" shows under the stem of every flagged question, timed papers included, and one tap opened its note and
sources before an answer. On 26 of the 94 flagged questions they named the key: com5 568 "ควรเป็น InPouch TF-Feline
หรือ PCR", com1 90011 "Answer set to inspiratory", and vca 692050's source names actinomycosis. The chip still warns
before the answer; what it opens waits for the key, like the source panel (`revealed={keyShown}`).

## Review and the topic screen

- Review printed the source pointer but never `examOrigin`: of the 3,824 questions that carry an origin, 894 have no
  pointer and showed no source at all after a timed paper. It now prints the origin, then the pointer in brackets.
- The topic screen counted past papers as "อิงแนวเดิม X/Y", the card chip's word for a wider set (marked points and
  mocks too). It now reads "ข้อสอบเก่า X/Y", the word the source line uses for the same predicate. The chip's rule is
  left alone until after 4 Dec.

## The guard

- `npm run regen:source-line` (in `regen:all`, after `regen:clip-labels`, whose output it imports) writes
  `src/data/source-line.generated.js`: the held topics and the unconfirmed cohorts. Both are decided in Node with
  `scripts/lib/answer-leak.mjs` and the parser, so the browser carries two short lists instead of a Thai word
  segmenter it lacks on iOS 14 or the parser.
- `npm run lint:source-line` (in `lint:data`) fails when that file drifts from the bank, the curriculum or the
  filing, and when any question's pre-answer source line, flag or topic gives its key away; `--full` prints how many
  full lines (23) and flag notes (26) the wait holds back.
- `tests/unit/source-line-no-spoiler.test.mjs` renders the real chip, the real flag and the real card's meta row for
  every question before the key. Against 5.179.0's chip it fails with the 23 rows above; its meta-row test failed on
  this branch with the 129 and its flag test with the 26 before each fix.
- `tests/e2e/source-line-spoiler.spec.js` answers four of the leaking rows in a shared set and reads the chip and the
  meta row through the page before and after.
- The checker reads words, not meaning: a deck called "Metencephalon" above a key that is the pons gets past it.

## Mochi off the verdict

The verdict panel's dashed Mochi stamp ("ถูกแล้ว" / "ลองอีกที") is gone in both states, with its CSS, the `feedback`
slot rule in `mochiEnabled` and `mochiPresenceFor`, and the pose and Motion Kit state `feedbackReaction` chose for
it. The run chip and the haptic stay; MochiLive keeps its one user, the result card.

Measured at 375 px on Chromium and WebKit, wrong and right alike: the headline spans the header (268 of 268 px), 15 px
below the card's top edge, 10 px of padding and the 1 px rule below it, then 12 px to the เฉลย or เหตุผล box. That is
the header's own padding with nothing taller beside it, so no CSS changed. The header keeps its height from the
moment the card appears (31.25 px) and Chromium records no layout shift without input.
