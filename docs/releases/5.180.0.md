# 5.180.0: Avian and Equine Repro topics follow the 2569 syllabi

The syllabus lane read the 2569-1 syllabi (docs/SYLLABUS-2569-1-Y5.md on its branch) and found two topics scoped
`both` that the 2569 timetables teach on one side of the midterm, plus the POA lecturers the 3107522 syllabus names.
Each row moves by its content, so no final content leaves the final.

- **Avian, `avian-intro`:** the intro is taught on 25 Aug, before the midterm, and the final covers 6 Oct to 17 Nov,
  yet 84 rows sat under it. A classifier read each row against the 2569 decks: 56 go to midterm disease topics (AI 8,
  coccidiosis 7, omphalitis-ascites-staph 6, Marek 6, AE 5, IBD 5, salmonella 4, adeno 4, leuco 3, NE 2, and one
  each to ND, fowl cholera, mycoplasma, MPV, CIA and reo), 22 to final topics (mycosis 9, ORT 3, Campylobacter 2,
  histomoniasis 2, and one each to NDS, duck plague, DVH, duck flavivirus, listeriosis and serology), and 6 stay.
  `avian-intro` is now `midterm`. The pastpaper rows carried the topic id as a tag too; it moved with them.
- **Vet 85's avian summary** sat under `avian-intro` because the final topics did not exist when it was filed: its 24
  disease sections now sit under the topics that teach them, and its "questions the seniors could not answer" section
  is split bullet by bullet to the topics they ask about, each keeping its warning.
- **Equine Repro:** stallion reproductive infections are taught on 22 Oct, after the midterm, and the mare's
  infectious diseases before it, with no topic of their own. `eqrepro-mare-infect` (midterm) takes the 5 mare rows
  (EHV-1 and the transport-medium item) and the 27 Aug lecturer set's mare deck; `eqrepro-stallion-infect` keeps 16
  rows and becomes `final`. The 10 rows that carried a per-question `examScope` lose it, since the topic now decides
  their paper as the 2569 teaching does.
- **Serum pooling:** 105871 (intro: 20 birds a house, 5 sera pooled) and 109012 (serology: 1 bird is 1 sample, about
  30 a house, no pooling) key opposite answers, each faithful to its own lecture; the 2569 serology deck is not out yet.
  Both stay, each with a documented-conflict note the question view shows.
- **POA:** ten lecturers filled from the 3107522 syllabus (romanised as the instructor directory spells them).
- **Release records:** production proof for 5.179.0.
