# 5.222.1: one prion line in the Zoonoses notes

The Zoonoses final is on 26 Nov 2026 (13:00-16:00). A sweep of the repository's own documents listed the prion
lines in the Zoonoses notes as still open (`docs/releases/5.216.0.md`, "Still teaching").

## What changed

- `src/data/notes-85-zoonoses.js`, topic `zoo-prion`, section "โปรตีน 2 form และการวินิจฉัย", second bullet:
  - old: "ปกติทั้งสอง form เจอได้ใน CNS แต่ **PrPSc เจอน้อยมากและอยู่ในรูป inactive**"
  - new: "ปกติทั้งสอง form เจอได้ใน CNS แต่ **PrPSc เจอน้อยมาก**"
- The search index for the Zoonoses wiki was regenerated from it.

## Evidence

- This year's deck, "Prion Diseases" (library, academic year 2026), page 5: "normally present in cells but PrPSC
  proteins are found in very small amount (mainly in the central nervous system)". The 2024 edition, page 5, prints
  the same sentence. Neither says PrPSc is inactive.
- Standard references (Prusiner 1998, PNAS 95:13363; CFSPH prion fact sheets; Merck Veterinary Manual) describe
  PrPSc as the misfolded, protease-resistant, disease-associated form. An "inactive" PrPSc is supported by no source,
  so the words were removed. The rest of the line follows the deck.

## Not changed, and why

- `src/data/notes-y5-zoonoses.js` line 3185 ("พบตามปกติใน cells แต่ PrP^SC proteins พบในปริมาณน้อยมาก (mainly in
  the central nervous system)") translates deck page 5 word for word, and the remaining 85 line says the same. The
  standard references hold that PrPSc is not present in a healthy animal. This is a difference between the deck and
  the references, not a copying error. It is not recorded in `src/lib/vetwiki/corrections.js` yet; it is left for
  the owner to decide. Question 210594 already carries a flag that sets out both sides.
- No note calls PrPSc the normal protein: every note and wrap-up names PrPC as the normal cellular form.
- The other prion sentences (wrap-up `zoonoses-final.js`, questions 8041, 8240, 210594, 106039 (serology), video
  summaries and cards, verification overlay) were read and agree with the deck and the references.
