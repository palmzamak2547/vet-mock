- **ข้อสอบเก่าปีการศึกษา 2553 เรื่อง bacterial zoonoses 5 ข้อ** ถูกผิด 4 ข้อ และจับคู่โรคกับเชื้อก่อโรค 1 ข้อ 5 คู่
  ในหัวข้อ Bacterial zoonoses โจทย์ตรงตามที่บันทึกไว้หลังสอบ ข้อที่บันทึกไว้ว่าผิดบอกส่วนที่ผิดในคำอธิบาย
- **mycoses ปรนัยเพิ่ม 12 ข้อ** ในหัวข้อ Mycozoonoses 5 ข้อจากรายการข้อสอบเก่าที่บันทึกไว้ และ 7 ข้อเรื่อง
  histoplasmosis กับ blastomycosis จากจุดที่ทำเครื่องหมายไว้ในแผนผังสรุป มีป้ายอิงแนวข้อสอบ
- ทุกข้อบอกหน้าสไลด์ปีนี้ที่ตอบได้ และหน้าของชุดเตรียมสอบที่บันทึกข้อนั้นไว้

## What changed

- `src/data/questions-y5-zoonoses-final-pack.js` (new): 17 items, ids 219900 to 219916.
  - Zoonoses paper of academic year 2553 as it was written down after the paper (Bacterial Zoonosis, pages 11 and 12
    of the class pack): true/false 2, 8, 19 and 21, and the five matching pairs as one matching item. Stems keep the
    recorded wording; in true/false 8 the first word could not be read and was filled from the sentence, as the item's
    `verified` note says. The record lists 50 items; the other 41 are already bank items (28), cannot be read, carry no
    key or are not the item itself (5), are missing from the photographed pages (3), or rest on a point this year's
    decks do not settle or that conflicts with a bank item (5).
  - Mycoses from page 3 of the pack: 5 items from the recalled list of choice items (`student-compilation`) and 7 from
    the starred or highlighted nodes of the mind map (`exam-aligned`, tagged อิงแนวข้อสอบ). Of the 42 points on the
    page, 16 became these 12 items, 21 are already bank items and 5 are left out: one stem names its own key, two
    recorded answers disagree with the reading material, one asks for a treatment the text does not give, and one is a
    study hint rather than an item.
- `src/data/exam-origins.js`: the 2553 origin is filed as a paper with cohort and paper unknown. The record names the
  academic year, not the cohort that sat it or whether it was the midterm or the final.
- `src/data/source-docs.js`: the class pack (24 pages) is registered, so its page citations are structured.
- `vite.config.js`: the new bank file gets its own chunk.
- `npm run regen:all`: bank registry, question counts, share bank, recurrence and glossary links.

## How it was tested

- Every key was answered again from the deck text without seeing the key: 17 of 17 agree.
- Ingest checks pass (format, option count and length, wording, duplicates against the bank).
- 60 page citations, each matched to a registered document; none left as free text.
- Spot check against the record: every past-paper and recalled-list stem was compared with the line it came from;
  three true/false stems that had been reworded were put back to the recorded wording.
- Unit tests and the data lints.
