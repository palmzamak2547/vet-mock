# 5.168.0: VCA round 1/2570 and the 150-question paper

The documents Palm sent on 28 Oct 2569 (the CUVET86 meeting summaries of 17 Mar, 19 Oct and
28 Oct 2569) settle three facts that vca.vetmock.com and VetMock's VCA subject had wrong or
lacked. This release carries only those facts; the round 1/2569 Part 3 questions and the
external bank follow in their own releases.

- **Round 1/2570 sits on 5 and 6 June 2570.** The faculty's academic office confirmed the dates
  on 28 Oct 2569: Part 2 on the first afternoon, Part 3 the next morning, Part 1 not examined.
  The evaluation centre has not published 2570 yet, so the round is filed under a source of its
  own (`SOURCES.faculty`, no link: a meeting, not a public page), with the half day in place of
  hours and no results day. The exam page still counts down to round 2/2569 (23-24 Jan 2570,
  the centre's), and names 1/2570 under it as the next round; the plan offers every announced
  round as a chip; the calendar file and the Google Calendar link put an unannounced sitting in
  as an all-day event and point at the centre's announcement page.
- **150 questions a part, not 200.** Both faculty meetings (17 Mar and 28 Oct 2569) give 150
  five-choice questions a part; the site's 200 came from the cohorts' almanac. The full mock is
  now 150 questions in the same three hours, which moves the pace from 54 to 72 seconds a
  question (the default in the set builder, and the short paper of 50 questions now runs 60
  minutes). The almanac legend no longer claims a yearly total out of 400.
- **The pass mark and the mix.** A part passes at 55%, 83 of 150; about 50 to 60 questions ask
  for recall and 90 to 100 apply it. Both are on the exam page's band and in the VCA subject's
  notes in VetMock.

What the documents do not settle, kept out of the data: the 2570 hours and length of each part,
the results day, the exact dates of round 2/2570 ("ม.ค. 71") and of the faculty's mock exam
(week 3 or 4 of May 2570), and the second criteria file the faculty mentioned.
