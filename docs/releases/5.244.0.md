# 5.244.0: question and explanation read-aloud starts at once on every question, and never goes silent

## What a student notices

- A question or an explanation with no stored clip reads at once in the device's own voice, a sentence at a time,
  instead of waiting for a whole text to come back from the cloud voice. A long explanation reads to its end.
- A stored clip that has not arrived after 4 s hands the whole question to the device voice (one voice per question).
  A device with no Thai voice uses the cloud voice on the same player; with neither, the bar says why and what to do
  (add a Thai voice in the device's settings, or connect; signed out, sign in with a Chula email for the cloud voice),
  and play tries again from the start, also when the note came before anything was read.
- The device voice has the clip controls: pause (the bar then says it is paused), resume, previous and next sentence,
  speed, and the sentence being read is marked, moving on with each sentence.
- English questions, and Latin and drug names in Thai ones, reach the live voices with every letter.
- Moving to another question stops the old voice at once.

## What changed

- `src/lib/read-aloud.js`: device mode is a plan of sentence-sized pieces (`livePlan` in `src/lib/tts.js`, at most
  160 characters, cut by `clipSegments` in `src/lib/tts-text.js`). A piece is said by the device voice when the device
  has a good one for the language, else fetched from the cloud voice and played on the read-aloud's own element (two
  pieces ahead, never all at once), else the other of the two; both failing leaves a note (`dead`). Limits:
  `FIRST_WAIT_MS` 4 s for the first stored clip, `CLOUD_WAIT_MS` 4 s for a cloud piece, `NO_START_MS` 2.5 s for a
  device voice that takes the text and never speaks. The device's voices are waited for up to 1.2 s on a session's first
  tap (Chrome lists them late); before, 30 ms, which sent a first tap to the cloud even on a device with a good voice.
  The live code and its Thai spelling table load and warm while the clips' manifest loads.
- The cloud ladder no longer asks iApp first on every first tap of a session (five requests answered with an error
  before the voice that answers).
- `src/components/ReadAloudBar.jsx`: the same controls for clips and the device voice.
- Clip generations: `PAXA_TTS` (`p1`) in `tts-text.js`, voiced a sentence at a time, served for a subject only when its
  manifest says `complete` (so a subject never mixes voices). `scripts/tts-clips.mjs --gen p1 --check` voices,
  transcribes back the clips with a digit, a unit or an English or Latin term and one in ten of the rest, compares
  numbers, units and terms (`scripts/lib/tts-check.mjs`), voices a failing clip once more and uploads nothing that fails
  twice. The explanation of every question type is voiced (the card offers it on every type).
- The script's spending rules (`scripts/lib/tts-budget.mjs`): a paid run (e1, p1, `--align`) refuses to start without
  `--keep-remaining` or `--max-credits`; a transcription balance that cannot be read stops the run and never pays for a
  second voicing; a clip that failed its check twice is voiced again only with `--retry-failed`; a served subject stays
  served on a later run unless `--unpublish`. A missing p1 manifest no longer reads as a finished subject.
- `clipSegments` cuts a long Thai run with no space at an `Intl.Segmenter` word boundary, never before a vowel or tone
  mark. No existing p1 clip text changes (0 of 72,188 pieces over the bank); 46 live pieces in 23 texts change.
- A piece's word offset (`from`, where the highlight starts) comes from `segmentStarts`; before, a lost backslash
  (`/S+/`) left every piece marked from the first word, and another (`/s+/`) deleted every "s" from what the live
  voices were given.
- `PAXA_API_KEY` in the environment contract (scripts only).

## How it was checked

- Unit: `tests/unit/read-aloud-live-text.test.mjs` (the text a live voice receives, English and Thai with Latin names;
  the word each piece is marked from, live and p1; the Thai cut); `tests/unit/tts-budget.test.mjs` (an unreadable
  balance buys nothing, floors, retries, the served flag); `tests/unit/read-aloud-live.test.mjs` (the sign-in note,
  play after a note that came before any piece, no Thai voice, no cloud voice, a device that never speaks, the 4 s
  limit on a late clip, a 2,800-character explanation to its end, switching questions, pause, resume, speed on the
  device voice); `tests/unit/read-aloud-card-text.test.mjs` (the voice's text against the card's rendered text for
  questions 209588 and 209599 and a table stem); the existing read-aloud suites, two cases moved to a later piece
  because a failed first clip now hands over to the device voice.
- e2e: `tests/e2e/read-aloud.spec.js`, the device voice with a stubbed speech engine: play, pause, resume, next point,
  speed, close.
- Tap to first sound measured in real browsers before and after (see the pull request).

## Not verified

- Nobody listened by ear. No real iPhone or Android phone was used; Playwright's WebKit on Windows has no speech engine.
- Whether phones show lock-screen keys while the device voice reads (speech is not a media element), and how soon an
  iPhone speaks after the tap when no clip exists (speech is not primed inside the tap; priming it would change the
  question card, which an exam session also shows, so it is left).
