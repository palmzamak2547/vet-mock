# 5.217.0: wrap-up reading, slice 1

The part every redesign direction keeps, built on today's page with no new data: the wrap-ups were "walls of
text", and these five changes help a student find, check and place what they read. The slide pictures under a
cited sentence are a later slice.

## What changed

1. **A cited clip minute opens the clip at that minute.** `src/lib/wrap-cite.js` splits a bullet's citation into
   the clips it names (each with its minutes) and text, through the existing `recordingMoments` and `momentHref`
   in `source-label.js`. Each minute is a link to `/app/videos?v=<id>&at=<second>` in a new tab, 44 px to the
   thumb and drawn as a small pill. A clip of the page's own course is named by its date ("คลิป 7 ต.ค."). A line
   with no clip, or an unknown recording, reads as before.
2. **"เคยออก N รุ่น".** `src/lib/wrap-cohorts.js` reads the ids an item's "มีในข้อสอบเก่า" lines cite against
   `exam-recurrence.generated.js` (cohorts that sat each key, plus copies of the same item). 209058 resolves to
   Vet 78, 81 and 85; the Swine final's PE item to five cohorts. History only; an id the file does not attribute
   adds nothing, and the wording in the wrap-up ("(Vet 80)") is never read. The file loads lazily, so the text
   never waits for it. Items with a tag: Swine final 16 of 28, Equine final 7 of 65, One Health final 9 of 12,
   Zoonoses final 7 of 20.
3. **Signal icons.** NavIcon gains `said`, `sheet` and `caution`, one per kind of line (stressed, past paper,
   pitfall), each in one AA text token and always beside its label; the page's key shows them once.
4. **Bold terms.** `keyTerm` also splits "Term: detail" at the first ": " (a time has no space after its colon),
   for rows and chips; the term is at most 60 characters with its brackets closed, and a numbered list is left
   as written.
5. **Progress strip.** The sticky index pins "5 จาก 28" and marks the item being read, from an
   IntersectionObserver over a reading band under the bar (no scroll listener). The strip follows the marked chip;
   that and an index jump land at once under reduced motion.

Both reading modes keep working; in keywords-only the tag, bold terms and strip show and the bullets stay hidden.
