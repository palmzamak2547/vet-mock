# 5.217.0: the wrap-up redesign (reading, slide frame, revision deck)

Ships once, when the whole redesign is done (Palm's decision). Slice 1 is the reading features every
direction keeps; slice 2 is direction B (the revision deck) as the core, with direction A's real slide under the
line that cites it. Direction C (a differential board) is later.

## Slice 1: reading

The part every redesign direction keeps, built on today's page with no new data: the wrap-ups were "walls of
text", and these five changes help a student find, check and place what they read.

## What changed

1. **A cited clip minute opens the clip at that minute.** `src/lib/wrap-cite.js` splits a bullet's citation into
   the clips it names (each with its minutes) and text, through the existing `recordingMoments` and `momentHref`
   in `source-label.js`. Each minute is a link to `/app/videos?v=<id>&at=<second>` in a new tab, 44 px to the
   thumb and drawn as a small pill. A clip of the page's own course is named by its date ("คลิป 7 ต.ค."). A line
   with no clip, or an unknown recording, reads as before.
2. **"เคยออก N รุ่น".** `src/lib/wrap-cohorts.js` reads the ids an item's "มีในข้อสอบเก่า" lines cite against
   `exam-recurrence.generated.js` (cohorts that sat each key, plus copies of the same item). 209058 resolves to
   Vet 78, 81 and 85; the Swine final's PE item to five cohorts. History only; an id the file does not attribute
   adds nothing, and the wording in the wrap-up ("(Vet 80)") is never read. The file loads lazily, so the text
   never waits for it. Items with a tag: Swine final 16 of 28, Equine final 7 of 65, One Health final 9 of 12,
   Zoonoses final 7 of 20.
3. **Signal icons.** NavIcon gains `said`, `sheet` and `caution`, one per kind of line (stressed, past paper,
   pitfall), each in one AA text token and always beside its label; the page's key shows them once.
4. **Bold terms.** `keyTerm` also splits "Term: detail" at the first ": " (a time has no space after its colon),
   for rows and chips; the term is at most 60 characters with its brackets closed, and a numbered list is left
   as written.
5. **Progress strip.** The sticky index pins "5 จาก 28" and marks the item being read, from an
   IntersectionObserver over a reading band under the bar (no scroll listener). The strip follows the marked chip;
   that and an index jump land at once under reduced motion.

Both reading modes keep working; in keywords-only the tag, bold terms and strip show and the bullets stay hidden.

## Slice 2: the slide frame and the revision deck

1. **The slide under the line that cites it.** `src/lib/wrap-slide.js` reads a citation's "เดค <title> หน้า N"
   and looks the page up in the answer evidence's record (`src/data/evidence/<subject>.generated.js`), which is
   also the exact list of pages the image store holds: a page is uploaded only when a question's evidence row cites
   it. Titles match exactly after folding case, "&", punctuation and spacing; a title two documents share is
   dropped; a range ("หน้า 1-10") gets no picture. `components/WrapSlide.jsx` draws the page whole and unmarked
   (the stored highlights mark what a question asked, not this line), at the record's shape, lazily, one frame per
   page per item, through the same `/api/evidence-pass` address as the answer evidence (members only). States:
   loading (the box is already its final size), error (tap to retry, which asks for the address again), offline
   (fills in when the connection returns), none (draws nothing). The page waits to draw until the record and the
   wall have answered, 1.5 s at most, so no frame pushes a line down after reading has started.
2. **The revision deck** (`components/WrapDeck.jsx`, `lib/wrap-deck.js`), a third mode, "ทวนทีละใบ". One card per
   screen; each part covered (เชื้อ, คีย์เวิร์ด, ระวังสับสน, มีในข้อสอบเก่า, อาจารย์เน้น); "เปิดทั้งใบ" opens all
   but the lecturer's long list; "จำได้" and "ต้องทวน" appear only once the card is open; ต้องทวน cards wait in a
   pile (a sheet listing them, a round of their own, practice on their topics); a quick round shows the keywords
   and the mix-up uncovered and larger, with "ถัดไป" that moves on without a mark. Progress is a segment per card
   (known, to revise, on screen). State per device in `vmx-wrap-deck-<subject>-<term>-<phase>`; a missing, old or
   corrupt value starts clean. Keyboard: Space, 1, 2, arrows, Escape. No sync, no exam session.
3. Motion is one spring system (three damped springs baked into `linear()` curves, from the prototype), each
   effect off under reduced motion. Styles are chunk-scoped in `src/styles-wrap.css`.

### Which citations have a picture

Measured with `lib/wrap-slide.js` against today's evidence record. Pages cited one by one: 678; with a picture:
127; lines that get a frame: 125. Epidemiology final 64 of 233 pages, Equine Medicine final 63 of 224. Swine,
Zoonoses and One Health finals: none. The midterm wrap-ups cite no deck pages in this form.

Two different gaps:
- **The deck is in the record, the page is not rendered**: the upload sends only pages a question cites. Producing
  them needs no model call: a page list built from the wrap-ups' citations, `pages.py render --pages` on it
  (the PDFs are already fetched for these decks), `build.mjs upload` taught to send that list as well as the
  question rows, and the page sizes (in `pages.py lines`' output) written to the evidence index so the frame can
  reserve its box.
- **The deck is not in the record at all** (Swine Enteric Diseases, Swine Medicine 2024, ASF, CSF, PCV2,
  Pseudorabies, Nipah, JE, Exudative Epidermitis, M. suis; Mycozoonoses, Sporotrichosis, Prion Diseases,
  Epidemiological approach to zoonoses; the One Health final's eight decks; 1_vet epidem intro, 2_vet epidem basic
  concept, Intro. to Module 1, The Future Veterinarian, two Thai policy decks; Equine Surgery and Anesthesia,
  Equine lameness, Diagnostic analgesia). Some are the same document under another name ("Equine Surgery and
  Anesthesia" against "Equine surgery & anesthesia 5th year", "Mycozoonoses" against "Mycozoonoses Reading
  Material" and "... Sporotrichosis Present"); those need an alias checked page by page against the PDF, never a
  fuzzy match. The rest need the deck added to the subject's catalog (`build.mjs catalog`), `pages.py fetch` and
  `lines`, then the render and upload above.

None of this was run: no rendering, upload or model call in this release.
