# 5.212.0: ten more stems show their numbers as a table

Follows 5.204.0, which did this for 13 Epidemiology stems. The same method, applied to the rest of
the bank: a stem whose figures are compared or cross-classified (a 2x2 table, groups against
outcomes, values per group or per time window) keeps only its question, the figures sit in a table
above it, and the old sentence stays in `say` for the voice, the text-only screens, the models and
the evidence slide.

## How it was done

A script held each new stem, title and passage per id, read the old stem from the bank and copied
its literal into `say`, and refused to write unless, for every question: the object and its stem
literal were found once; it had no passage, `say` or figure and was `mcq`; `lintQuestionTables`
passed on the converted question (the figures of `say` are the figures of title, passage and stem;
the stem ends as `say` ends; 2 to 6 columns; a sentence above the table); `namesDocument` accepted
the stem; and every word of every header and row label occurs in the old stem. After writing it
re-read each bank and checked that every other question, and every other field of the ten, is
unchanged. Each bank keeps its quoting style (JSON keys or bare keys, double or single quotes) and
its CRLF line endings.

## Known limits

- The lint compares sets of figures; it cannot see two cells swapped. Each table was read against
  its sentence by hand, and should be again in review.
- A question's content revision (`questionRevision`, `src/lib/study-events.js`) hashes the stem
  and not the passage or `say`, as in 5.204.0. These ten stems changed, so their revision changed.
  A student who has one of them in a set started before the release and submits after the server
  has it: the run is still saved, but scored from the count the device sends instead of checked
  against the server's bank (`api/_lib/exam-scoring.js`), and a race room opened before the release
  asks the players to refresh. A set started after the release is not affected.
- Evidence slides: `wholeStem` is `say`, the old sentence, so a converted question keeps its slide
  (100040, 107002, 214545, 214603 and 214636 have one; `lint:evidence` still reports 702 slides
  and names none as waiting).
- Not checked on a real phone, at tablet or desktop width, or in the dark theme; no audio was
  listened to.
