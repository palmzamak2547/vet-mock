# 5.212.0: three more stems show their numbers as a table

Follows 5.204.0, which did this for 13 Epidemiology stems. The same method, tried on the rest of the
bank: a stem whose figures are compared or cross-classified keeps only its question, the figures sit
in a table above it, and the old sentence stays in `say` for the voice, the text-only screens, the
models and the evidence slide. Ten stems were converted first; two reviews found only three of the
tables better than their sentences, so the release ships those three and puts the other seven back.

## How it was done

A script held each new stem, title and passage per id, read the old stem from the bank and copied
its literal into `say`, and refused to write unless, for every question: the object and its stem
literal were found once; it had no passage, `say` or figure and was `mcq`; `lintQuestionTables`
passed on the converted question (the figures of `say` are the figures of title, passage and stem;
the stem ends as `say` ends; 2 to 6 columns; a sentence above the table); `namesDocument` accepted
the stem; and every word of every header and row label occurs in the old stem. For this version it
ran on the seven bank files as `origin/main` has them; afterwards every question of those seven
files was compared with `origin/main`, parsed and deep-equal: 841 questions, 3 differ (5088, 202274,
214636, each in `q`, `say`, `passage` and `passage_title` only). Each bank keeps its quoting style
and its CRLF line endings.

## Known limits

- The lint compares sets of figures; it cannot see two cells swapped. Each table was read against
  its sentence by hand and by the data review.
- A question's content revision (`questionRevision`, `src/lib/study-events.js`) hashes the stem
  and not the passage or `say`, as in 5.204.0. These three stems changed, so their revision changed.
  A student who has one of them in a set started before the release and submits after the server
  has it: the run is still saved, but scored from the count the device sends instead of checked
  against the server's bank (`api/_lib/exam-scoring.js`), and a race room opened before the release
  asks the players to refresh. A set started after the release is not affected.
- At 320x640 none of the three shows an option on the first screen (two had none before either).
- Not checked on a real phone, at tablet or desktop width, or in the dark theme; no audio was
  listened to.
