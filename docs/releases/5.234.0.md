The Equine Medicine and Surgery final is on 24 Nov 2026. This term's final matches the seniors' papers least of the
ten Year 5 finals, because the paper was split differently for them: Vet 84 sat infectious disease at their midterm,
and sat dentistry, GI and respiratory at their final. This release adds the recorded items found for topics that are
on this term's final only, records four marks a senior cohort left, and writes three questions for one thin topic.

## This term's midterm and final are kept apart

Every item was placed by its content alone. This term's paper for a topic was taken from two things that had to give
the same answer: the dates of the topic's sessions in the 2569 syllabus data against the midterm of 23 Sep
(`sessionPaper`), and `topicOnPaper`.

- 10 of the 18 topics the engine serves on the final agree: `equine-infectious`, `equine-lameness`, `equine-hoof`,
  `equine-anesthesia`, `equine-ophth`, `equine-poa`, `equine-derm`, `equine-surgery-wound`, `equine-exercise`, and
  `equine-pythium` (no session lists its id; its content is pp. 66-70 of the 15 Oct infectious deck).
- 7 are not the same: `equine-intro`, `equine-nutrition`, `equine-dentistry`, `equine-gi`, `equine-colic-bestfit`,
  `equine-parasites`, `equine-respi`. Every session of theirs ran before the midterm; the curriculum scopes them
  `both` because the final's POA part may draw on every topic (class announcements of 9 Aug and 16 Sep). Nothing was
  converted into these seven, and neither `curriculum.js` nor `exam-scope.js` was edited.
- 1 has no session this term: `equine-tendon`. Nothing was converted into it.
