# 5.155.0: read-aloud from shared Gemini clips (Avian Medicine first)

The question read-aloud plays pre-generated clips before it tries any live voice. Each stem,
option and option label ("ตัวเลือก A") is spoken once by Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, voice Kore, and
stored in the public R2 bucket `vetmock-tts` behind https://tts.vetmock.com, named by a hash of
model, voice and the exact text (`src/lib/tts-text.js`, shared by the page and the generator). A
per-subject manifest (`g1/m/<subject>.json`) says which clips exist; a question plays from clips
only when all of its pieces are listed, otherwise the old chain runs. Clips are AAC in `.mp4`
(Cloudflare's edge caches `.mp4` by default, not `.m4a`), cached a year (names never change for
the same text) and kept in the device's IndexedDB (`vmx-tts-shared`), so a question heard once
replays offline.

Generator: `node scripts/tts-clips.mjs --subject <id> | --all [--dry]` (OpenRouter
`google/gemini-3.8-flash-tts`; needs `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` or `TTS_KEYS_ENV`, a Cloudflare token
with R2 write, ffmpeg). Resumable: the bucket listing is the record of what is done; edited text
gets a new name and is generated on the next run. A clip whose length is far from its text's
(a voice that loops or stops early) is not uploaded.

Also fixed for every subject: options are read in the order on screen (they were read in source
order, before the per-question shuffle); a clip the browser refuses to play no longer leaves the
button stuck on "หยุดอ่านออกเสียง" (`playArrayBuffer` resolves false); Stop settles the clip it
pauses.

## Quality check

20 Avian stems with the most English terms, transcribed by Thonburian Whisper
(biodatlab/whisper-th-medium-combined) and scored by character error rate against the text on
screen. Baseline: Google th-TH-Standard-A reading the old pipeline's text (English spelled in Thai
sounds), standing in for the device voice students fall back to, because Microsoft's Edge voice
answers 403 to every client today (vetmock.com/api/tts included) and iApp answers 402.

- Mean CER: Gemini 0.253, baseline 0.370; Gemini lower on 12 of 20, equal on 3.
- The two read English terms differently: Gemini says them in English (Whisper writes them in
  Latin letters), the baseline in Thai sounds. CER penalises a script mismatch, so the absolute
  numbers overstate errors for both; the comparison is like for like.
