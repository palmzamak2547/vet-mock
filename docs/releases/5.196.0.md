## Method

- **Wording.** The clipping's own words and option order. Plain misspellings corrected; set labels
  ("ชุดB") moved to `source`; the typist's remarks and hedges taken out of 32 stems; where the
  picture is not in the clipping, what it shows is kept as `(ภาพ: ...)` text without the
  typist's inferred diagnosis. A gap in the options was filled with one distractor of the same kind
  and `source` says so; other gaps were dropped.
- **Year.** From the compilation item the clipping was cut from, only when the stems are at least
  0.2 alike or a person confirmed the pair; otherwise no year.
- **Keys.** Every record was answered blind (no marks, no keys), then compared with the answer the
  note's author marked (or the key of the rewrite it supersedes). Agreement: 652. Every disagreement,
  every doubt and every item without a second answer went to a review against sources actually
  opened (MSD/Merck Veterinary Manual, WOAH Manual of Diagnostic Tests for Aquatic Animals, ACVIM and
  AAFP consensus statements, PubMed Central): 44 settled with their sources in
  `verificationSources`, 1 with a different key than the blind answer
  (aquatic-p07-106: WOAH puts the highest titres in liver, which the author also marked). Where
  sources disagree, the stem lacks what decides it, or the picture decides and is missing, the item
  is held with the reason.
- **Supersedes.** 46 clippings are the originals of rewrites (37 in `questions-vca.js`, 9 in
  `questions-vca-reviewed.js`). Each enters with the rewrite's id, so answers students gave still
  count, and the rewrite leaves its file.
- **Figures.** Cut from the notes at the embedded image's resolution, or taken from the compilation
  docx. Every figure was viewed: 8 were cropped again to drop a typed label or the note author's
  marks ("TVT", "Giardiasis", "milk spots" named the answer), and the items whose figure is blank,
  the wrong picture or the note author's sketch were left out or held.
- **Citations.** The five PDFs are listed in `src/data/source-docs.js`; every item carries
  `sourcePages`. The text-only page citations went down, so `FREE_TEXT_BUDGET` is lowered to 4395.
