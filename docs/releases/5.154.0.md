# Study 5.154.0: full screen for writing, the way back from a document, VetWiki fixes

Follows 5.152.0 (library pages linked to topics). Owner asks on 2026-10-21: no clutter, no
middle dots or other slop on production, check VetWiki for bugs and fix them all, full screen
everywhere a student writes.

## What changed

- `src/hooks/useFullscreen.js`: one hook for the PDF reader, the handwriting pad and the image
  markup. The surface is pinned over the viewport (CSS, works on iPhone) and the PAGE asks for
  real fullscreen where allowed; the page and not the element, because element fullscreen hides
  the app's DialogHost and a confirm would wait unseen. Button, Esc and the browser's own exit
  all end both layers. In the image markup, Esc while full screen only leaves full screen (it used
  to close the board and throw the drawing away).
- Reader way back (App.jsx returnToLibrary): steps back in history when the origin is the entry
  right behind the reader, so browser Back afterwards no longer lands on an emptied reader; a
  VetWiki origin sets subject and topic from its URL (it used to reopen the index or the first
  article read). The related-documents return point is written only on this way back
  (`src/lib/related-return.js`), so it cannot fire on a later visit.
- VetWiki (audit by a review agent, 7 confirmed findings, all fixed): the menu opens the index
  because the URL decides on mount; tab titles for the index and each article; ReportConcern
  keeps focus and announces the thank-you; ProvenancePanel and WikiLinkForQuestion text 14 px;
  related documents are an end-of-article block in the same rows as the others, with an h2.
- No middle dots on screen: `plainSeparators()` in RichText turns " · " into ", " for the 1,615
  dots in governed notes and questions without touching their reviewed text; curriculum labels,
  atlas, legacy copy and the instructor directory (and its two generators) edited at the source.
  `exam-origins.js` keeps its dots: they are lookup keys.
- Evidence lines: symbol-font bullets become commas, partial first and last words are dropped
  only when they can be told apart (lower-case Latin, a Thai mark that cannot begin a word),
  the matched term is never cut. Checked against all 871 live evidence lines: 0 bullets or glyphs
  left, 0 terms lost.
- Image markup: icon tools at 44 px, Thai labels, colour swatches with a 44 px hit area.
