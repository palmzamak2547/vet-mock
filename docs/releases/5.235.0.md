# 5.235.0: the start of the practice setup sits at the right end of its row, and stays in reach

On 15 Nov the owner sent two pictures of the practice setup (`ตั้งค่าการฝึก`) on his iPad in landscape: the back button
and `เริ่มฝึก N ข้อ →` sat side by side at the far left under the summary card, `ปรับเอง` sat under the start as if it
were a third button, and with `ปรับเอง` open the start was only reachable at the very bottom, again on the left. He drew
the start moved to the right end of the row. The screens had been judged at 390 px and 1280 px only.

## The rule

Written in `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, "Action rows". The way back is first, at the left; the primary action is last and
ends at the right edge of the content it acts on. `.vmx-btn-row` places every such row; screens stop setting
`justify-content` or `flex-direction` inline. A disclosure such as `ปรับเอง` belongs inside the summary it adjusts, so
the screen reads summary, adjust, act. When the settings above can run past one screenful the row is sticky at the
bottom edge of the content area, above the phone's bottom bar, on the page background.

## What changed

- `src/styles.css`: `.vmx-btn-row` ends at the right (`flex-end`); the first of several children, or a way back alone,
  takes `margin-right: auto`. `.is-bar` keeps one line on a phone (back short, primary the rest), `.is-center` keeps a
  pair together in a centred empty state, `.is-stack` stays the list variant. `.vmx-config-actions` is sticky at every
  size, `bottom: calc(var(--vmx-bottom-nav-h, 0px) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px))`, page background, a 1 px
  border line on top; the phone-only floating card is gone. `.vmx-config-actions-row` is gone.
- `src/views/ConfigView.jsx`: `ปรับเอง` moved inside the `.vmx-decide` card under the reason line; the start bar is
  drawn once, after the rows, in both states (it was drawn in three places). The exam setup keeps its one-line summary
  above the same row. Switch copy: `เปิด: ตอบแล้วเห็นเฉลยและคำอธิบายทันที`, `ปิด: ดูเฉลยหลังส่งทั้งชุด`,
  `ปิด: ทำได้ตามสบาย ไม่จับเวลา` (no long dash, no plus sign).
- Rows put in rule order: `AuthRequiredState` (the sign-in wall of groups, race, leaderboard: back, then sign-in),
  `NotesView` load error (back, then retry), `DiagramLabelDrill` finished state (change diagram, then redo). Inline
  placement removed from `AuthView`, `HighlightToCard`, `VoiceSettings`; `PrivacyView`'s two lists of choices are
  `is-stack`.

Rows found and the verdict for each: `work/action-rows/LIST.md` in the owner's checkout (62 rows; 10 changed in 9 files;
the rest already followed the rule or belong to a lane in flight).
