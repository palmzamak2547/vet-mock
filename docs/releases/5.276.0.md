# 5.276.0: Equine Reproduction final wrap-up

The Equine Reproduction final is on 1 Dec 2026, 08:30-10:00. This release adds its wrap-up page, the one page a
student reads the evening before the paper.

## What changed

- `src/data/wrapups/equine-repro-final.js`, listed in `src/data/exam-wrapups.js` under the 2569-1 year-5 `1-final`
  paper, between Milk & Meat and Epidemiology (paper order).
- 3 groups by lecturer, 18 items, 105 lines. Topics: `eqrepro-endometritis` (3 items), `eqrepro-male` (2),
  `eqrepro-ai` (2), `eqrepro-stallion-infect` (3), `eqrepro-pregnancy` (5, neonatal foal care included as the 29 Oct
  session teaches it), `eqrepro-postpartum` (1), `eqrepro-surgery` (2). Every topic is a final topic by
  `topicOnPaper(subject, topic, '1-final')`.
- Every line cites a page of this year's deck for its topic (six 2569 decks, 121 distinct pages); nothing from earlier
  decks, recordings or memory. Lines are ordered within an item by what the seniors' papers asked most, without saying
  so on the page. The `examined` lists are empty: no line rests on a past paper.
