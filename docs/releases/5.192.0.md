## What changed

1. **The read-aloud is a player** (`src/lib/read-aloud.js`, `src/lib/read-aloud-store.js`, `ReadAloudBar.jsx`).
   - A fixed bar sits above the bottom nav and a phone's sticky previous/next row, so it never moves the page. It
     has previous and next point, play and pause, a scrubber over the whole reading, speed 0.75x to 2x (kept per
     device), and close. Its controls are 44 px, and the state line is `role=status`: กำลังโหลดเสียง,
     หยุดชั่วคราว, an error in words, or เสียงอ่านของเครื่อง.
   - Points are the stem and each option row of a question, and the lines of a multi-line explanation. A
     one-paragraph explanation moves 10 s.
   - Where a student stopped is kept for the session, so play resumes there.
   - Media Session gives lock-screen and headset controls: play, pause, previous, next, seek.
   - One `HTMLAudioElement` plays every clip. `primePlayer()` plays a silent sound on it inside the tap, the rule
     iOS sets for later async plays, and keeps it playing from clip to clip with the screen locked.
   - The silent sound's own events are ignored. Its `ended` had advanced a job whose first clip was still
     downloading, found while testing.
   - An interruption (a call, other audio) shows as paused. A fast tap on next cancels the pending play
     (AbortError) without counting as a failure.
2. **What is being read is marked in place.**
   - The block (stem, option row, เหตุผล) gets an inset edge.
   - The phrase being spoken gets a CSS Custom Highlight, with no DOM change, so nothing reflows. The page
     follows it unless the student scrolled in the last 4 s, and it keeps the piece above the bar.
   - The spoken text and the shown text are matched character by character (`align`, a longest common
     subsequence), so a symbol said in words still lands on its phrase.
   - Word timings come from `e1/<key>.json`. Hidden tabs stop animation frames, so the media `timeupdate` also
     moves the highlight.
3. **เหตุผล has a read-aloud control.** A real 44 px button draws a 32 px circle. The pseudo-element hit area of
   the first version broke STABILITY.md §1. It reads `q.explain` as one clip.
4. **A second clip generation, `e1`**, beside Gemini's `g1` on the same public R2 bucket behind tts.vetmock.com:
   ElevenLabs `eleven_v4_turbo`, voice Sarah, which Palm rated 5.00 in both blind rounds (tts-lab RND.md).
   - `sharedPlan` takes the newest generation whose manifest lists every clip of the question, so a question is
     never read in two voices.
   - Every generation's manifest is requested at once. Only the chosen one's first clip is fetched ahead, so a
     voice that lacks the question costs no 404 and no download.
   - A browser without AAC in MP4 goes straight to the live voices.
5. **The live voices**: text over 900 characters goes in pieces (`speechPieces`). 135 explanations are over
   1,000 characters, and the cloud voices refuse those (a refusal also switched iApp off for the session).
6. **Speech text says what the screen means** (`stripForSpeech`, `normalizeSymbols`).
   - Before this release:
     - every `>` and `~` was deleted, so "fibrinogen > 700" was read "fibrinogen 700";
     - emoji were read as their names.
   - Now:
     - emoji and check marks are dropped;
     - `>` or `<` before a number is มากกว่า or น้อยกว่า, and "HCO3 >32" is a comparison;
     - a chain of two or more `>` between bare numbers ("2 > 4 > 1 > 3") is a step order, read as pauses;
     - `~` before a number is ประมาณ;
     - `10^6`, °C, °F, `↑`, `↓`, `≈`, `≠`, `√`, `♂`, `♀`, `↔`, and `*` or `−` between numbers are said in words;
     - arrows are read before comparisons, so "7.4 -> 7.2" is not "more than";
     - line breaks, bullets and spaced table bars are pauses, and a bar inside a formula, P(D+|T+), stays.
   - Measured on the stored clips: the final rules change none of the 10,844 Year-5 final texts the e1 clips were
     made from. They rename 4 of 1,945 stored g1 avian clips (°C), and those 4 were regenerated in g1, so no
     question lost its clips.
7. **The generator** (`scripts/tts-clips.mjs`).
   - Options:
     - `--gen e1`;
     - `--year 5 --scope final`: the subjects the timetable seats at that paper;
     - `--explain`, `--ids`, `--manifests`, `--align`.
   - Order: subjects take turns by exam date, each through its own Panic pool in Panic Mode's order, then the rest.
   - Budget guards reserve an estimate per request and settle it from the `character-cost` header (`--max-credits`).
     They also watch the account balance (`--keep-remaining`): it is read every 40 requests and retried on 429.
   - Encoding is async, which took the run from 7 to about 40 clips a minute.
   - Manifests are written even when a run fails, and R2 writes retry through a dropped connection: a DNS outage
     on 2 Nov killed a run before its manifests.
   - Word timings come with e1 clips at no extra cost (TTS with timestamps). For older clips, forced alignment
     makes them and tolerates whitespace it drops.

## Reviews

Two independent reviews before the merge; every finding is fixed.

- **First (2 Nov), 7 findings:**
  - the new e2e was flaky by construction;
  - the budget guard could fail open (no ok check, no in-flight reserve);
  - two readings could overlap;
  - text over 1,000 characters switched iApp off;
  - a pseudo-element hit area broke STABILITY.md §1;
  - g1 lost its first-clip prefetch;
  - three speech rules misfired off the paid set ("HCO3 >32", ♂ ♀ ↔, "7.4 -> 7.2").
- **Second (2 Nov, fixed 7 Nov), 13 findings, each with the fix:**
  1. A failed download stuck. The rejected promise is dropped, play fetches the piece again, and the fetch timeout covers the body.
  2. A seek during a slow download left the old clip playing and let its `ended` end the job. A new piece now takes the element at once.
  3. A pause in the gap survived next and skipped a label. It is cleared on every jump.
  4. The bar sat 58 px high in focus mode. It now reads the nav height from `.vmx-app`, and the end spacer is measured from the bar: on an iPhone 13 frame it sits 12 px above the previous/next row.
  5. The bar was above dialogs. It is now `calc(var(--z-nav) + 1)`, 701, under every modal, overlay and toast.
  6. The lock screen's pause could resume. Play and pause are now separate actions.
  7. Prefetch cost about 8 MB per 50 questions. Warm-up now fetches the manifests and the stem's clip. An explanation's clip comes only for a student who has listened to one, never under Save-Data, and only the chosen voice downloads.
  8. 'loading' flashed and was announced at every piece. It shows only while a clip is still on its way.
  9. A stop during a download saved the old clip's time. A paused job's clip is no longer replaced by the tap's silent sound.
  10. A slow engine load could start the previous question's job. The tap now checks the card still shows that question.
  11. The scrubber: a screen reader's adjustment and pointercancel now commit the seek. The main button lost its aria-pressed, and the time moves with each jump.
  12. The stem's highlight is cleared when the options start. The text map is banded (about 0.3 M cells instead of 2.8 M on the longest explanation).
  13. The generator:
      - no retry on a 4xx;
      - every manifest is tried, and the temp folder is always removed;
      - unmatched alignments are remembered and not billed again;
      - the account is read by requests since the last read;
      - the clip fetch for alignment retries.
