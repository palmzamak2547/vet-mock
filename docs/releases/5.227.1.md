# 5.227.1: the wiki term glossary on a phone, and in the wiki's typefaces

Found by the production walk of 15 Nov 2026 (eight releases in 24 hours, served build `d759d79d`). The full walk,
with every bug, odd spot, crowding count and timing, is kept outside the repository; this release fixes the two
defects that were small, certain and in a screen those releases shipped.

## What changed

1. **Typefaces.** The glossary box at the end of the 16 zoonoses chapters (5.223.0) set `color` from two font-stack
   tokens (`--vw-display`, `--vw-body`). A font stack is not a colour, so both declarations were dropped: the title
   was plain body text instead of the chapter's display face. Now `font-family` takes the token and the colour is
   `--clr-ink`. The title follows the Thai display rule (line-height 1.2, no letter-spacing).
2. **Phone layout.** At 390 px the three columns were about 90 px each, so the meaning ran one or two words to a
   line. Under 640 px each term is one entry: the term in bold, its Thai below in the soft ink, then the meaning.
   The column heads stay for screen readers only, and explicit ARIA roles on the table keep it a table to
   assistive tech when the rows are laid out as blocks. On zoo-rabies the box goes from 3,328 px to 2,053 px.
   1280 px is unchanged apart from the faces.

## Guard

`tests/unit/wiki-glossary-layout.test.mjs`: no stylesheet in `src/` sets a colour from a font-stack token; the
glossary title and cells take the wiki faces; one phone block lays each term out as one entry, and the table carries
its roles. All three fail on main and pass here.

## Design gate (Rule 11, the clutter gate)

The same chapter end before and after at 390 px and 1280 px, light and dark, was compared on production with the new
rules applied in the browser.

1. One primary action: the box has none (reading only); unchanged.
2. Count at 390 px, first screenful of the box: controls 0 before, 0 after; labels 4 before (title and three column
   heads), 1 after (title; the column heads are now for screen readers only).
3. Reach: no targets added.
4. One tap away: nothing added on screen.
5. Groups: each term with its Thai and meaning; three text roles (term, Thai, meaning) on one spacing scale.
6. Words: unchanged copy.
7. States: long meanings wrap within the column width instead of one word to a line; nothing jumps.
8. After the tap: not applicable.
