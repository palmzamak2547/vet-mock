# 5.207.0: data the app held but a reader dropped

An audit against origin/main at 512a3479 measured places where a question or summary carried a
value that the code reading it never used. This release fixes five of them and adds a guard for
each so it cannot come back unnoticed.

## What a student sees

- **Anki cards carry their figure.** 168 questions in the bank have a figure (104 `imagePath`,
  64 `image`); 155 of them are deliverable, so they can be exported. Before: the front of the card
  was the stem ("ดูภาพประกอบ") with no picture. After: all 168 get an `<img>` on the front (160
  same-origin paths as `https://vetmock.com/...`, 8 `data:` images as they are). A figure Anki
  cannot open (a `blob:` URL, a host the app would not render) leaves the question out, and the
  export screen now says how many. In today's bank that number is 0.
- **17 questions are in their topic.** 40 questions had no `topic` (repro 19, surg3 8, surg2 9,
  com4 4), so no topic-filtered set ever drew them. 17 now carry a topic taken from their subject's
  own curriculum list (table in the pull request). All 17 are deliverable.
- **Lecture summaries show their length and a heading.** 18 COM I summaries wrote
  `duration: 'N min'`, which nothing reads; they now carry `durationMin`, so the modal shows
  "N นาที". Four preclinic-orientation summaries had `title: null`: the heading was empty and
  saving as a file threw on `title.replace`. The modal now uses the summary's own first `# `
  heading (2 of the 4 have one), else "สรุปคลิปบรรยาย", and the saved file is named after the
  videoId when there is no title. Saved file names also keep Thai letters now.

### The 14 flags

Before, 14 questions carried `flag` as a string: `'verify-2026'` x11 (poultry 1702, 1707, 1714,
1719, 1729, 1731, 1736, 1741, 1747, 1750, 1763), `'corrected-from-student'` (poultry 1728),
`'tricky-stem'` (poultry 1759) and `'minor'` (vet-juris 93037). `questionNeedsAnswerReview` already
withheld all 14. The card, the source chip and the review screen read `flag.note`, which a string
does not have, so nothing was shown; `pickTodaysQ` tested `!q.flag?.severity`, which a string
passes (Home filters by deliverability first, so no withheld question was actually picked).

Each now has `severity: 'unclear'`, the one severity that keeps a flagged question withheld
(`major` and `minor` with a note are delivered with a warning). The notes come from what the bank
already recorded: the poultry file header (borderline items to check against the current lecture
slides; 1728's answer corrected from the student's F to T), 1759's explanation, and 93037's
`verified` field (two sources disagree, e versus c; c chosen by section 4 of the 2558 Act).
Withheld before: 14 of 14. Withheld after: 14 of 14. `lint:delivery` reports 211 withheld, as
before.

### What could not be filled

- **23 questions still have no topic.** None of their subject's topics covers them: Repro Lab has
  four lab topics and 9 items are treatment or endocrinology; Surg Lab II has no mammary or wiring
  lab (5); Surg Lab III has no femoral approach or suture lab (5); COM IV has no nephrology or
  urology lecture (4). They stay in the subject's whole-subject sets.
- **Two summaries have no title anywhere** (OG8btFPo6U0, r-LT4yGdHCY): no title, no `# ` heading,
  and `VIDEO_META` is derived from the same entries. They show "สรุปคลิปบรรยาย".
- **Nine summaries have no length on record**: the four orientation entries (`null`) and five COM II
  entries (`durationMin: 0`, which the modal hides). No transcript is on disk to measure them.
