# 5.207.0: three more subjects get the slides under their answers

The second batch of [5.199.0](5.199.0.md)'s feature: Epidemiology, Avian Medicine and Food Industry
(FIQC). Three more generated indexes and one change to how a slide is tied to its question. Design
and pipeline: [ANSWER-EVIDENCE.md](../ANSWER-EVIDENCE.md).

## What is in it

222 questions of three finals, each on a page of a 2569 library deck. With the first six subjects
that makes **702 questions in nine subjects**.

| Subject | Final pool | Proposed | Passed the last reading | Pages | Decks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Epidemiology | 355 | 185 | 150 | 124 | 12 |
| Avian Medicine | 109 | 58 | 50 | 46 | 10 |
| Food Industry (FIQC) | 61 | 22 | 22 | 20 | 3 |

No model was paid for in this release: the matching had already run on 11 Nov, and what was left
was the reading by eye, the index and the upload.

## A stem laid out as a table keeps its slide

5.204.0 moved the numbers of 13 Epidemiology stems into a passage table and kept the sentence they
were in as `say`. A slide is shown only while its question hashes as it did when the slide was
matched (`evidenceHash`: the stem and the key), so three of those questions (214506, 214595,
214637) would have hidden a slide that still belongs to them. `evidenceHash` now reads the whole
sentence (`wholeStem(q)`), which is what the slide was matched to. For a question without `say`
nothing changes. Editing `say` drops the slide, as editing a stem does.

## How it was checked

- **The last reading.** All 265 proposed slides were drawn as the student sees them
  (`pages.py sheets`, 90 sheets) and read by three independent checkers: 218 right, 45 weak,
  2 with the key in doubt, none wrong.
- **A second reading of the close calls.** The checkers had read calculation questions two ways:
  one passed a slide that prints the formula the key uses, another turned such slides down because
  the result is not printed. The rule is now written down (a calculation's result comes from the
  question's own figures, which no slide prints: the formula or the definition on the lines is
  enough; another example is not). Four panels were read again against it and passed: 214603,
  214629, 214650, 214652, three of them looked at by hand. That gives 222.
- **By hand.** Four sheets (twelve panels) from two of the checkers, compared with their verdicts:
  nine held, and the other three are among the calculation panels above.
- **Every page image answers.** A request for each of the 190 cited pages at the slide store
  returns an image (124, 46 and 20).
- `npm run lint:evidence`: 702 slides in 9 subjects, none waiting to be matched again.
  `tests/unit/answer-evidence.test.mjs`: 14 of 14, with new assertions for a stem laid out as a table.
