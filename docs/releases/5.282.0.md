# 5.282.0: the screen after a set is one card and one button, and a set goes to a friend from one place

## What a student notices

1. **The score is said once.** One card holds the subject and topic, the verdict word
   (ถึงเกณฑ์ซ้อมแล้ว or ยังไม่ถึงเกณฑ์ซ้อม), the number, one line "ถูก 8 จาก 10 ข้อ ใน 6 นาที" (with
   "ข้าม N ข้อ" when some were left blank) and the bar with one tick at 60%. Gone: the personal-best
   banner, "คะแนนตรวจอัตโนมัติ", the 0% and 100% ends, the time chip, the three count tiles, the
   ladder's "ได้ 80%" and its sentence, and the "ต่อจากนี้แนะนำ" box. A low score is drawn in ink, not rose.
2. **One filled button, under the thumb.** On a phone it is pinned to the bottom edge, full width, 56 px,
   above the tab bar; on a wide screen it heads the right-hand column. Practice: แก้ข้อที่ผิด N ข้อ. After an
   exam, which showed no key while it ran: ดูเฉลยข้อที่ผิด N ข้อ, with the redo second. More than half
   wrong, when the set's topic has a summary: อ่านสรุปเรื่องนี้ก่อน, with the redo as the next step. No
   misses: the next rung of the practice ladder.
3. **The rest is one tap away.** ดูเฉลย and ส่งให้เพื่อน side by side, then one next-step row. Below: the
   หาจุดร่วม card, one line about the review queue, the ladder strip, and the day count and the reminder
   offer when they apply.
4. **One way to send the set.** ส่งให้เพื่อน opens one sheet: the set as the friend will get it
   (subject, topic, count, "ไม่มีคะแนนของคุณ"), the link, ส่งใน LINE, คัดลอกลิงก์, แอปอื่น where the
   browser has a share sheet, สตอรี่ (the score picture, at 70% and up or for a written set, as before)
   and ทำพร้อมกันตอนนี้ (the live race). It replaces ท้าเพื่อนทำชุดนี้, ส่งเข้ากลุ่ม, สร้างห้องแข่ง,
   สร้างรูปคะแนน, and แชร์ชุดนี้ on the answers page.
5. **The link is short and carries no score.** `vetmock.com/s/<code>`, about 50 characters for ten
   questions (it was about 470). The code holds the subject and the question ids only: no score, time,
   name or anything typed. The chat preview shows the subject's cover and the count. Links made before
   this release still open, and their comparison still shows.
6. **A friend who is not signed in sees the set.** The sign-in page reads "เพื่อนส่งชุดโจทย์มาให้" with
   the subject and count, and after signing in the set opens. A signed-in friend goes straight to it.
7. **A late-night promotion is said on ความคืบหน้า,** in the ยศโต้รุ้ง card, not on the results.

## Not changed

Scoring, what is saved and when, the order in which results are saved and the finished set is cleared,
the failure count added in 5.274.0, the answers page's cards, and the exam itself.

## Checks

- `tests/unit/results-calm.test.mjs` drives the real `ResultsView.jsx`: the score once, one filled
  button and its target in practice and after an exam, one share entry and what the sheet is handed,
  ink at a low score. Each fails on the screen before this release.
- `tests/unit/share-link-custom.test.mjs`: the /s/ link round trip, nothing but ids in it, the fallback,
  and old links still read.
- `tests/e2e/chula-wall.spec.js`: a shared link signed out shows the set and opens it after sign-in;
  signed in, the set opens.
- `tests/e2e/results-peak-end.spec.js` and `tests/e2e/share-cards.spec.js` walk the new screen and sheet.
- The stylesheet lost the rules of the old results screen and of the old matching layout: 134 lines no
  element can match (every class was looked for by name across the code, the data, the pages and the
  tests). First-load CSS is 50,186 B gzip, down from 50,653 B; the atlas page's is 53,734 B of its
  54,400 B budget. No budget was raised. `tests/unit/exam-results-ladder-pins.test.mjs` keeps the old
  screen's classes from coming back without an element.
- `tests/e2e/case-reader.spec.js`: the case where a question's time runs out with the case open full
  screen now moves the page's clock itself from the time-out on, so the wait for the browser's popstate
  is not counted on it. It had failed once on a busy runner; after this change it still passed only on
  its retry once in CI at phone width, so its cause is not settled.
- `tests/e2e/case-sheet-paper-end.spec.js` finds the results by the region named ผลชุดนี้, the new
  screen's own landmark, instead of a class of the old screen. Its three cases pass on desktop and
  phone Chromium.
