# 5.190.0: the video library, second batch

The second batch of the video library (docs/VIDEO-LIBRARY.md). The first batch, 5.189.0 (#139, 64530c34), shipped the page and 17 cards; this one adds 54 cards, so 71 clips now have a study card.

## What changes

- **Cards:** 71 in all: 70 lectures of @dai.1387 and 1 practical-class clip of a classmate's channel.
  - Every card passed the summary gates.
  - Every new card was also read by hand.
  - 5 clips have our own copy, with a skip list that saves 39 minutes in all.
- **Classmates' channels:** the 8 channels whose clips sit in Dai's "by student" playlists are listed in `src/data/video-channels.js`, by id only. The main session decided this on 2 Nov from public metadata, with Palm's agreement for the cohort. The evidence for each channel is in the lane ledger.
  - Most of their 40 clips are short practical demos with little speech. 25 had too little to summarise, and 1 is in this batch.
  - A US clinic's video that sits in the same playlist stays out.
- **Voice rules, after the read-through:**
  - "ต้องจำ", "ท่อง", course logistics (reports, homework, "นักศึกษา") and a one-liner that opens with "บรรยาย" are now lint errors.
  - 8 passing cards were repaired under these rules (one of them, qQaAsu7Igsw, was in batch 1 and is corrected here). Three more are held until repaired.
  - Two lines were fixed by hand:
    - a repair had made CEM "the only" bacterial venereal disease of horses;
    - the captions heard "remove" as "รีโหมด".
- **Mapping:** "emergency" no longer matches a topic by itself, since every session of that course carries it. A triage lecture no longer links to the cardiovascular-emergency session.
- **Headings:** a letter-spaced deck heading with a middle dot is named properly, and no middle dot reaches a chapter title.
- **Pipeline:**
  - An ElevenLabs Scribe transcript (`video.scribe.json3`, from the audio lane) is preferred over YouTube's captions. An analysis older than a new transcript or a new silence measurement is redone.
  - `--no-asr` uploads a copy with no captions, so Scribe can read its audio from R2.
  - The registry can publish only a batch that has been read through (`VIDEO_BATCH`).
