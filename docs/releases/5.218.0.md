How they were checked:

- Each note was read a second time against the page images by a reader who had not written it, item by item
  (about 110 corrections). No formula was the wrong way round. Most corrections removed a sentence the
  slide does not say or added content a page teaches; every change is logged with before and after.
- A slide that could lead to a wrong answer is kept as printed and marked with a neutral flag naming the page
  (for example the measurement deck p.49, which divides by the non-diseased, and the statistics deck p.99, a t-test
  example under a Z-test title). A rounding difference in the last digit or a spelling slip is not flagged.
- Three image-only decks (the module intros and The Future Veterinarian) were read page by page as images.
- The software topic: no deck of this year teaches WinEpiscope or SPSS. The note says so and covers the
  calculators the sample size deck shows (OpenEpi, Statulator, Epitools, G*Power).
- The policy deck's penalty figures for Section 7 cannot be read from the slide. The note gives Section 55 of the
  Animal Epidemics Act B.E. 2558 from the Act's own text (English translation on the Ministry of Agriculture and
  Cooperatives site), named as its source.
- The app draws `**bold**` only with no star inside it and pairs two lone stars as italics (`src/lib/richtext.jsx`),
  so formulas write the multiplication sign as × and a line naming G*Power carries no bold.

## VetWiki: one checked claim for each new section

The wiki requires a claim backed by a real external source for every section it shows. 111 new sections:

- 98 carry a claim (`src/lib/vetwiki/verification-epidemiology.js`, merged in code by `verification.js`). Each
  claim quotes the words of its source. 48 sources are new in `sources.js`: 37 PubMed records and 11 pages (the
  WOAH Terrestrial Manual chapter on validation, the WOAH Terrestrial Code, OpenEpi and Epitools documentation,
  the Animal Epidemics Act); the others cite the CDC "Principles of Epidemiology" guide already registered.
- Every source was fetched again after the claim was written and the quoted words were looked for in it: 85 found
  by a script in the record's abstract, free full text or page; 9 in the text of a PDF; 3 in the Act's Word file;
  1 by reading the passage (a reference mark sits inside the quoted sentence).
- 13 are exempt with a reason each in `non-verifiable.js`: class exercises, lab briefings, made-up teaching
  scenarios, and descriptions of what a slide or a calculator screen shows.
- `tests/unit/vetwiki-sources-work.test.mjs` pins the number of works on the masthead: 2,106 to 2,154.
