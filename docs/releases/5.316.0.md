## How the items were checked

- The copy's pages that the 24-page copy lacks, and every mark on it, were read page by page. Each mark was matched
  against the bank, and a point the bank already asks was not written again.
- 20 items were drafted. Two prion statements appear twice in the copy (pages 11 and 18) and were folded into one
  item each: 18 filed.
- Every key was answered blind from the deck text by a second reader. 14 of the 15 choice and true/false keys agree,
  and the three short answers carry the same points. The one the blind reader could not settle from the deck
  (scrapie and CWD through milk, 219939) keeps the record's answer, false, which agrees with Tamgüney et al. 2012
  (Prion 6:52-61): milk and placenta carry scrapie prions, while CWD prions are shed in saliva, urine and feces.
- The chain-of-infection grouping (219937) was checked against the figure on the deck's page 22.
