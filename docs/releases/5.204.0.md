# 5.204.0: the numbers of 13 Epidemiology stems sit in a table

Follows 5.202.0, which left these out on purpose. Thirteen Epidemiology stems carried their data in
the sentence itself: "กลุ่มสูบบุหรี่ 800 คน เป็นมะเร็งปอด 90 คน (ความเสี่ยง 0.113) กลุ่มไม่สูบบุหรี่ 1,200 คน ...".
The student had to draw the 2x2 before the question started. Moving the numbers out changes the stem,
and the stem is what the voice reads, so the voice had to be taught first.

## What a student sees

- **13 questions**: 214506, 214535, 214565, 214572, 214595, 214624, 214626, 214637 (marked), 213503,
  213520, 213528, 213557 (recalled) and 105090. The numbers are a table above the question, under a
  title and one sentence that says what the table is; the stem asks only what is asked. 214637 is
  two small tables, one per stratum, each under its own line with its OR.
- **Read aloud** still says the whole original sentence, every number in it. A passage is not read
  aloud, so without this the voice would have asked "จากข้อมูลนี้ ค่า relative risk เท่ากับเท่าใด" and
  said none of the data.
- **Recorded voice kept.** 213503, 213520 and 213528 had a recorded clip of their stem. The clip is
  named by the sentence, and the sentence is unchanged, so the same clip plays.
- **The coach after a wrong answer** is still shown the sentence with its numbers.

## How

A question whose numbers moved to a passage table keeps its old stem in a new field, `say`.

| | |
|---|---|
| `src/lib/rich-blocks.js` | `spokenStem(q)`: `say` where a question has one, the stem otherwise |
| `src/components/Question.jsx` | the read-aloud job takes `spokenStem`; no phrase is marked in a stem read from `say`, because the words being said are not the words on screen |
| `scripts/tts-clips.mjs` | names and records the stem clip from `spokenStem`, so a later run records the sentence the page asks for |
| `api/study-coach.js` | the wrong-answer coach and the session review are given `spokenStem` |
| `scripts/lint-question-tables.mjs` | `say` only on a question whose passage holds a table, never equal to the stem, never a table; the figures of `say` are exactly the figures of the passage and the stem; a passage table needs a sentence above it |
| `docs/QUESTION-STANDARD.md` C5, `AGENTS.md` | the rule for writers |

## What was checked

- **The numbers.** Each stem was converted by a script that refuses a question unless the figures
  of the old sentence and the figures on screen (passage and new stem) are the same set; the lint
  now holds that for every question with `say`. No figure was added: a "รวม" column appears only
  where the sentence gave the totals (214572, 213520, 105090).
- **The clips**, against the live manifest of `tts.vetmock.com` on 11 Nov: 55 Epidemiology stems
  match a recorded clip by the sentence read aloud, as before this release. By the stem on screen it
  would have been 52; the three kept are 213503, 213520 and 213528.
- **The voice is asked for the numbers.** An e2e test opens such a question with no recorded clip,
  presses read aloud and reads the request sent to the live voice: every number that left the stem
  is in it.
- **The coach.** A unit test drives the real handler and reads what the model is shown.
- **By eye** on an iPhone 13 viewport and at 1280 px, five of the thirteen.
- **Tests.** `tests/unit/rich-blocks.test.mjs` (10), `tests/unit/study-coach.test.mjs` (25),
  `tests/e2e/question-tables.spec.js` (4, on Chromium desktop, Chromium mobile and Firefox:
  12 of 12 against the dev server), `npm run lint:data`.

## Known limits

- The thirteen leave the screens that draw a stem and its options and nothing else, as every
  passage question does: the daily question, the race, the Instagram card and the quiz cards the
  AI hands out (`isTextOnlyQuestion`, `isQuizzable`). They need their table to be answered.
- While the voice reads one of these stems, no phrase is marked in it.
- In search and on the pinboard the question is listed by its new, shorter stem. A pin made before
  keeps the label it was made with and still opens the question.
- A passage is not sent to the wrong-answer coach. That was already so for every passage question
  (reading passages, shared data passages); these thirteen are spared only because `say` holds
  their data. Giving the coach the passage changes what the model is shown and needs paid test
  runs, so it is left for its own release.
