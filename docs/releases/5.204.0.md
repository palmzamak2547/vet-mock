# 5.204.0: the numbers of 13 Epidemiology stems sit in a table

Follows 5.202.0, which left these out on purpose. Thirteen Epidemiology stems carried their data in
the sentence itself: "กลุ่มสูบบุหรี่ 800 คน เป็นมะเร็งปอด 90 คน (ความเสี่ยง 0.113) กลุ่มไม่สูบบุหรี่ 1,200 คน ...".
The student had to draw the 2x2 before the question started. Moving the numbers out changes the stem,
and the stem is what a voice reads, what a model is shown and what several screens draw alone, so
the old sentence had to stay within reach of all of them first.

## What a student sees

- **13 questions**: 214506, 214535, 214565, 214572, 214595, 214624, 214626, 214637 (marked), 213503,
  213520, 213528, 213557 (recalled) and 105090. The numbers are a table above the question, under a
  title and a sentence that says what the table is and its unit. The stem asks only what is asked,
  and where a result has to be interpreted (the RR of 214624 and 214626, the kappa of 214565) the
  result stays in the stem, beside the options. 214637 is two small tables, one per stratum;
  213557 is the neuter-status row, then the breeds.
- **Read aloud** still says the whole original sentence, every number in it. While it does, the bar
  says "โจทย์และตาราง", the table carries the reading edge and is brought into view.
- **Recorded voice kept.** 213503, 213520 and 213528 had a recorded clip of their stem. The clip is
  named by the sentence, and the sentence is unchanged, so the same clip plays.
- **Nowhere do they lose their numbers.** The daily question, the race and the share card draw a
  stem and options and nothing else: for these 13 they draw the whole sentence, as before. The
  wrong-answer coach and the AI's question corpus are given it too.
- **Search** finds a question by its passage's title ("ยาคุมกำเนิด", "มาลาเรีย"), shown beside its place.

For every question with a passage, not only these:

- **The button back to the passage** no longer covers the next button on a phone (it sat on the
  right half of "ข้อถัดไป"). It shows only while the passage is off screen, says "ดูตาราง" when the
  passage is a table, and opens a passage the student had hidden.
- **A passage hidden on one question** no longer stays hidden on the next question's different
  passage (the next question arrived with no data on screen).
- **The passage's title** is 14 px and as typed. It was 11 px capitals: "2x2" read "2X2".

## How

A question whose numbers moved to a passage table keeps its old stem in a new field, `say`.
`wholeStem(q)` (`src/lib/rich-blocks.js`) is `say` where a question has one and the stem otherwise:
the question as one sentence that holds its own data. Whatever meets a question without its passage
asks there.

## What was checked

- **The numbers.** Each stem was converted by a script that refuses a question unless the figures
  of the old sentence and the figures on screen are the same set. The rewording after the reviews
  was done by a second script that rebuilds every table body from the table already in the bank and
  refuses a changed cell. No figure was added: a "รวม" column appears only where the sentence gave
  the totals (214572, 213520, 105090).
- **Two independent reviews**, one on logic and data, one on what a student meets on a phone, a
  tablet and a desktop in both themes. No blocker. The first read all 13 tables against their
  sentences cell by cell and recomputed every keyed answer from the table as drawn. What they found
  and this release fixes:
  - readers of a stem the first version missed: the gentle order of a set, the duplicate and
    recurrence detectors, search, the evidence and proof tooling, and the text-only screens and the
    AI's corpus, which the 13 had simply left;
  - `say` and the stem were held together only by their digits (now also by their last words), a
    figure in a title was not checked, and a second table could go without a line above it;
  - the value to interpret had left three stems; headers broke inside a word on a phone
    ("ตาย / จากอหิ / วาต์ (คน)"); two headers did not say what they count; a stem repeated its title;
    one title said more than its sentence, and one called every row an associated factor when the
    key is the row that is not;
  - nothing on screen followed the voice through a 30 to 55 second stem;
  - and, already so for every passage question: the button over the next button, a hidden passage
    that stayed hidden, the 11 px title.
- **The clips**, against the live manifest of `tts.vetmock.com` on 11 Nov: 55 Epidemiology stems
  match a recorded clip by the sentence read aloud, as before this release. By the stem on screen it
  would have been 52; the three kept are 213503, 213520 and 213528.
- **By eye and by measure** on an iPhone 13 viewport, at 360 px and at 1280 px, all 13 after the
  fixes: the button never over the next button, no page overflow, no table scrolling sideways,
  titles at 14 px.
- **Tests.**
  - `tests/e2e/question-tables.spec.js` (5 tests, 15 of 15 on Chromium desktop, Chromium mobile and
    Firefox against the dev server). The fourth serves the clip host as a recording run leaves it
    and checks that the page asks for the clip named by `say`, never the one named by the stem on
    screen, that the bar says "โจทย์และตาราง", that the table carries the edge and that no phrase is
    marked. The fifth walks the button back to a table: absent over the table, clear of the next
    button at the end of the page, reopening a hidden table.
  - `tests/e2e/read-aloud.spec.js` on the same three browsers.
  - Unit: `rich-blocks` (the lint's rules), `study-coach` (the miss and the session review are shown
    the sentence), `text-only-surfaces`, `ai-chat-retrieval` (the 13 stay in the corpus, read as
    their sentence), `practice-ladder`, `lint-q-dupes`, `exam-recurrence`; each new case fails
    without its fix. `npm run test:unit` and `npm run lint:data` on the final tree.

## Known limits

- The lint compares sets of figures, typed alike on both sides. It catches a figure dropped or
  added, not two cells swapped: a table is still read against its sentence by a person.
- 214565's sentence gives kappa = 0.460; its four cells give 0.456. Both read as moderate agreement
  and the key holds. Left as the source has it, for the bank's owner.
- The session review shows a model the first 300 characters of a question, which cuts the ask off
  for 213520, 213528, 213557 and 214637. It did before this release as well.
- A passage is not sent to the wrong-answer coach for a passage question that has no `say`
  (reading passages, shared data passages). Already so; it needs paid test runs, so it is left for
  its own release.
- A question's content revision hashes its stem, not its passage or `say`. Already so for every
  passage question.
- The review list draws the stem above the table, the reverse of the card.
- Not checked on a real iPhone or Android phone (Chromium emulation and Playwright WebKit and
  Firefox), and no audio was listened to.
