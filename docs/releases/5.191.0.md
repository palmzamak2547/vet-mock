# 5.191.0: the video library, second batch

The second batch of the video library (docs/VIDEO-LIBRARY.md). The first batch, 5.189.0 (#139, 64530c34), shipped the page and 17 cards; this one adds 54 cards, so 71 clips now have a study card.

## What changes

- **Cards:** 71 in all: 70 lectures of @dai.1387 and 1 practical-class clip of a classmate's channel.
  - Every card passed the summary gates and was read by hand. An independent review then found the problems listed under Review, and all of them are fixed here.
  - 5 clips have our own copy, with a skip list that saves 39 minutes in all.
- **Classmates' channels:** the 8 channels whose clips sit in Dai's "by student" playlists are listed in `src/data/video-channels.js`, by id only. The main session decided this on 2 Nov from public metadata, with Palm's agreement for the cohort. The evidence for each channel is in the lane ledger.
  - Most of their 40 clips are short practical demos with little speech. 25 had too little to summarise, and 1 is in this batch.
  - A US clinic's video that sits in the same playlist stays out.
- **Chapter titles:** a title is a slide's heading, read whole.
  - A heading that runs onto a second line is completed from the slide's text ("Clinical examination of the" becomes "Clinical examination of the penis/prepuce").
  - A heading set in spaced capitals reads as words ("Issue 2: Role of veterinarians").
  - A stretch with no slide has no title, and its line says what it teaches. Before, such a stretch was titled by two words it heard ("หวัด, ไข้"), which was 446 of 959 chapters.
  - A date, the faculty's name or a thank-you slide is no title. The study panel shows an untitled chapter's line as its text.
- **What a PDF does to a deck's text** is undone in titles and the index: Thai sara am split off ("การจ าแนก"), a degree sign read as "o" ("20 oซ"), an exponent set inline ("8 x 106").
- **One fact once:** an emphasis or remember item that a key point beside it already says leaves the card, and the key point is marked stressed.
- **Voice rules:**
  - Study advice ("ต้องจำ", "ท่องจำ"), course logistics (reports, homework, credits, grading, roll call, group sizes, "นักศึกษา") and set-up talk ("เตรียมระบบ", "ยังไม่มีเนื้อหา") are lint errors.
  - They match whole words by Intl's Thai dictionary, so "ต้องจำกัดอาหาร", "นักท่องเที่ยว" and "เคยออกอาการ" stay facts.
  - A one-liner that opens with "คลิป" or "บรรยาย" fails.
  - The card test now checks every line a student reads, chapter titles included.
- **Mapping:** "emergency" no longer matches a topic by itself, since every session of that course carries it.
- **Pipeline:**
  - A summary is written against one analysis. When an analysis is redone (a Scribe transcript or new silence arrives), the one the summary was written from is kept as `analysis.basis.json`. The registry pairs chapter lines with their chapters by start time and holds a card whose lines no longer find their chapters.
  - A held summary over a redone analysis is drafted afresh. A redone analysis replaces the transcript students read in R2.
  - An ElevenLabs Scribe transcript (`video.scribe.json3`) is preferred over YouTube's captions, and a lone caption letter is no speech.
  - `--no-asr` uploads a copy with no captions, so Scribe can read its audio from R2.
  - `VIDEO_BATCH` adds a read-through batch to what is published, and refuses an empty file.

## Review

An independent review of the batch found these; each is fixed in this release:
- **A redone analysis would have put chapter lines under the wrong chapters** on three live cards (0gTn7Up9Vj0, Fqgp8DoPDws, pj_zkis2ziU), whose analyses were redone from Scribe transcripts. Their bases were rebuilt from the captions they were summarised from. Each rebuild matches the published chapters exactly, and the three cards keep every line.
- **`VIDEO_BATCH` replaced the published set** instead of adding to it; a file of one week's clips would have removed every other card.
- **Wrong facts on cards,** each checked against the recording:
  - corneal sutures are 8-0 to 10-0 (the captions heard 80, 90, 100);
  - the H0 of a one-sample Z-test is about the population mean;
  - the extinct Thai deer is สมัน;
  - "ห้ามดลูก" was a slip for หา;
  - the purebred cat predisposed to diabetes is the Burmese, not the Siamese;
  - an elephant-and-hippo dosing point now says what the lecture compared.
- **Logistics and set-up reached cards:** a Senior Project announcement after the lecture ended, a course's credits and grading, an afternoon-lab note, 19 minutes of broadcast set-up, and a one-liner that named itself. Removed, and now lint errors.
- **The lint held true facts,** matching "ต้องจำ" inside "ต้องจำกัด" and "ท่อง" inside "นักท่องเที่ยว". It now matches whole words.
- **Garbled titles:** letter-spaced headings, dates with stacked "(ต่อ)", Thai broken by the PDF text layer, lost superscripts, and an ophthalmology chapter titled "ปัสสาวะ".
- **The same fact twice:** the old check split Thai only at spaces. The review listed 14 cards; the 9 pairs that say the same thing in the same words are now one item. The other pairs share a topic but add a detail (a number, a technique), so they stay.
- **A key point at 2:12** pointed into twelve minutes of silence, through a stray caption letter. It now points to where the lecture says it (14:46).
- **One misplaced line found by hand:** on yacB9zJJpTA, chapter lines had slipped one chapter late. They are moved back, and the closing talk is no chapter.
