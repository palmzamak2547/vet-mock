# 5.161.0: library sources from outside Chula, zoonoses and One Health

PR #60, merged as `ef444c33`. A pilot for the zoonoses and One Health subjects (`docs/library/open-sources.md`).

- 31 sources, each with its licence and the evidence for it (`src/data/library-open-sources.js`,
  `src/lib/open-sources.js`): 20 from official bodies (WOAH, FAO, WHO, UNEP, the Department of Livestock Development,
  the Department of Disease Control and others), 8 peer-reviewed articles and 3 reference entries. Six whose licence allows it are hosted in
  the library and open in the reader; the rest are link cards that open the source site ("เปิดที่เว็บต้นทาง"). Works
  that are free to read but under copyright are links only.
- A card from outside Chula names its source, year, kind and licence on a line of its own.
