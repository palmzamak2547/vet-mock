# 5.249.0: the listener tunes the read-aloud voice (16 Nov 2026)

## What a student notices

- **The speed button on the read-aloud bar opens "ตั้งค่าเสียงอ่าน".** One small dialog, two taps from the bar to any
  setting: three named settings (ช้าและชัด 0.75x, ปกติ 1x, เร็ว ทบทวน 1.5x), speed 0.75x to 2x, tone (ทุ้มมาก to
  สูงมาก, five steps), which of the device's own voices reads Thai and which reads an English question, ลองฟัง with
  one fixed sentence, and คืนค่าเริ่มต้น. A reading in progress takes a new speed at once and a new tone or voice
  from the sentence it is on. The bar's height and the card do not move.
- **Speed keeps the pitch.** A stored clip plays faster or slower with its pitch kept (`preservesPitch` set
  explicitly); the device voice gets the speed as its rate.
- **The dialog says which voice reads here.** "ข้อที่ไม่มีเสียงอัดไว้ เครื่องนี้อ่านด้วยเสียงของเครื่อง (name)" or "...เสียงออนไลน์ ซึ่งปรับได้เฉพาะความเร็ว". The rule is one function (`readerSource` in `src/lib/tts.js`) used by the reader, this line and ลองฟัง: the device voice reads when it is a good one, when the student chose it, when they changed the tone, or when the cloud voice refused them. So ลองฟัง sounds like the reading, and a tone set is a tone heard. A change made while a question is being read follows the same rule: when it moves the reading between the device voice and the online voice, the rest of the question moves too and the sentence being read starts again, so the line, ลองฟัง and the reading never disagree.
- **ลองฟัง never plays over a reading.** It pauses the reading through the reader's own pause (the bar shows paused), from the bar, the profile or search alike, and a reading that starts stops the sample. A sample stopped, or a dialog closed, while the device is still listing its voices is never heard, and a sample that has ended leaves nothing for the reading to cancel.
- **A late voice list.** The dialog reads the device's voices again on `voiceschanged` and says "no Thai voice" only once the device has listed its voices.
- **A chosen voice that does not speak** (in a timed paper too, where the dialog cannot be opened) gives way at once to the device's own best voice, then the cloud voice, never silence.
- **Tone applies to the device voice.** Shifting a stored clip's pitch on the device without changing its speed
  would need a pitch shifter that costs the clip its quality and its lock-screen playback, so the dialog says
  plainly that tone changes the device voice and recorded clips change speed only.
- **No credit is spent.** A setting change asks no voice at all; ลองฟัง uses the device voice, or the free cloud
  voice where that is what reads on this device; never a paid voice. A device with no Thai voice is told how to add one.
- **Kept on this device** in the voice settings' own local key (`vmx-tts-prefs`); nothing goes through sync. The
  speed picked before this release carries over. The same dialog opens from the profile and from search; its old
  pause sliders set a reader that no longer reads and are gone.
- **Timed paper unchanged.** Inside a timed exam the speed button steps the speed as before and no dialog opens
  over the running clock.

## How it was checked

- `tests/unit/voice-prefs.test.mjs`: defaults, bounds, a value stored in the older shape, a refusing storage, a
  device with no Thai voice, a chosen voice the device no longer has, the sample's voice, speed and tone, no network.
- `tests/e2e/voice-settings.spec.js` (stubbed speech engine) and the updated `tests/e2e/read-aloud.spec.js` on
  Chromium desktop and mobile, Firefox, and WebKit mobile. The spec also changes the tone, and puts the settings back, while the online voice (stubbed, answering) or the device voice reads, and checks the voice and tone of every sentence after; `tests/unit/read-aloud-live.test.mjs` does the same with the reading paused and playing.
- Screenshots at 390 x 664, 820 x 1180, 1180 x 820 and 1280 x 800, light and dark.
- `npm run build`, `npm run perf:budget`, `npm run lint:data:all`, the unit suite.

Not checked on a real iPhone, iPad or Android phone.
