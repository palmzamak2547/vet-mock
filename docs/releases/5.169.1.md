# 5.169.1: right-aligned and centred Thai text lands in place on iPad

On Palm's iPad (Safari, 28 Oct), the printed exam timetable drew every right-aligned or centred Thai line starting at its anchor. The semester, the issue date and every room ran off the page. Playwright's WebKit does not reproduce the fault.

`card.text` in `src/lib/share-card/kit.js` now asks the canvas for left alignment only. It measures each line and works out the x for right-aligned and centred text itself, so the page relies on no engine's alignment. The masthead and the timetable's `textRight` measure their overhang the same way.
