# 5.226.0: doors between the VetMock sites, quieter screens, and the screen leftovers

Three branches parked on 11 Nov 2026 folded into one release. Each lane's own notes (what changed, its
measurements, its reviews and its open points) are kept as written in `docs/releases/5.226.0/`:
`family-seams.md`, `screen-leftovers.md`, `quiet-screens.md`.

## What a student notices

- **Doors between the sites** (`claude/family-seams`, head `fa181041`): VetMock, VCA, AI and Research name and link
  each other from one list (`src/lib/family/family.js`) in headers, footers, the landing page and the feature
  registry. A chosen theme (and the landing language, to a site that has one) travels with a followed link and
  applies to that visit only. A redirect sign-in returns to the page the student was on. VCA's wording says what
  stays in the browser and what is saved to the account (the totals a student shows on a profile). A VCA question
  has an address, `/d/<domain>/<no>`, with a link to the same question in VetMock. One sign-in for all four sites is
  designed only (`docs/FAMILY-SIGNIN.md`).
- **Screen leftovers** (`claude/screen-leftovers`, head `daa83ed2`): the search box shows at once while the search
  code downloads and keeps what is typed; `/wiki/<subject>` opens the index at that subject; the faculty search finds
  a Thai name spelled a little differently; the topic screen's reading counter says what it counts; a document that
  fails to open from search says so; a stored `null` no longer blanks the app (`storage.get` and `readStoredJson`).
- **Quieter screens** (`claude/quiet-screens`, head `d61c27b4`): a feature hint shows once ever; VetWiki folds each
  section's sources into one closed row and marks a lecture-evidence disagreement once, at its section; subject cards
  lose the grading chip (one closed row on the subject screen); topic cards say "เป็นข้อสอบเก่า P%" and keep only
  caveats; Home's update chip is news only, once a day, with no count; tuition shows on Home only in its last 14 days;
  the exam settings say the clock rule once. Five rules for this are in AGENTS.md, "Quiet screens".

- The lanes' own release number 5.214 was void (it went to another release); their comments and test names now say
  5.226. Their pending notes moved under `docs/releases/5.226.0/`; AGENTS.md points there.
- `regen:all` after the merges changed only the timestamps of `bank-registry.generated.js` and `q-counts.js`; both
  were restored. main's four screen guard tests (`text-control-font-floor`, `comma-spacing`, `inline-font-floor`,
