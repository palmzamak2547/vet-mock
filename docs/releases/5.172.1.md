# 5.172.1: read-aloud keeps one voice through a question

An independent review of #106 (5.169.3) found the gap. The browser cache of Edge clips
(`src/lib/tts-cache.js`, kept 30 days) keyed on `lang|rate|text` only. After the Thai voice moved
from Premwadee to Niwat, a question could fetch its stem fresh in Niwat and play options cached
in Premwadee, including every clip cached before 21 Oct.

- **Shared voice map.** The voice map moves to `src/lib/tts-voices.js`, shared by `/api/tts`
  (which re-exports `voiceFor`) and the Edge client.
- **Cache key.** `audioCacheKey` takes an optional `voice`, and the Edge client passes it. A voice
  change now retires the clips cached in the old voice by itself.
- **iApp.** iApp passes no voice, so its keys and cache are unchanged.
