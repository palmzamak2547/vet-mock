# 5.199.0: the slide an answer rests on, under the answer

Palm, 11 Nov 2026: show the real page of the lecture slide under each answer, with the lines that
state it highlighted, so a student can see the key is what was taught. Start with the Year-5 finals.
Design and pipeline: [ANSWER-EVIDENCE.md](../ANSWER-EVIDENCE.md).

## What a student sees

After an answer (practice, the written-answer reveal, the review list), under the reason: "จากสไลด์ที่เรียน",
the document's name and page, and the part of that page around the highlighted lines. A tap opens the
whole page; on a phone it opens magnified at the lines and is panned by dragging. A timed paper reveals
nothing in session, as before. A visitor the Chula wall does not serve sees the name and page, no image.

## How it was checked

- **The pipeline's own yardstick.** Where the bank already cites a 2569 library page, the pick landed
  on that page or the one beside it for 54 of 55 Equine Medicine questions and 28 of 33 Zoonoses ones.
- **The last reading.** All 450 proposed slides of these five subjects were drawn as the student sees
  them and read by an independent checker: 379 right, 64 weak (right topic, the lines do not show the
  key by themselves), 1 wrong, 5 where the slide disagrees with the bank's key, 1 unreadable. Only the
  379 are in the index. An earlier sample of 252 from all ten subjects, before this step existed, had
  come back 231 right, 17 weak, 3 wrong, 1 key in doubt: that is why nothing is published without it.
- **By hand.** 15 panels of One Health and Equine Medicine, the 8 `conflict` rows, and the card itself
  in a browser at 1024 px and 375 px, light slides and a dark one, the lightbox, a refused pass, a
  failed image.
- **Tests.** `tests/unit/answer-evidence.test.mjs` (12), `tests/e2e/answer-evidence.spec.js` (3, run on
  chromium desktop and mobile), `npm run lint:evidence`.

## Two reviews before it shipped

The question card is the exam flow, so the diff was read twice by reviewers told to break it.
What they found, all fixed in this release:

- With the wall's switch off the gate lets everyone through, and the first draft of
  `/api/evidence-pass` would then have handed the image address to any visitor. It now answers
  members only whatever the switch says, and its answer is never cached.
- The frame was drawn before the address was known, so for a visitor the wall refused it appeared and
  then vanished, moving the next button; and such a visitor caused one request per answer. The frame
  is now drawn only when the address is already known as the answer appears, a refusal is remembered
  for five minutes, and a failed image keeps its box with a line in it.
- A verdict from the last reading was recorded against whatever pick the registry held at that
  moment. It is now recorded against the pick the sheet showed.
- The lightbox sat inside the verdict panel, a live region: it is rendered in `<body>`.
- The review list asked for every image at once: they load as they come into view.
- A browser that cannot shape a box by a ratio (iOS 14) shows the citation line and no image.
- A removed question made `lint:evidence` fail for whoever removed it: it is counted instead.
