# 5.273.0: Atlas names that never cross, readable chart dates, and smaller header and plan fixes

## What a student notices

1. **Atlas names no longer sit on each other.** On the visible dog, the names of organs that lie close
   together (stomach, liver, kidneys, intestines) were printed over one another. Each name now takes the
   first free place: above its point, then below, right or left of it. The names of a crowded cluster go
   out from the middle of the cluster, each along its own point's direction, with a thin line back to a
   dot on the point. Every name stays text; none is replaced by a bare dot. Label sizes are measured again
   when the stage resizes or the web font arrives, so the spacing follows the widths on screen. The phone
   view (a stage under 480 px) keeps its dots, as before.
2. **The learning-curve dates on Progress are readable.** The dates sat inside the scaled chart at about
   9 px and the last two days were printed over each other. They are now page text at 14 px under the
   chart: the first day, the last day and at most two between, never two neighbours.
3. **Tapping the theme button no longer scrolls a phone's page.** Focus moves into the theme panel only
   when it is opened from the keyboard.
4. **Header and Home details.** A short name in the account pill sits in the middle of its fixed slot
   instead of leaving a gap before the arrow, and a long name that is cut shows whole on pointing at it.
   A plan step drawn as an arrow is read by a screen reader as its word ("เริ่มฝึก"), not as an arrow.
   The year picker's count beside "ดูชั้นปีอื่น" is no longer faded to 3.3:1 in the dark scheme. Home
   reads the year's question bank as a store, so the day plan is drawn as soon as the bank lands.
5. **Easier to tap and read.** The confidence chips on the landing page (เดา, ไม่แน่ใจ, มั่นใจ), the
   option text boxes and the difficulty slider on Contribute are 44 px tall (they were 40, 41 and 16 px).
   The circled "ฟรี" on the dark landing card was 2.5:1 and is now the lamp gold.
