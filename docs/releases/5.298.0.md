# 5.298.0: กดเมนูบนหน้าแนะนำ แล้วหน้าเลื่อนไปถึงหัวข้อที่กด

## สิ่งที่นิสิตเห็น

- **เมนูบนหน้าแนะนำ:** กดหัวข้อในเมนูด้านบนของหน้าแนะนำ หน้าจะเลื่อนไปจนถึงหัวข้อนั้น
  เดิมมีจังหวะที่หน้าหยุดกลางทางหรือไม่ขยับเลย ต้องกดซ้ำ พบในการทดสอบอัตโนมัติบน WebKit
  ซึ่งเป็นตัวที่ Safari ใช้แสดงหน้าเว็บ

## What changed

- `src/styles-landing.css`: scroll anchoring is off while the landing shows
  (`html.lp-active { overflow-anchor: none; }`). In WebKit, when the layout above the screen shifts while
  a smooth scroll is in flight, the engine corrects the scroll position and that correction stops the
  scroll where it is. Chromium and Firefox finish the scroll at the destination worked out when it was
  asked for. The class is on the root only while the landing shows, so no other page changes.
- `tests/e2e/landing-navigation.spec.js`: each tap on the nav must bring its section to rest under the
  nav (its top within 2 px of its scroll margin, read twice 120 ms apart) instead of only being on
  screen, and the case reads the rule itself. The two cases at desktop width run at a scale of 1: at
  the phone projects' 3x scale the nav case took 35 s on WebKit against 8 s.
- `tests/e2e/learner-flow.spec.js`: the 320x812 results case waits for the personal best instead of
  reading it at once. The results view records it in an effect, which can run after the card is on
  screen.
- `tests/e2e/voice-settings.spec.js`: the case that opens the voice settings reads the card's place once
  the motion that carries a new question in has ended. A new question slides in from 8 px to the right
  for 160 ms, and the case could read the card inside that slide.

## Checks

- The nav case on WebKit with six workers, before the change: 10 of 40 runs failed (the page at rest at
  the earlier section, stopped part of the way, or 7 to 28 px off). With anchoring turned off by the
  test, 0 of 40 in the same run. On the build with the rule, the whole landing spec passed 120 of 120
  (40 rounds of its three cases).
- A bare page, 24 px of growth above the screen during a smooth scroll: WebKit stopped short in 12 of 15
  runs, Chromium and Firefox finished in 10 of 10 each, WebKit with `overflow-anchor: none` finished in
  10 of 10.
- Not tested: Safari itself. Every WebKit number here is from the test runner's WebKit build. Which
  element of the landing sets the correction off was not identified; its sections kept their heights in
  18 measured runs. The other pages that use a smooth scroll were not measured.
- Personal best: a probe read the key when the results card appeared. In 3 of 10 runs it was still empty
  and was written 24 to 27 ms later. In CI on 17 Nov 2026 the read was empty on both attempts of one run
  on WebKit. With the wait the case passed 30 of 30, 15 each on Chromium and WebKit.
- Voice settings: in CI on 17 Nov 2026 the case failed once on mobile Chromium and passed on its retry,
  the card read 1.5 px short of its place. A probe saw the card 8 px to the right when it first exists
  and at rest 161 to 173 ms later (three runs). With the wait started as the card appears, the card was
  at rest afterwards in 20 of 20 runs, 4 of which started inside the slide. The case passed 20 of 20 on
  mobile Chromium and the file 28 of 28 on the four browsers.
- Both spec files on the four browsers with three workers: 71 of 72 passed. The one that failed,
  `learner-flow.spec.js:81` on Firefox (the first double click moved two questions), passed 10 of 10
  when run alone and is not touched here. A first run with six workers is not counted: 13 cases ran out
  of time across the four browsers (page loads of 30 s and more), and the double key press of that same
  Firefox case moved two questions.
- Unit suite 5,168 pass, 0 fail; `lint:data` clean (72 steps); `lint:dist` clean; size budgets met
  (first-load JS 232,823 B of 240,800 B, first-load CSS 50,186 B of 52,200 B).
