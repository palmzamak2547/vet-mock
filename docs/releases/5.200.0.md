# 5.200.0: Milk Hygiene and Meat Inspection joins the slides under the answers

The sixth subject of [5.199.0](5.199.0.md)'s feature, and the first of the finals' second week
(paper on 30 Nov). Data only: one more generated index, no code. Design and pipeline:
[ANSWER-EVIDENCE.md](../ANSWER-EVIDENCE.md).

## What is in it

101 questions of the Milk & Meat final, each on a page of a 2569 library deck (85 pages of 5 decks:
cleaning and disinfectants, milk processing, microbiology of meat, and the two meat inspection decks).
With the first five subjects that makes 480 questions in six subjects.

## How it was checked

- **The last reading.** All 102 proposed slides were drawn as the student sees them and read by an
  independent checker in two halves: 100 right, 2 weak.
- **By hand.** 24 of those panels (8 sheets), chosen for risk: the one `conflict` row, the two rows
  whose key holds a number the text layer did not print, the picture page, a true/false key, a
  "which is not correct" stem, and two the checker had flagged as close. Every `ok` held. One `weak`
  was overruled: the checker said the key's row of a table was outside the highlight, and it is inside
  it (`milk-meat-hygiene:214002`). So 101 are in the index and 1 stays out.
- **Images.** The 85 pages answer `200 image/webp` from `slides.vetmock.com`.
- **Tests.** `npm run lint:evidence` (480 slides in 6 subjects), `tests/unit/answer-evidence.test.mjs`.

## The provider's balance (11 Nov, about 02:00)

The pipeline's first full run spent the DeepSeek account to zero. Production's features call the same
account with another key, so from that moment they would answer from their second provider. Production's
runtime log shows no model request between the moment the balance ran out and 02:33, so no student met
it in that window. Both pipeline scripts now read the balance before they pay for anything, stop under a
floor (40 CNY by default) and end on the first refusal; this went out with 5.199.0. The remaining
subjects wait for a top-up.
