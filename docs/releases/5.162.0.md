# 5.162.0: shared links reach their page, no cookie card over the hero question

Round two of the vetmock.com journeys (`docs/ux/journeys.md`, items 4 and 7).

- **Shared links keep their destination.** A link to a year-scoped page (`/app/study`, `/app/review`,
  `/app/progress`, `/app/leaderboard`) opened before any year was picked shows the front door
  (landing, year, phase), and the student then landed on Home instead of the page the link named.
  The front door now leaves that page in sessionStorage (`rememberFrontDoorReturn`,
  src/lib/phase-return.js; these four views only, taken once), and the phase pick (or the year pick
  for years without one) opens it. The topic screen's own phase return still wins, and "หน้าแรก" on
  the phase picker forgets the link. `tests/unit/front-door-return.test.mjs`.
- **The cookie card waits for the first scroll.** On a 390x844 phone it covered y 692-836 at first
  paint (measured on vetmock.com, 22 Oct), over the hero question the landing asks the visitor to try.
  Consent stays 'ask' until a choice, and nothing optional runs before one, so asking after the first
  scroll costs nothing; a visitor who never scrolls is never tracked. `connected-study.spec.js`
  asserts the card is absent at first paint and appears after a scroll, still compact.
