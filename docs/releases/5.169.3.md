# 5.169.3: Thai read-aloud in Edge's Niwat voice

5.168.2 brought English back. Thai still answered 502 in production, with "Stream closed before
the synthesis completed (no turn.end received)". The real handler reproduced it locally, and
then the library alone did too. Premwadee closes the socket on question-length Thai about 2.7 s
in, so a long question waited and then read in the device voice. Edge lists two Thai voices, and
Niwat read every stem.

| Voice | Two-sentence stem | One-sentence stem | Short line |
|---|---|---|---|
| th-TH-PremwadeeNeural | 3 of 12 (mp3 48k 0/3, mp3 96k 0/3, opus 1/3, mp3 48k 2/3) | 10 of 12 | 3 of 3 |
| th-TH-NiwatNeural | 6 of 6 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 |

The Thai voice is now male. The alternatives were to keep Premwadee, so that most long Thai
questions fall to the device voice after a wait, or to top up iApp, which the client tries
first for Thai.

## Follow-up

An independent review found that the browser's clip cache keyed on lang, rate and text only. A
question could therefore play a fresh Niwat stem with Premwadee options cached before the
switch. 5.172.1 (#117) keys the cache on the voice.
