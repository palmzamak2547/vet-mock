Then: "ข้อมูล syllabus บางทีก็เปลี่ยนได้ด้วย อย่าเชื่อทั้งหมด แต่ดูความmakr sense ของสิ่งต่างๆที่ผมส่งให้และเอามาmatchกันครับ".

Numbered at merge time (the ledger's rule of 2 Nov): drafted as 5.182.0 and 5.183.0; main held 5.185.0 and #139 was merging as 5.186.0.

What a student notices:
- **ประมวลรายวิชาและตารางสอน ปี 5 (11)** on the schedule page (`/app/schedule`, Year 5): each 2569-1 course with two buttons.
- **ประมวลรายวิชา** draws the course on one A4 page:
  - when and where it meets, and its coordinator;
  - for each paper, its date, time and room from the faculty's exam table, the dates of the classes it covers, and which lecturer taught which sessions;
  - the score split where the syllabus states one, per lecturer where it gives that, or that it states none.
- **ตารางสอน** lists every class on the day it actually ran, from the cohort's lecture recordings (listed to 27 Oct) and the class LINE group's announcements (to 28 Oct), with its lecturers, the two papers in their place, and a gold or sage bar for the paper the class sits on:
  - a class that ran on another day than the course document planned reads "เดิม <the planned days>";
  - a past class with no recording or announcement showing it ran reads "ตามแผน", and one that did not run on its day with no word yet of when reads "ยังไม่ทราบวัน";
  - a note says once that dates after 28 Oct are the plan;
  - announcements (online, moved, cancelled, not examined) sit under the class with the day they were posted, and a recording whose title differs from the plan is quoted.
- A page whose Thai sets wider on some device adds a page instead of running text into the notes: the timetable takes as many pages as it needs, the midterm's classes and the final's each from a fresh page when that costs no page; the syllabus carries its later lecturer groups and the score split to a second page, inside a paper when it must. On the engines checked, every syllabus is one page. A trial layout measures at the scale its page is drawn at, so what fits in the trial fits on the page.
- Both download as an A4 PDF or save as a picture, through the exam timetable's sheet.

Evidence: `docs/SYLLABUS-2569-1-Y5.md` (sources, the term as it ran, what each paper covers, and the comparison with every topic's `examScope`). Data: `src/data/course-syllabi.js`.

## Checks

- Unit: `tests/unit/course-syllabi.test.mjs` 13/13. It checks that every course of the programme is present under its code, and that every session is dated in the term, in order, outside the midterm week, with its lecturers and real topic ids. A `planned` day must be another day, and no class is `held` after the evidence ends. Scores add up, and no topic is scoped to the paper its sessions are not on. Each paper row matches the faculty table. The only class taught across the midterm from its plan is the stallion exam, and it sits on the final. Every term has its calendar and evidence date. A status breaks between dates and keeps its spaces. A two-page document is a two-page PDF. After merging main at 9b8e7c51, the touched suites passed 67/67 (with schedule-study-launch, exam-timetable, share-card-kit, no-lookbehind, latest-changelog, changelog-voice and project-docs).
- Full unit suite, before the review fixes: 3,584 tests, 3,576 pass, 2 fail. One failure was a Windows temp-folder lock in `gate-native-artifacts` (EPERM on cleanup). The other was a regex lookbehind in the first status splitter, which `no-lookbehind` caught and which is gone.
- Layout: all 22 documents drawn at the app's own 2x in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox with the fonts loaded. Every page is clean, every syllabus is one page, and the timetables run one or two pages. With every measured width set 10 percent wider (Firefox at 1.5x, Chromium at 2x), every page is still clean: the Epidemiology timetable takes three pages, and the Aquatic and Zoonoses syllabi take two.
- CI on 82fe56c9 found Firefox setting two timetables a few pixels past their notes. The cause was that trial layouts measured at 1x while the page was drawn at 2x, and Firefox's Thai ink ascent differs by about 0.1 px between them. Trials now measure at the draw scale.
- e2e `course-docs.spec.js` on the dev server: 15/15 on firefox-desktop and on chromium-desktop before the review fixes. Later local runs timed out loading pages (page.goto past 45 s) while this machine carried other lanes, so CI's four browsers are the gate.
- One independent review, which found 17 issues; all are fixed except two. The page header still reads `SEMESTER`'s exam-table name, which does not matter while only the current term's documents are listed. The trials are still one synchronous block, though they no longer paint. Its evidence findings were each checked against the class LINE export and the recording manifest before they were applied.

## Not covered

- Changes after 28 Oct (the class LINE export ends there; recordings are listed to 27 Oct). The pages say so.
- Real iPhone and iPad hardware: checked in Playwright's WebKit only. Wider Thai is covered by the extra page, tested by measuring every width 6 and 10 percent wide.
- A page drawn before its fonts load (offline with an empty cache) uses the fallback faces; the extra page keeps it inside A4, and the sheet's layout check reports anything else.
