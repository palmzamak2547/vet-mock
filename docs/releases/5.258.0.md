# 5.258.0: Equine Medicine and One Health finals, the marked points the earlier passes left

Equine Medicine sits its final on 24 Nov 2026 and One Health on 25 Nov. Releases 5.234.0 (Equine Medicine) and
5.246.0 (One Health) did the finals gap pass for each paper and left a list of held and unread sources. This
release works through that list. A highlight, an underline, a star or red pen on a senior's own copy of a deck is
read as the mark of an examined point.

  question asked yet, each checked against this year's deck:

    head and control tail in recovery (218501), quick stand in one time (218502), first-intention healing in ponies

- **One Health: 26 new items tagged อิงแนวข้อสอบ** (218600-218625), from Vet 84's highlights in their 2567 decks,
  only where this year's deck still teaches the point and no row asks it: risk estimation in import risk
  assessment (1); farm-to-fork contamination, prevention at the source and slaughter hygiene (3); why GMOs are made
  and the 5 percent labelling threshold (2); AMR as English true/false (5); livestock waste management (15).
- **Dental star key corrected** (207524, and 207587 which keyed the same point). Both keyed the dental star as
  tertiary dentin. This year's dentistry deck (p. 3) prints that the pulp cavity forms the dental star when filled
  with secondary dentin. The published study of the dental star (Muylle, Simoens and Lauwers 2002, Equine Vet J
  34:230-234, doi:10.2746/042516402776186038) found "a central core of tertiary dentine, an intermediate ring of
  pale secondary dentine" with a dark outer rim of secondary dentine. The keyed option now reads secondary dentin
  filling the pulp cavity with a core of tertiary dentin, and both explanations say which layer is the dark brown
  one and why it stains (wide, dense tubules take up food pigment). The earlier explanation's claim that silica
  from grass gives the colour is removed: the study does not support it. The source pointers are unchanged; the
  deck page and the study are added to `verified`.

No screen changes. Three aligned origins are filed in `src/data/exam-origins.js`, and five of the seniors'
annotated decks (Vet 84's infectious, ophthalmology, lameness, diagnostic analgesia and 2024 AMR copies) are listed
in `src/data/source-docs.js`, so every new row cites the marked page in `sourcePages` next to this year's page.

## Midterm and final kept apart

- Every new item sits in a topic `topicOnPaper` serves only on the final (`equine-infectious`, `equine-pythium`,
  `equine-ophth`, `equine-lameness`, `equine-anesthesia`, `equine-surgery-wound`; the eight One Health final
  topics), and each fact is on a deck page of a session after this term's midterm. `curriculum.js` and
  `exam-scope.js` are untouched.
- A marked point was held when this term also teaches its fact before the midterm: the vaccine schedules of
  tetanus, influenza with EHV, rabies and Japanese encephalitis (the 13 Aug general practice deck, pp. 40-43);
  surra and piroplasmosis (the 10 Sep parasites deck); guttural pouch mycosis (the 17 Sep respiratory deck); the
  forms of EHV (named on the 13 Aug vaccine slide); and in One Health the international bodies and the
  multidisciplinary team (midterm topics `oh-global-network` and `oh-concept`).
- New Equine rows carry no `examScope` (the topic decides, as in 5.234.0); new One Health rows carry
  `examScope: 'final'` (as in 5.246.0).
