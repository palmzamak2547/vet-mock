# 5.247.0: Zoonoses final, questions from the points earlier cohorts marked

The Zoonoses final is on 26 Nov 2026. Every item earlier cohorts recorded on this term's five final topics was
already accounted for: 231 were in the bank before October, 93 were converted one for one in 5.175.0, 53 are
duplicates and 16 are held with a reason. What was left were the points the seniors marked in their copies of the
decks and in their summaries. This release turns the marked points the bank did not yet ask into 64 questions.

## What changed

- **64 exam-aligned questions** in a new bank, `questions-y5-zoonoses-final-marked.js` (ids 216800-216863):
  `zoo-bacterial` 42, `zoo-prion` 9, `zoo-epi-approach` 5, `zoo-mycoses` 4, `zoo-eid-wildlife` 4 (true or false,
  the format of the wildlife part Vet 83 recorded). Each is written from a point with an exam-word mark (a star,
  asterisk or "!!!", or a legend saying a cohort's paper had it) that no bank row asked, and keys on the 2569 deck
  page named in `source`; `verified` lists every mark with its file, page and cohort. `sourceType` is
  `exam-aligned`, every row carries อิงแนวข้อสอบ, and no row carries `examScope`, so the topic decides.
- **Two origins** filed as `aligned` in `exam-origins.js`: "Zoonoses จุดที่รุ่นพี่มาร์คว่าเคยออก" (15 rows, a mark
  whose legend ties it to a cohort's paper: Vet 84's "ออกสอบ 84" stars, the Vet 83 notebook's stars, Vet 85's
  post-exam notes) and "Zoonoses จุดที่รุ่นพี่มาร์คในสไลด์หรือสรุป" (49 rows). Neither names a paper.
- **Four documents** in `source-docs.js` (the 2569 prion and epidemiology handouts, the Vet 84 copies of the 2567
  bacterial and mycoses decks) and a second wording for the Vet 80 summary file; 236 structured page cites.
- An exact `manualChunks` rule for the new bank in `vite.config.js`.

## This term's midterm and final are kept apart

- The five topics served on the final (`zoo-mycoses` 2 Oct, `zoo-bacterial` 30 Oct and 6 Nov, `zoo-prion` and
  `zoo-epi-approach` 13 Nov, `zoo-eid-wildlife` 20 Nov) all ran after the midterm of 25 Sep, and `topicOnPaper`
  serves all five on the final. `zoo-epi-approach` and `zoo-eid-wildlife` are scoped `both`; neither had a session
  before the midterm. `curriculum.js` and `exam-scope.js` are untouched.
- The 28 rows whose senior label says midterm (Vet 85's mycoses recall, an undated epidemiology recall, Vet 83's
  prion item) sit on topics this term teaches after the midterm, by the session data and the recordings.
- A marked point whose fact is also taught in a class before the midterm was held, with both topics named: four
  anthrax basics (also in the 4 Sep field outbreak deck) and two Q fever facts (also in the 10 Sep rickettsia deck).
