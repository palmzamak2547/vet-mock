# 5.182.0: an easy syllabus and the teaching timetable for every Year-5 course

Palm, 1 Nov 2026: "นำcourse syllabus แต่ละรายวิชามาmapก็ได้นะ จะแยกกลางภาคกับปลายภาคแม่นขึ้น พยายามหาsyllabus ให้เจอ และเอามาทำใหม่ให้คนกดดาวน์โหลดได้ด้วยเหมือนตารางสอบก็ดีในฉบับที่เข้าใจง่าย และก็ตารางสอนด้วย"

What a student notices:
- **ประมวลรายวิชาและตารางสอน ปี 5 (11)** on the schedule page (`/app/schedule`, Year 5): each 2569-1 course with two buttons.
- **ประมวลรายวิชา** draws the course on one A4 page:
  - when and where it meets, and its coordinator;
  - for each paper, its date, time and room from the faculty's exam table, what it covers (dates and number of classes), and which lecturer taught which sessions;
  - the score split the syllabus states, per lecturer where it gives one, or that it states none.
- **ตารางสอน** lists every session by date with its lecturers, the two papers in their place, and a gold or sage bar for the paper the session sits on. Sessions the class LINE group announced as moved, cancelled or not examined say so beside them, with the day it was posted. A timetable longer than one page prints on two, split after the midterm.
- Both download as an A4 PDF or save as a picture, through the exam timetable's sheet.

Evidence: `docs/SYLLABUS-2569-1-Y5.md` (sources, what each paper covers, and the comparison with every topic's `examScope`). Data: `src/data/course-syllabi.js`.

## Checks

- Unit: `tests/unit/course-syllabi.test.mjs` 10/10 (every course of the programme present under its code; every session dated in the term, ordered, outside the midterm week, naming its lecturers and real topic ids; scores add up; no topic scoped to the paper its sessions are not on; each paper row matches the faculty table; a two-page document is a two-page PDF).
- Layout, all 22 documents (11 syllabi, 11 timetables) drawn in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox with the fonts loaded: every page clean (nothing off the page, cut or overlapping, no Thai under 22 px, no name broken inside a surname).
- e2e `tests/e2e/course-docs.spec.js` on the dev server: 28/28 on chromium-desktop and webkit-mobile (each course's two documents clean, the one-page and two-page PDFs).
- UI on iPhone 13 and iPad (WebKit), Pixel 7 (Chromium) and desktop: no horizontal overflow, the sheet's page clean.
