# 5.203.0: one registry of every picture, measured, and pictures that keep their place

Palm, 11 Nov 2026: "ทำระบบจัดเก็บรูป วิดีโอ แล้วนำออกมาใช้ได้อย่างรวดเร็วแม่นยำ และ polish ขั้นสุดในทุกจุดเลย
และไม่มีบัคเลย". This is release one of that: every picture a question or a note shows is recorded and
measured, a lint holds the set clean, and no picture moves the page while it loads. Guide:
[MEDIA.md](../MEDIA.md). Video and a lookup across media come next and are listed at the end.

## What a student sees

- A question's figure, an explanation's figure and a note's deck figures have their frame from the
  first paint. The stem, the options and the paragraphs below stay where they are when the file arrives.
- Year-2 notes show figures, not slide templates: 292 pictures the decks repeat on nearly every page
  are gone (backgrounds and banners, and two biological pictures, below), 77 shapes the slides draw
  (dark backdrops, arrows, bars) are gone, and a picture the lecturer put on several slides shows once
  per section. A deck figure is never drawn wider than its own pixels or taller than 80% of the screen.
- A picture that fails to load keeps its frame, with its description and "ลองโหลดอีกครั้ง"; the stem and
  the options do not move. "ขยายภาพ" fits the picture to the screen, and the exam's keys do nothing
  while it is open.
- Question images load faster: 20 heavy ones are 4.6 MB lighter together at the same pixel size.
- The "ขยายภาพ" hint is a strip under the picture instead of a chip over its corner.
- The review card shows its figure as the practice card does: zoomable, with caption and credit, and a
  screen reader hears the question's own Thai description.

## What is in it

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Deck figures on disk (`public/figures/<subject>/`) | 2,218 files, 150.4 MB | 1,833 files, 139.9 MB |
| Slide-figure map | 976 sections, 2,537 entries | 906 sections, 2,127 entries |
| Question images (`public/figures/questions/`, `public/images/`) | 141 files, 9.9 MB (20 PNG, 73 JPEG, 48 WebP) | 135 files, 5.3 MB (1 PNG, 72 JPEG, 62 WebP) |
| Heaviest question image | 756 KB | 179 KB |
| Byte-identical groups among those pictures | 92 (469 files) | 0 |
| Pictures with a recorded size | 0 | 1,969 |

- **The registry.** `scripts/media-registry.mjs` writes `src/data/media-registry.generated.json` (path,
  width, height, bytes, hash, kind, what shows it) and two size modules for the browser. `lint:media`
  runs in `lint:data` and fails on a missing picture (`explainImage` included, which nothing checked
  before), an unreferenced file, byte-identical files of one kind, a question image over 200 KB, a
  figure file named after its answer, a figure in the wrong folder and a stale registry.
- **Repeated pictures.** Every one of the 90 groups of byte-identical deck figures was looked at. The 9
  groups with four or more copies in one deck are pictures the deck repeats on its own pages (59, 56,
  50, 39, 34, 30, 15, 5 and 4 copies). Seven are backgrounds and banners (197 files). Two are biological
  pictures the cytology deck draws on nearly every page and its note never mentions: a skin section
  stained H&E (`histo--cytology/p10-0`, 56 copies) and a cell drawing without labels (`p10-1`, 39
  copies). Those two were not checked against the deck, which is not on the build machine; to bring
  one copy back, restore it from git history and place it with a `REKEYS` row (the commands are above
  `KEEP_REPEATED` in `scripts/lib/slide-figure-placements.mjs`), then `npm run regen:media`;
  the 81 groups with two or three were slide content. The lead this work started from said three
  repeats: that would have removed five real figures (a labelled cell, an agar plate, the inoculation
  routes of an egg, a skin section, trypanosomes in blood). The rule is four, in
  `scripts/lib/slide-figure-clean.mjs`; 88 copies of the 81 real pictures went, two empty dark frames
  were dropped by name, and four files the map had already dropped left the disk. 70 sections in six
  decks showed nothing but template art and now show no figure.
- **Heavy images.** 19 PNGs and one JPEG re-encoded as WebP at the same pixel size (quality 86; 38.9 to
  44.8 dB against the originals, flattened on white and on the lightbox's dark). One 10 KB pictogram
  stays a PNG, which is smaller.
- **The size limit, 200 KB.** The WebP figures cut from decks top out at 110 KB and the JPEG photographs
  at 167 KB; every file over the line was a PNG screenshot of a photograph or one 221 KB JPEG.
- **No layout shift.** `ZoomableImage` and `SlideFigures` give each `<img>` its width and height. The
  question figures' sizes are a 1.7 KB chunk that arrives with the question banks; the deck figures'
  sizes (11.9 KB) load beside the slide-figure map (26.7 KB).

## Bugs found on the way, fixed here

- **Seven figure files were named after their answer**: `repro-us/q7-estrus.png` above the key "Estrus
  stage", `q4-ceh`, `q5-bloodvessel`, `q6-fluid`, `q10-hydrocephalus`, `eqmed-intro-face-star`,
  `eqmed-dent-buccal-ulcer`. Renamed; `lint:media` now fails on a name that carries a word only the key
  has (`tests/unit/media-registry.test.mjs` holds the seven).
- **The review card** announced "Question 822 image, com3/..." for `image`, a stock phrase for
  `imagePath`, and printed neither caption nor credit. It now shows the figure as the practice card
  does (`tests/unit/sr-session-image-url.test.mjs`, four tests that fail on the old view).
- **The zoom hint covered the picture**: on a phone it hid the "6 feet" mark of zoonoses 104006, a
  question that asks which distance the figure labels.
- **YouTube was framed from www.youtube.com**, where `VIDEO-LIBRARY.md` section 5 says
  youtube-nocookie.com and `vercel.json` has always allowed it. The doc was right; the code follows it.
  That is the documented host and nothing more. The IFrame API script still loads from
  www.youtube.com and may set youtube.com cookies before any frame loads, so the frame's host by
  itself keeps nothing from YouTube, and nothing in this release says it does.
- **`explainImage` was checked by nothing**: a missing file would have shown a broken figure after the
  answer.
- Two sets of four questions each stored one picture under four names (equine-medicine 209595-98,
  milk-meat-hygiene 212014-17).

## Leads that turned out wrong

- "Three or more repeats in a deck is furniture": five real figures repeat exactly three times. Four.
- `public/brand/google-docs.svg` is not unreferenced: the `ai/` app's build copies it
  (`ai/build/shared-assets.mjs`) and fails without it. Kept.
- `public/brand/dropbox.svg` is unreferenced by code, but it is the mark for the Dropbox card release
  5.153.0 lists as an owner step, and `public/brand/ATTRIBUTION.md` lists it. Kept; the owner decides.
- The six deck figures the map did not list were not strays: they were the `drop` rows of the
  extractor's own table, which required the files to stay. The table no longer does, and they are gone.

Removed as proven unreferenced: `public/motion/assets/mochi-{happy,sleep,wave}.webp` (animated, 1.3 MB;
no source, style sheet, manifest, script or test names them and nothing builds such a name).
