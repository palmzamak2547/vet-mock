## The final shows only this year's final content (Palm, 10 Nov)

Every year-5 topic the final mode shows was checked against the 2569 syllabi in `src/data/course-syllabi.js`
(teaching date against that subject's own midterm date). Seven subjects were already right; two were not.

- **Swine:** `swine-pornchalit-set` was `both`, so 64 rows taught before the midterm (bacterial respiratory and
  locomotor) sat in the final. It is now `midterm`; the two rows that ask reproductive disease (8352 vaginal
  discharge in sows, 8355 MMA signs) moved to `swine-repro`.
- **Equine Med:** five topics no 2569 session teaches (acupuncture, Farabella, tumor series, rectovaginal fistula,
  LapOVE; no rows) are now `continuous`, the scope Epidemiology already uses for topics outside the 2569 lecture table.
  The seven midterm topics stay `both`: the class was told on 16 Sep that the final's POA part draws on every topic
  (recorded in `course-syllabi.js`).
- **Daily question:** it filtered by year only, so in the final weeks it could hand out a midterm topic. It now keeps
  the paper in scope that day (`detectCurrentPhase` on the date, so the class still shares one question). The first
  version kept questions of unknown scope and never asked a course's term, so a review's simulation over the bank found
  Year 5 drawing VCA practice on 11 of 30 November days and Year 4 drawing term-2 courses on about 25. The pool now also
  asks `topicOnPaper` (the term) and `SUBJECT_PAPERS` (the course sits this paper), and drops hidden topics before the
  paper filter, so a paper left with only hidden topics falls back to the year instead of to nothing. Re-simulated:
  30 of 30 November days on paper for both years, the same pick whatever order the bank loads in.
- **Source note:** "the source names the midterm, this year it is on the final" read only `examOrigin`, so a row with
  no origin showing "Aqua Med Mid 86.pdf" (202424, fish vaccination) carried no note. It now reads the source too;
  188 year-5 rows carry the note.
- Not covered: year-4 scopes. The repo has no 2569 syllabus for any year-4 course, so they could not be checked.
