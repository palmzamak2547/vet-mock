# 5.169.2: Thai share-card headings bold on Safari

WebKit paints the variable Thai serif ("VetMock Card Serif") at its default instance, whatever
weight is set. Measured with Playwright on the app's own font, the ink of "ตารางสอบ" at weight
700 over weight 400: Chromium 1.55x, WebKit 0.977x. The printable timetable (5.167.0) had its own
workaround. Every other card drew its Thai headings regular on iPhones and Macs.

- **What changed in the kit.** The kit now does it for every card. `paintsSerifWeight()` reads
  the ink once per card, after the fonts load, and returns true when the canvas cannot be read
  back. `fauxBold()` sizes the stroke: 0.04 of the size at weight 700 and 0.028 at 600. `card.text`
  strokes each Thai run of a serif text at its measured offset. Latin letters and digits are
  left alone, because their static faces already paint at weight. An explicit `embolden` still
  wins.
- **The layout box.** It stays the fill's ink. Counting the stroke (about 2 px at the largest
  heading) turned the timetable and the recap from clean to tight on WebKit, because
  `layoutProblems` allows 1 px.
